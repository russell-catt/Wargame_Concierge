# Volkus Playbook — Kommandos

Full-complexity table plan for Killzone: Volkus. Terrain teaching: [`../../setup/killzones/volkus.md`](../../setup/killzones/volkus.md). Rules detail: [`Team_Rule_Guide.md`](Team_Rule_Guide.md).

**If the store uses Crit Op / Kill Op / Tac Op:** keep one operative free to score Tac Op; do not spend the whole team on a single fight lane. Kill Op still likes Bomb Squig as Expendable.

---

## How should this team be played on Volkus?

1. **Strongholds and ruins are your approach lanes.** Stay on **Conceal**, use **Skulk About**, and Dash with **Sssshhhh!** (not TP1) to close without giving free shots.
2. **Doors matter.** Breacha Boy’s Breach marker turns thin walls/doors into Accessible cuts — pair with Door Fight awareness from the Volkus sheet (Door Fight is a killzone extra, not the core valid-target tree).
3. **Vantage / fire steps are for Snipa Boy and Dakka Boy**, not for your whole team. One gun on a high floor; the rest stay on the floor for Charge lanes.
4. **Throat Slittas sequence:** Conceal → (optional Dash) → Charge from Conceal → fight with **Kunnin’ But Brutal** / **Waaagh!** as CP allows.
5. **Bomb Squig** is a door-knocker and Boom! threat — do not treat it like a Boy that can Charge from Conceal (it is excluded from Throat Slittas).
6. **Grot** climbs with Grappling Hook, stays Conceal forever, contests markers while elites trade.

---

## How to win vs elite teams

**Examples:** Plague Marines, Angels of Death, Deathwatch, Murderwing.

Elites are few, tough, and punish fair fights. You win by **not** standing in the open for double-Shoot/Fight activations.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Trade Bomb Squig / Boy into a fight you already won on the board | Send Slasha into a fresh Champion alone |
| Use Just a Scratch on the Normal Dmg die that would finish a specialist | Stack your whole team in one Condensed Stronghold for their torrent |
| Split activation: score with Grot/Boy while Nob + Slasha pick one elite | Engage early and lose Conceal defence |

**Focus fire:** one elite per turning point. Rokkit / Burna / Dynamite for clusters; otherwise isolate with Breach + Charge.

---

## How to win vs horde teams

**Examples:** Death Korps, Vespid Stingwings, Celestian Insidiants.

Hordes out-activate you and swarm markers. You win on **blast, torrent, and denying their cheap trades**.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Burna deluge / Rokkit Blast into clumps | Chase every operative across the board |
| Waaagh! + multi-fight Nob into a pile | Let them wrap your Snipa on Engage |
| Contest two markers with Grot + Boy while specialists delete a pocket | Ignore Kill Op if they flood bodies — Expendable Squig helps the math |

Keep one Conceal gun alive; use Sssshhhh! to reposition after they overextend.

---

## How to win vs balanced teams

**Examples:** Canoptek Circle, Hierotek Circle, Kommandos (mirror), other non-elite/non-horde owned teams.

Balanced teams match your tools. Win the **Conceal war** and the **door war**.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Skulk About every TP you are shot on Conceal | Mirror their Engage mid-board brawl |
| Breacha their stronghold approach | Ignore their objective runners |
| Comms / Get It Dun! APL on the operative that scores | Over-commit Squig Boom! into your own cluster |

Play the mission: 1" control on markers beats a dramatic but empty wipe.

---

## Vs Plague Marines (Saturday mirror)

They are **elite**: six models, Astartes double actions, Poison chips, Disgustingly Resilient.

1. **Do not fair-fight their Champion brick.** Soften with Squig / Rokkit / knives, then Charge from Conceal into a weakened target.
2. **Poison:** if tagged, activate that operative carefully (chip on activation) — Shake It Off does not stop Poison damage; Just a Scratch only ignores Normal Dmg dice from attacks.
3. **Their Counteract works on Conceal** — assume they get an extra action after you are done.
4. **Cloud of Flies / obscuring:** force Door Fight / Seek tools (Burna deluge has Seek) rather than staring into their marker.
5. **Objective plan:** Grot + Boy hold; Nob + Slasha + Breacha take one fight lane per TP.
