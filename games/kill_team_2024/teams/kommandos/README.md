# Kommandos — Kill Team 2024 (KT24)

**Ork Kommandos.** Sneaky but brutal: infiltrate on **Conceal**, then **Charge** without flipping to Engage (**Throat Slittas**), and finish fights with dakka and choppas. Archetypes: **Infiltration**, **Seek & Destroy**.

**Full guide — track `learn_to_play_event` S1.** Rules quoted from owned Teams PDF. 40K Orks inventory sync is **out of scope** (pending).

## The team in one paragraph

You field **1 Boss Nob** plus **9 selections** from the specialist list (Boys may duplicate; every other type once). **Bomb Squig** and **Grot** count as half a selection each (both together = one selection). The faction rule lets Kommandos (except Bomb Squig) **Charge while Concealed** — that is the whole identity: stay hidden, close, then explode into fights.
