# Starter Roster — Angels of Death

Kill Team has no points-based list-build (see [`../../rules/Overview.md`](../../rules/Overview.md)) — Angels of Death selection is purely **1 leader + 5 operatives from the six-entry list**, with Intercessor Warrior as the only repeatable slot. This page works through one complete roster and a swap-in alternative, using the terms defined in [`Team_Rule_Guide.md`](Team_Rule_Guide.md) and [`operatives/Operatives_Index.md`](operatives/Operatives_Index.md).

Ownership column is `pending check` throughout — no audit or photos yet (S10 gate). Fill in once confirmed.

## Roster A — Sniper-forward (Seek & Destroy lean)

| Slot | Operative | Role | Owned? |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| Leader | **Assault Intercessor Sergeant** | Melee lead, free Assault/Tactical doctrine | pending check |
| Operative | **Assault Intercessor Grenadier** | Melee + free grenades | pending check |
| Operative | **Assault Intercessor Warrior** | Melee backup | pending check |
| Operative | **Eliminator Sniper** | Precision long-range, Conceal-friendly | pending check |
| Operative | **Intercessor Gunner** | Rifle + grenade launcher, flexible fire support | pending check |
| Operative | **Intercessor Warrior** | Rifle, board presence | pending check |

**Chapter Tactics suggestion:** Primary **Aggressive** (rewards the melee-heavy front half), secondary **Stealthy** (protects the Eliminator Sniper while it sits in Conceal). Swap the secondary to **Siege Specialist** with Adaptive Tactics on a turn you expect the enemy to be hunkered in cover.

**How it plays:** the three melee/grenadier operatives push forward together (Astartes lets each of them double up on Fight actions once engaged), while the Eliminator Sniper and Intercessor Gunner hold back on Conceal, picking off anything that gets exposed. The Sergeant's free doctrine use covers the turn you commit to the charge.

## Roster B — Gun-line (Security lean)

Swap the Eliminator Sniper for the **Heavy Intercessor Gunner**, and lean the leader choice toward ranged support:

| Slot | Operative | Role | Owned? |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| Leader | **Intercessor Sergeant** | Rifle + melee hybrid, free Devastator/Tactical doctrine | pending check |
| Operative | **Assault Intercessor Grenadier** | Melee + free grenades | pending check |
| Operative | **Heavy Intercessor Gunner** | Anchor gun line, heavy bolter | pending check |
| Operative | **Intercessor Gunner** | Rifle + grenade launcher | pending check |
| Operative | **Intercessor Warrior** ×2 | Rifle, board presence | pending check |

**Chapter Tactics suggestion:** Primary **Sharpshooter** (rewards the rifle-heavy roster holding still), secondary **Hardy** (keeps the slow Heavy Intercessor Gunner alive while it holds a firing lane).

**How it plays:** plant the Heavy Intercessor Gunner somewhere with a wide firing lane early and don't move it once it's in position (Sharpshooter needs the operative to skip Charge/Fall Back/Reposition that turn). The rest of the roster holds objectives and screens the gunner rather than pushing aggressively — this is the roster for **Security**-flavoured missions and objective-heavy killzones.

## Choosing between them

| If you... | Take |
|-----------|------|
| Own more painted Intercessors than Eliminators/Heavy Intercessors | Whichever specialist you actually own — don't buy a kit just to match this page |
| Are playing a killzone with long, open firing lanes (e.g. Volkus) | Roster B (Heavy Intercessor Gunner) |
| Are playing a tight, blocked killzone (e.g. Gallowdark-style close terrain) | Roster A (Eliminator Sniper, melee-forward) |
| Want the simplest possible first game | Roster A — three melee operatives with the same basic plan ("get close, fight twice") is easier to run on turn one than coordinating a static gun line |
