# Angels of Death — Kill Team 2024

**Priority team (S6 — full package).** A generic Adeptus Astartes kill team built from Intercessor-pattern operatives plus three optional leaders — the "any Chapter, any Space Marine box" option in KT24.

**Status:** `draft`. Written from the living Wahapedia page and a secondary published review, both retrieved **2026-08-17**. The owned team PDF has not been opened this slice — cross-check before a tournament.

**Start here:** [`Team_Rule_Guide.md`](Team_Rule_Guide.md) for the faction rules, then [`Starter_Roster.md`](Starter_Roster.md) for a first list, then [`Quick_Reference_Play_Guide.md`](Quick_Reference_Play_Guide.md) for the table laminate.

## Identity

Angels of Death is what you get when you point the Kill Team 3e engine at a plain Space Marine Intercessor collection. There is no gimmick faction rule tied to a named Chapter or sub-faction — every operative is **big, armoured, and reliable**, and the team's whole personality comes from **Astartes** (every operative can double up its actions) and **Chapter Tactics** (a shared, list-wide pair of traits you pick once per battle, or once per campaign).

Archetypes: **Seek & Destroy** and **Security** — this team's secondary objectives skew toward killing things and holding ground, not stealth or objective-running.

Compared to a specialist team, Angels of Death has **fewer hard build decisions and more play-time decisions**: which Chapter Tactic pair, which Combat Doctrine each turning point, and when to spend Wrath of Vengeance. That makes it a good team to learn KT24's ploy economy on, especially for a player who already owns Space Marine Intercessors from the 40K side.

## Team structure

One **leader** (choose one), plus **five more operatives** from a six-entry list:

| Slot | Choose | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Leader** | 1 of: Space Marine Captain, Assault Intercessor Sergeant, Intercessor Sergeant | Each grants a different free-ploy or extra-tactic perk — see [`Team_Rule_Guide.md`](Team_Rule_Guide.md) |
| **Operatives** | 5 of: Assault Intercessor Grenadier, Assault Intercessor Warrior, Eliminator Sniper, Heavy Intercessor Gunner, Intercessor Gunner, Intercessor Warrior | Every entry except **Intercessor Warrior** can only be taken once — Intercessor Warrior is the only repeatable slot |

Six operatives total. Because only Intercessor Warrior repeats, and the list has six unique entries for five slots, most rosters end up **choosing between the Eliminator Sniper and the Heavy Intercessor Gunner** rather than fitting both alongside a Grenadier, an Assault Warrior, and a Gunner. That trade-off — long-range precision vs. a slow, heavy-hitting gun line — is the team's main build decision. Full operative-by-operative notes: [`operatives/Operatives_Index.md`](operatives/Operatives_Index.md).
