# Operatives Index — Angels of Death

Role-level notes only. **No statlines here** — check your team PDF, the Kill Team app, or Wahapedia for exact APL/Move/Save/Wounds and weapon numbers. Base sizes are noted where relevant because they matter for the 40K dual-legality cross-check, not because they are rules text.

Written from the living Wahapedia Angel of Death page, retrieved **2026-08-17**. Status: `draft`.

---

## Leaders (choose 1)

### Space Marine Captain

The straightforward "big punch" leader: a plasma pistol and power fist combo unavailable on the other two leaders, plus a once-per-battle damage-ignore and the ability to use ploys for free a couple of times a battle. Pick this leader when you want the single hardest-hitting model in the roster and don't mind losing some of the doctrine flexibility the sergeant leaders bring. Base ⌀40mm.

### Assault Intercessor Sergeant

A melee/pistol leader with more loadout flexibility than the Captain (hand flamer or heavy bolt pistol, paired with a choice of close-combat weapon, or a plasma-pistol-and-chainsword combo instead). Gets free use of the Assault or Tactical Combat Doctrine once each per battle, plus an extra personal Chapter Tactic on top of the team's shared pair. The stronger pick on close-quarters killzones where you expect to lean on Assault/Tactical doctrine every turn. Base ⌀32mm.

### Intercessor Sergeant

A rifle-and-melee hybrid leader: any of the team's bolt rifle variants, paired with a choice of melee weapon. Gets free use of the Devastator or Tactical doctrine once each per battle, plus the same extra personal Chapter Tactic as the Assault Intercessor Sergeant. The pick for open maps where long-range fire matters more than a melee spike. Base ⌀32mm.

## Operatives (choose 5; only Intercessor Warrior can repeat)

### Assault Intercessor Grenadier

An Assault Intercessor loadout (heavy bolt pistol and chainsword) plus free frag and krak grenades that hit a little more reliably than normal for this operative specifically. A near-automatic include if you're taking any melee presence at all — the free grenades cost you nothing else in the build. Base ⌀32mm.

### Assault Intercessor Warrior

The baseline melee trooper: heavy bolt pistol plus chainsword. Reliable, unglamorous, and exactly what "Space Marine punches something" looks like at the table — good on close-terrain killzones where you can close distance behind cover before committing. Base ⌀32mm.

### Eliminator Sniper

The team's precision option: a fast-moving, comparatively fragile specialist with a sniper rifle that swaps ammunition type for the situation (armour-piercing, blast, or anti-elite), a cloak that improves its own cover saves, and a utility action that strips the enemy's ability to hide from it. Rewards sitting in Conceal and picking off exposed targets rather than getting into a straight fight. Base ⌀40mm.

### Heavy Intercessor Gunner

The team's gun line: slow and heavily armoured, carrying a heavy bolter that switches between a punchier focused-fire mode and a wider sweeping mode for clustered targets. Best planted somewhere with a firing lane rather than advanced aggressively — its low Move is the trade for its durability and firepower. Base ⌀40mm.

### Intercessor Gunner

A rifle trooper who also carries an auxiliary grenade launcher, giving it a blast option and an armour-piercing option on top of its normal rifle fire. A flexible backstop pick that adds answers to both hordes and tougher single targets without giving up a rifle. Base ⌀32mm.

### Intercessor Warrior

The team's only repeatable slot and its most generic trooper: a straightforward rifle (auto bolt rifle, bolt rifle, or stalker bolt rifle, chosen per model). Fill remaining slots with these once you've picked your specialists — they're the roster's most efficient way to add board presence and extra activations. Base ⌀32mm.

## Build takeaway

Six slots, six unique non-repeatable entries competing for five operative slots (plus the leader) — the practical choice for most rosters is Eliminator Sniper **or** Heavy Intercessor Gunner, not both, alongside a Grenadier, an Assault Warrior, a Gunner, and Intercessor Warriors to round out the count. See [`../Starter_Roster.md`](../Starter_Roster.md) for a worked example of both directions.
