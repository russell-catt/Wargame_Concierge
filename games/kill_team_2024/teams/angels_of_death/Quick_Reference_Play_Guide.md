# ANGELS OF DEATH — TABLE REFERENCE | Page 1 of 2

## TEAM SHAPE

**1 leader** (Space Marine Captain / Assault Intercessor Sergeant / Intercessor Sergeant) **+ 5 operatives** from: Assault Intercessor Grenadier, Assault Intercessor Warrior, Eliminator Sniper, Heavy Intercessor Gunner, Intercessor Gunner, Intercessor Warrior (repeatable). **Archetypes:** Seek & Destroy / Security.

---

## ASTARTES — every activation

- Perform **two Shoot actions** or **two Fight actions** (not a mix) instead of the usual restriction.
- Two Shoot actions: **at least one** must use a bolt weapon. Doubling the **same** heavy bolter or bolt sniper rifle costs **+1 AP** on the second shot.
- **Counteract regardless of order** — even while Concealed (normally Conceal blocks it).

---

## CHAPTER TACTICS — pick primary + secondary at list-build

| # | Tactic | One-line effect |
|---|--------|------------------|
| 1 | **Aggressive** | Melee weapons crit more easily |
| 2 | **Dueller** | Normal successes in a Fight can block enemy crits, not just normal successes |
| 3 | **Resolute** | Ignore APL penalties and enemy Stun-style weapon rule |
| 4 | **Stealthy** | Better cover save while any cover save is available |
| 5 | **Mobile** | Cheaper Fall Back; can Charge out of an enemy's control range |
| 6 | **Hardy** | Defence dice crit more easily; softens first big retaliation hit |
| 7 | **Sharpshooter** | Bolt weapons more reliable on a turn you didn't Charge/Fall Back/Reposition |
| 8 | **Siege Specialist** | Ranged ignores cover; melee denies enemy assist |

**Swap secondary for the turning point:** Adaptive Tactics (Strategy Ploy).

---

## STRATEGY PLOYS (Strategy phase gambit)

| Ploy | Use it for |
|------|------------|
| **Combat Doctrine** | Reroll for shooting-at-range / shooting-up-close / fighting — pick to match this turn's plan. Use almost every turn |
| **And They Shall Know No Fear** | Ignore Injured penalties team-wide, one turning point |
| **Adaptive Tactics** | Swap secondary Chapter Tactic, one turning point |
| **Indomitus** | Fix a bad defensive roll when an operative is shot |

## FIREFIGHT PLOYS (spend mid-phase)

| Ploy | Use it for |
|------|------------|
| **Adjust Doctrine** | Change Combat Doctrine's target situation mid-phase |
| **Transhuman Physiology** | Upgrade a defence success to critical while being shot |
| **Shock Assault** | Extra bite on the first strike of a post-Charge Fight |
| **Wrath of Vengeance** | Counteracting operative gets a 2nd, different, free 1AP action |

## EQUIPMENT (pick at list-build)

Purity Seals (fix a bad roll, 1/turning point) · Chapter Reliquaries (free Wrath of Vengeance for one Engaged operative) · Tilting Shields (deny enemy crit-upgrades in a fight, 1/turning point) · Auspex (remove Obscured near shooter, 1/turning point).

# ANGELS OF DEATH — TABLE REFERENCE | Page 2 of 2

## OPERATIVE ROLE TABLE

| Operative | Role | Base | Play it by... |
|-----------|------|------|----------------|
| Space Marine Captain (L) | Hardest single punch | ⌀40mm | Committing to melee, using free ploys |
| Assault Intercessor Sergeant (L) | Flexible melee lead | ⌀32mm | Free Assault/Tactical doctrine, extra Chapter Tactic |
| Intercessor Sergeant (L) | Rifle + melee hybrid | ⌀32mm | Free Devastator/Tactical doctrine, extra Chapter Tactic |
| Assault Intercessor Grenadier | Melee + free grenades | ⌀32mm | Near-auto include if fielding melee at all |
| Assault Intercessor Warrior | Baseline melee | ⌀32mm | Closing distance behind cover, then committing |
| Eliminator Sniper | Precision ranged | ⌀40mm | Sitting Conceal, punishing exposed targets |
| Heavy Intercessor Gunner | Static gun line | ⌀40mm | Planting once, holding a firing lane, not moving |
| Intercessor Gunner | Flexible fire support | ⌀32mm | Rifle + blast/AP option vs. hordes or elites |
| Intercessor Warrior | Generic trooper | ⌀32mm | Filling remaining slots — only repeatable operative |

**Build note:** six unique entries, five operative slots — most rosters pick **Eliminator Sniper OR Heavy Intercessor Gunner**, not both.

---

## DO / DON'T

| | |
|---|---|
| **DO** match Combat Doctrine's situation to this turn's actual plan | **DON'T** pick a doctrine for range when you're about to charge |
| **DO** remember Astartes lets Conceal operatives counteract | **DON'T** assume Conceal blocks it here the way it does elsewhere |
| **DO** budget +1 AP before doubling a heavy bolter/bolt sniper shot | **DON'T** find out mid-activation you're 1 AP short |
| **DO** pick at least one defensive/utility Chapter Tactic | **DON'T** take two offensive tactics and leave the team fragile |
| **DO** plant the Heavy Intercessor Gunner once and hold | **DON'T** walk it into the open — it's slow and needs a firing lane |
| **DO** use Adjust Doctrine when the plan flips mid-phase | **DON'T** forget it only works if Combat Doctrine was already used |

## PRE-GAME CHECKLIST

- [ ] Leader chosen (Captain / Assault Sergeant / Intercessor Sergeant).
- [ ] Five operatives selected — check the Eliminator-vs-Heavy-Intercessor trade-off.
- [ ] Primary + secondary Chapter Tactic written down.
- [ ] Equipment picked (max per usual selection rules — check team PDF).
- [ ] Archetype-relevant Tac Op noted if playing Approved Ops (Seek & Destroy / Security).

## END-OF-TURN CHECKLIST

- [ ] Did you use Combat Doctrine this turning point? If your plan changed, did you Adjust Doctrine?
- [ ] Any operative eligible to counteract — including Concealed ones?
- [ ] Injured operatives noted (-2" Move, weapons' Hit worsened by 1) — see [`../../rules/Key_Concepts.md`](../../rules/Key_Concepts.md).
- [ ] Limited-use equipment/ploys ticked off as spent.
