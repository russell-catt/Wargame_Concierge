# Angels of Death — Owned Models Inventory

**As of 2026-08-21.** Two photo sets:

1. **Used Intercessor set (earlier):** five Primaris Intercessor-family models, Black Templars paint — **play as Codex Adeptus Astartes / Angels of Death, not Black Templars**.
2. **`C:\Personal\40K\Pics` AOD_* files (this pass):** individual specialist photos + group shot. Paint schemes are **mixed** (Blood Ravens, Deathwatch markings, yellow, black stealth, green Salamander/DW Captain). Owner intent: **dual-use into Codex Space Marines** for 40K when base-legal; KT play as Angels of Death.

Binaries stay outside git. Previews (not committed): `C:\Personal\40K\Pics\_preview\root\`.

**KT rule:** Angels of Death = **1 leader + 5 operatives** (six models total).
