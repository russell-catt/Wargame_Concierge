### Regeneration timing — closed loophole (August 2026 package)

Paraphrase from `eng_hierotek_circle_online_rules-df98wqycag-bek3q27dqk.pdf` errata p.11 (staging, read 2026-08-27), matching the WarCom quarterly balance commentary: **"a small tweak to the Hierotek Circle helps to close some unforeseen loopholes around their regeneration ability, now only letting it kick in the turn after an operative is brought back to life."**

What actually changed, mechanically: Hierotek Circle has **two** separate faction rules that both fire in the Ready step of each Strategy phase — **Living Metal** (regain up to D3+1 lost wounds) and **Reanimation Protocols** (bring back an incapacitated operative on a Reanimation marker, with 1 wound remaining). The August '26 errata pins down the order: **Living Metal now explicitly resolves before Reanimation Protocols in that step.**

- **The loophole this closes:** an operative that comes back via Reanimation Protocols is not yet "on the table" when Living Metal resolves that same Ready step (Living Metal goes first) — so it **cannot** also regain Living Metal wounds the instant it's reanimated.
- **The practical effect:** a freshly-reanimated Hierotek Circle operative sits at its **1 wound** from Reanimation Protocols for the rest of that turning point. It only gets a Living Metal top-up in the **following** Ready step — i.e. the regen benefit applies **the turn after** it was brought back, not the same turn.
- **Teaching takeaway:** don't plan around "reanimate this turning point, then immediately heal it back up with Living Metal" — that combo no longer works. Budget for the operative staying fragile (1 wound) until your next Strategy phase.
