# Owned Models Inventory — Canoptek Circle

**As of 2026-08-17.** Every operative this team needs is part of the already-owned, assembled, painted **Kill Team: Tomb World** box — see the corrected ownership record in [`../../../warhammer_40k_11e/armies/necrons/Owned_Models_Inventory.md`](../../../warhammer_40k_11e/armies/necrons/Owned_Models_Inventory.md). This page maps that box onto the eight Canoptek Circle operative slots.

---

## Dual-legality reminder

Owned ≠ dual-legal. Base size below is recorded from the living Wahapedia team page (KT rules of record). Whether the **same physical model on its current base** is also tournament-legal for a 40K datasheet slot is a separate question, marked `pending check` until the 40K Necron faction pack is opened and cross-referenced.
