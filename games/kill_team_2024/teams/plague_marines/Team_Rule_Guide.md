# Team Rule Guide — Plague Marines

**Personal use only. Never for sale.** Quotes from `eng_29-04_kt_teamrules_plague_marines-liggy6zl51-fa8nryqey9.pdf`, read **2026-08-17**.

---

## Astartes (quoted)

**Source:** Teams PDF p.3

> During each friendly PLAGUE MARINE operative's activation, it can perform either two Shoot actions or two Fight actions. If it's two Shoot actions, a bolt pistol, boltgun or PSYCHIC weapon must be selected for at least one of them. You cannot select the same PSYCHIC ranged weapon more than once per activation.
>
> Each friendly PLAGUE MARINE operative can counteract regardless of its order.

**What it means:** Two Shoots **or** two Fights — not mixed. If you Shoot twice, at least one shot needs a bolt pistol, boltgun, or psychic weapon. Same psychic ranged weapon only once. You can counteract on Conceal or Engage.

---

## Poison (quoted)

**Source:** Teams PDF p.3

> *Poison: In the Resolve Attack Dice step, if you inflict damage with any successes, the operative this weapon is being used against (excluding friendly PLAGUE MARINE operatives) gains one of your Poison tokens (if it doesn't already have one). Whenever an operative that has one of your Poison tokens is activated, inflict 1 damage on it.

**What it means:** Put a token on them when your Poison attack hurts them. They get hurt when they activate. One token only — it does not stack. You do not Poison your own Plague Marines.

---

## Disgustingly Resilient (quoted)

**Source:** Teams PDF p.3

> Whenever an attack dice inflicts damage of 3 or more on a friendly PLAGUE MARINE operative, roll one D6: on a 4+, subtract 1 from that inflicted damage.

**What it means:** Big single-die hits (3+) might lose 1 damage on a 4+. Small chip damage skips this roll.

---

## Roster (quoted)

**Source:** Teams PDF p.3 / p.10

> - 1 PLAGUE MARINE CHAMPION operative
> - 5 PLAGUE MARINE operatives selected from: BOMBARDIER, FIGHTER, HEAVY GUNNER, ICON BEARER, MALIGNANT PLAGUECASTER, WARRIOR
>
> Your kill team can only include **each operative on this list once**.

**What it means:** Legal kill team size is **6** — Champion plus five different names from that list. No duplicates.
