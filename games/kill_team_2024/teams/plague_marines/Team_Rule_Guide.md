# Team Rule Guide - Plague Marines

Everything below is teaching paraphrase - our own explanation of what each rule does, cross-checked against the living Wahapedia Plague Marines page on **2026-08-17**. No ploy text, equipment text, or datacard statline is transcribed; check your own team-rules PDF or the Kill Team app for exact wording.

---

## The three faction rules

Every operative with the **PLAGUE MARINE** keyword gets all three, all the time - there is nothing to activate or pay for.

### Astartes

**What it does:** during its activation, a Plague Marine operative can spend its AP on **two Shoot actions or two Fight actions** - never one of each. If it takes the two-Shoot option, at least one of those shots has to come from a bolt pistol, boltgun, or a psychic ranged weapon, and it cannot repeat the same named psychic ranged weapon twice in one activation. Separately, every Plague Marine can **counteract regardless of its order** - the normal restriction that only Engage operatives may counteract does not apply to this team.

**Why it matters:** this is a double-tap/double-swing engine, not a mix-and-match one. Decide up front whether an operative is shooting or fighting this activation, then commit to that action twice. The counteract exception means a Conceal Plague Marine left behind after your side runs dry is still a live threat, not a dead weight - plan your last-operative-standing accordingly.

### Poison

**What it does:** several of the team's weapons and ploys carry the **Poison** weapon rule. Landing damage with a Poison source marks the target with a Poison token (it does not stack - a target either has one or it does not). Every time a token-carrying operative activates, it automatically takes a small amount of damage from the token, on top of anything else that happens to it that turn.

**Why it matters:** Poison is a condition to spread and then exploit, not a big hit in itself. A tagged enemy operative bleeds a little every single activation regardless of what it does - which rewards you for tagging things early and for having other ploys (see below) that specifically punish poisoned targets, rather than chasing the token purely for its own tick of damage.

### Disgustingly Resilient

**What it does:** whenever a single attack die would inflict 3 or more damage on a friendly Plague Marine, there's a roll for a chance to reduce that inflicted damage by 1.

**Why it matters:** this only ever shaves the top off a *big* hit - it does nothing against small chip damage, and it is not guaranteed. Treat it as a modest tax on your opponent's best rolls, not a reason to walk a Plague Marine into open fire on purpose.

## Strategy ploys (Gambit step)

Used during the Strategy phase's Gambit step, before anyone activates. Paraphrased gist only - the four named ploys share one theme: **make Poison tokens and the Icon Bearer do more work at range.**

| Ploy | Paraphrased gist |
|------|-------------------|
| **Contagion** | Debuffs an enemy operative's Move and worsens its Hit stat, if it is near a friendly Icon Bearer, or if it already carries one of your Poison tokens and is near your Plague Marines |
| **Lumbering Death** | Grants a re-roll weapon rule to a Plague Marine that barely moved this activation, or that is retaliating |
| **Cloud of Flies** | Drops a marker that gives a nearby friendly operative an obscuring effect against shots from further away, then removes itself next turning point |
| **Nurglings** | Temporarily docks APL from a nearby enemy operative, with extended range if that enemy already carries a Poison token |

---

## Firefight ploys

Used mid-activation, spending CP for an immediate effect. Theme: **apply, protect, or cash in on Poison.**

| Ploy | Paraphrased gist |
|------|-------------------|
| **Virulent Poison** | Applies a Poison token to a nearby or visible-at-range enemy operative, independent of an actual hit landing |
| **Sickening Resilience** | Turns Disgustingly Resilient's chance-based damage reduction into a guaranteed one for the rest of that action |
| **Poisonous Demise** | When a friendly Plague Marine is incapacitated, it spreads or cashes in Poison tokens on nearby enemies as a parting shot |
| **Curse of Rot** | Punishes specific defence or attack die results your opponent rolls against a nearby Plague Marine in a fight or shooting exchange |

---

## Faction equipment

Selected before the battle, not spent like a ploy. Theme: **protect Plague Marines from their own weaknesses, and put Poison on more weapons.**

| Equipment | Paraphrased gist |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Plague Bells** | Lets Plague Marines ignore the stat penalties that come from being Injured |
| **Plague Rounds** | Adds Poison (and a bonus-success weapon rule) to the team's boltguns and bolt pistols |
| **Blight Grenades** | A limited-use grenade weapon (usable only a couple of times across the whole battle) with an area effect and Poison |
| **Poison Vents** | An aura that pressures enemy operatives who activate near a Plague Marine - it either risks applying a Poison token to them or, if they already carry one, deals them extra damage |

---

## How the pieces fit together

1. **Tag early.** Poison Rounds, Virulent Poison, and several weapons all exist to get a token onto a target before you need the payoff.
2. **Cash in with ranged ploys.** Contagion, Nurglings, and Curse of Rot all read better against a target that already carries your token - sequence them after the tag, not before.
3. **Lean into Astartes.** Decide Shoot-twice or Fight-twice per activation and commit; do not plan a mixed activation that Astartes will not let you perform.
4. **Treat Disgustingly Resilient as a bonus, not a plan.** It reduces big hits sometimes; it is not a reason to skip cover.
