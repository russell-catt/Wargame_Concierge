# Volkus Playbook — Plague Marines

For a **12-year-old** learning game on Killzone: Volkus. Read the killzone page first: [`../../setup/killzones/volkus.md`](../../setup/killzones/volkus.md).

You bring **6** operatives. You are slow and tough. You win by holding ground and spreading **Poison**, not by racing across the board.

---

## How should this team be played on Volkus?

Volkus is a cityfight board. Strongholds have upper floors (Vantage). Ruins and doors block sight.

**Do** walk up mid-board behind cover. **Do** put Poison on enemies you can see. **Do** hold markers with your Icon Bearer and Warrior.

**Don’t** sprint into open lanes. **Don’t** climb every roof on turn one. **Don’t** leave your Heavy Gunner with no targets.

Use doors and ruin corners so they must come to you. Door Fight is a Volkus rule — you can fight someone on the other side of a door if you are touching it (see [`volkus.md`](../../setup/killzones/volkus.md)). Save that for when someone camps a doorway.

Astartes means **two Shoots OR two Fights, not mixed**. Pick one plan each activation.

---

## How to win vs elite teams

Examples: plague marines, angels of death, deathwatch, murderwing.

Elite teams have few models. Each one hurts a lot.

**Do** tag them with Poison early. **Do** trade one of yours for one of theirs only when a marker or a key gun is worth it. **Do** use Disgustingly Resilient as a bonus, not a plan.

**Don’t** stand in a shoot-out in the open. **Don’t** charge their best fighter alone.

Focus fire. Remove one elite before you chip three.

---

## How to win vs horde teams

Examples: death_korps, vespid_stingwings, celestian_insidiants.

Horde teams have many models. They try to swamp markers.

**Do** use the Heavy Gunner and Fighter when enemies bunch up. **Do** Poison the ones that will activate next. **Do** sit on the markers that score — you do not need to kill everyone.

**Don’t** chase every model into side alleys. **Don’t** split so thin that three weak hits finish you.

Hold the middle. Let them come through doors and lanes you cover.

---

## How to win vs balanced teams

Examples: canoptek_circle, hierotek_circle, kommandos.

Balanced teams mix shooters, melee, and tricks.

**Do** keep your six together enough to support each other. **Do** Contagion / Cloud of Flies when the table gets messy (check ploy text on your PDF). **Do** counteract even on Conceal.

**Don’t** over-extend one model for a kill. **Don’t** forget equipment you picked before the game.

Play the mission. Poison the threats. Hold what scores.

### vs Kommandos (dad’s team)

Dad’s Kommandos are sneaky and like Charge while Concealed (their team rule). They want close fights and tricks.

**Do** keep Poison on his key boys. **Do** watch doors and ruin gaps — that is where he charges from. **Do** use your Fighter and Champion when he gets adjacent. **Do** counteract after he activates a scary model.

**Don’t** leave a lone Plague Marine in the open for a Charge. **Don’t** ignore the Bomb Squig / Grot tricks if he brings them — ask before the game what is in his list.

You are tougher per model. Make him pay for every Charge. Hold markers while Poison ticks.
