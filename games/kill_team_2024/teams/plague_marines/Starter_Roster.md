# Starter Roster - Plague Marines

Kill Team picks by **operative count and role**, not 40K points. For Plague Marines the owned Teams PDF sets a **fixed legal size of 6**.

**Legal list (owned PDF):**

1. **1** Plague Marine **Champion** (required)
2. **5** more Plague Marine operatives, chosen from this list, **each once only:**
   - Bombardier
   - Fighter
   - Heavy Gunner
   - Icon Bearer
   - Malignant Plaguecaster
   - Warrior

That is **6 operatives on the table**. You leave **one** of the six optional roles at home each game. You never take two of the same name.

See the quote in [`Team_Rule_Guide.md`](Team_Rule_Guide.md).

**Collection:** all **seven** are owned and photo-confirmed (Kill Team Starter Set, 2026-08-21).
