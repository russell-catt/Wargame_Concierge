# NPO Catalog — Kill Team 2024

Every released non-player-operative (NPO) tier this project knows about, as of **2026-08-17**. **Teaching paraphrase only.** No profile numbers (APL, Move, Save, Wounds, weapon Atk/Hit/Dmg) are reproduced anywhere in this page — that is a hard rule, not a style choice.

**Confidence key:** `draft` = paraphrased from a source that was actually read this session; `stub` = named but not read. Nothing here is `verified` — verification means cross-checking two independent sources and none of this has had that pass yet.

---

## 1. Generic Joint Ops NPOs (Core Book) — `draft`

**Source:** owned Core Rules PDF, cross-checked via a Wahapedia mirror of the same Core Book Joint Ops mission pack content (retrieved 2026-08-17). These are the NPOs built into the base game — no expansion purchase needed.

Six archetype datacards, built from two axes:

| Tier (rough toughness) | Trooper | Tough | Warrior | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Feel at the table | Baseline filler — dies fast, dangerous mainly in numbers | Soaks damage, slow to remove | Genuine threat, comparable to a real operative | Boss-tier for this pack — can act twice per activation |

Each tier comes in two **behaviours** (see [`NPO_Cheat_Sheet.md`](NPO_Cheat_Sheet.md) for the full loop):

- **Brawler** — closes distance to fight, taking cover on the way if it can.
- **Marksman** — falls back to a covered firing position and shoots.

One Marksman-tier Trooper variant can be equipped with a "special weapon" instead of its default firearm on a roughly 1-in-3 basis — paraphrased as a loadout note, not a stat.

**Used by:** the three core Joint Ops missions (Breach, Sabotage, Escape — see [`Playable_Scenarios_Owned_Terrain.md`](Playable_Scenarios_Owned_Terrain.md)), and as filler NPOs inside several of the themed mission packs below.

## 2. Terror on Devlan — `draft`

**Source:** owned local dossier + mission pack PDFs (both text-readable, read in full this session).

A self-contained boxed narrative release, not an accessory to Nemesis Operatives. Ships with its own kill team (**Spectre Squad**) and its own NPOs, built for **Killzone: Volkus**.

| NPO | Paraphrase |
|-----|------------|
| **Red Terror** | The headline threat — a fast, regenerating melee monster. Its behaviour always drives it toward the nearest player operative; it cannot be permanently removed, only forced to retreat and heal, so the mission design is about surviving and objective-running around it rather than killing it outright. Its control range is described as unusually tall, so being on an upper floor is not automatically safe. |
| **Termagants** | Numerous, individually weak Tyranid NPOs — a swarm that punishes splitting your attention away from the Red Terror. |
| **Ripper Swarms** | A second lesser Tyranid NPO type filling out the swarm alongside Termagants. |

**Structure:** nine linked missions (Mission 01 "The Hunt Begins" through Mission 09, with two named campaign-victory and two named campaign-defeat end states), playable as a one-off pick-any-mission session or as a full branching campaign — win a mission and the green-arrow path continues, lose and the red-arrow path does. NPO activation order in this pack is randomised by drawing from a numbered card deck rather than the generic Threat Principle alone, adding controlled unpredictability early in a mission that settles down as the deck thins.

**Related team ownership:** Spectre Squad is not one of this track's three priority guide teams (Canoptek Circle, Plague Marines, Angels of Death) — it ships in the box specifically to make Terror on Devlan playable standalone. A father-son session can use Spectre Squad as printed, or substitute an owned priority team.

## 3. Joint Ops: Tomb World mission pack — `draft`

**Source:** owned local mission pack PDF (text-readable, read in part this session).

Six missions, built for **Killzone: Tomb World**, each usable with any of six included killzone maps rolled or picked for variety. NPOs are Necron constructs drawn from the Kill Team: Tomb World box's own roster (cross-referenced via the Shadowhunt review below, since the Tomb World mission pack text itself points at the killzone book rather than restating NPO datacards): Scarab Swarms, Necron Warriors, Tomb Crawlers, and Macrocytes, described generically rather than with named individual profiles.

**Notable overlap:** these are the same Necron unit families already in this project's owned Tomb World collection (see the Necrons faction pages under `games/warhammer_40k_11e/armies/necrons/`) — once Tomb World terrain is assembled, the models a father-son session fights *against* here are close cousins of the models the Necron player already owns and plays *with* in 40K and in Canoptek Circle.

## 4. Joint Ops: Shadowhunt mission pack — `draft`

**Source:** local Shadowhunt mission pack PDF (partially read — Descent killzone rules confirmed) cross-checked against a public review and two retailer/preview pages (retrieved 2026-08-17).

Three cooperative missions using the **Descent killzone** — two linked half-boards, one Volkus-themed (upper) and one Tomb World-themed (lower), joined by Entryway markers operatives can climb between. NPOs are drawn from the Tomb World roster (Scarab Swarms, Necron Warriors, Tomb Crawlers, Macrocytes) plus the same generic Trooper/Tough archetypes as Section 1, escalating to a named boss encounter in the final mission: a **C'tan Shard of the Nightbringer**.

**Player side:** the box ships two new kill teams, **Murderwing** (Chaos Space Marines / Night Lords, jump-pack strike team) and **Celestian Insidiants** (Adepta Sororitas), both already owned per `raw/pointers/kill_team_2024_teams.md` — either can play the Joint Ops side, or a father-son session can substitute a priority team.

**A companion PvP pack, Adversary Ops: Shadowhunt, also ships in the same box** (three competitive missions on the same Descent killzone) — out of scope for Join Ops, listed here only so it is not confused with the co-op pack.
