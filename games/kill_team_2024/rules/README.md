# Rules - Kill Team 2024 / 3rd Edition

**Status:** Populated in **S1**. All four documents were written from the living Wahapedia core rules and Approved Ops 2025 pages, retrieved **2026-08-17**. The owned Core Rules PDF (`raw/pointers/kill_team_2024_core.md`) has not yet been opened - every claim here carries a `draft` or `unverified` status, never `verified`, until that cross-check happens.

Everything here is **teaching paraphrase** - our own explanation of how the rules work. No publisher text or datacard statlines are reproduced.

**Cross-checked against the KB.** The Librarian's L1 pass landed [`KB/glossary.md`](../../../KB/glossary.md) (Kill Team 2024 section) and six concept pages - [`turning_points`](../../../KB/concepts/turning_points.md), [`activations_apl`](../../../KB/concepts/activations_apl.md), [`orders_conceal_engage`](../../../KB/concepts/orders_conceal_engage.md), [`control_range_kill_team`](../../../KB/concepts/control_range_kill_team.md), [`cover_kill_team`](../../../KB/concepts/cover_kill_team.md), [`injured_operatives`](../../../KB/concepts/injured_operatives.md) - in parallel with this slice, from the same living Wahapedia source. Two things intentionally kept high-level rather than fully specified, per the S1 brief:

- **Ops and scoring.** `Key_Concepts.md` explains the shape of Crit Op / Kill Op / Tac Op scoring under Approved Ops 2025, but does not reproduce Approved Ops card text or per-mission Tac Op wording.
- **APL modifiers.** The ±1 net cap is stated as a rule; the specific rare abilities that raise or lower APL belong to team-specific content (S3-S6), not this shared spine.
