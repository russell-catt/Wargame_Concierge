Throat Slittas

Each friendly KOMMANDO (excluding Bomb Squig) can Charge while Conceal.

What it means: stay hidden for cover / Skulk About, then charge without flipping Engage first.

Roster

  • 1 Boss Nob (slugga + big choppa or slugga + power klaw)
  • 9 selections from: Bomb Squig, Boy, Breacha, Burna, Comms, Dakka, Grot, Rokkit, Slasha, Snipa
  • Boys may duplicate; other types once
  • Bomb Squig + Grot = half each (both = one selection)

Archetypes: Infiltration, Seek & Destroy.
