Plague Marines — Faction rules | Page 1 of 2

Teaching paraphrase for print. Exact wording stays in the owned Teams PDF / Kill Team app.

Astartes

Each activation: perform either two Shoot actions or two Fight actions — not mixed. If you Shoot twice, at least one shot must use a bolt pistol, boltgun, or psychic weapon. You cannot select the same psychic ranged weapon more than once per activation.

Counteract: each friendly Plague Marine can counteract regardless of order (Conceal or Engage).

Poison

  • When a Poison weapon’s success deals damage, the enemy gains one Poison token (if they do not already have one).
  • When that operative activates, they take 1 damage.
  • One token only — does not stack. Does not apply to friendly Plague Marines.

Toxic (weapon rule, when present): hits harder if they already carry your Poison token — check the datacard.

Disgustingly Resilient

When an attack die deals 3 or more damage to a friendly Plague Marine, roll a D6: on a 4+, that hit deals 1 less. Chip hits below 3 skip the roll.

Roster shape

6 operatives: 1 Champion + 5 unique picks from Bombardier, Fighter, Heavy Gunner, Icon Bearer, Malignant Plaguecaster, Warrior. No duplicates.

Table habits

  • Say “two Shoots” or “two Fights” out loud at the start of each activation.
  • Track Poison tokens where everyone can see them.
  • Counteract on Conceal is legal for you — use it.
