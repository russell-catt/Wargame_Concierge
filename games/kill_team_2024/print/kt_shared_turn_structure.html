Kill Team 2024 — Turning point checklist

Two phases only. Almost every decision lives in Firefight. Teaching paraphrase for a first table game.

The frame

A battle is a sequence of turning points. Each turning point: Strategy phase, then Firefight phase — in order, in full. Repeat until the battle ends (often four turning points).

1. Strategy phase

Initiative

  • First turning point: set by the mission pack sequence (often a setup roll-off).
  • Later: both roll off; winner decides who has initiative. On a tie, whoever did not have initiative last turning point decides.
  • Initiative player resolves their own simultaneous-timing decisions first.

Ready

  • Each player gains 1 CP. From TP2 on, the player without initiative gains 2 CP instead.
  • Every friendly operative is readied (order token to light / ready side; dark = expended).
Setup note: Operatives start the battle on Conceal. You set order when you activate. Conceal-in-cover only works after that order is set and they stay in cover.

Gambit

  • Initiative player starts. Alternate: one STRATEGIC GAMBIT (usually a strategy ploy) or pass.
  • Ends when both pass in succession.
  • Same named gambit only once per turning point.

2. Firefight phase

Initiative activates first. Then alternate one operative per activation.

  1. Order — Conceal or Engage (kept until next activation).
  2. Actions — Spend AP up to APL, one action at a time. Same action usually cannot repeat in one activation.
  3. Expended — Flip token dark; cannot act again this TP except Counteract.

Continue until one player has no ready operatives left.

Counteract

  • If all your operatives are expended but the opponent still has ready ones, pick one expended friendly on Engage (team rules may change this) and do one free 1AP action (not Guard).
  • Cannot move more than 2" while counteracting.
  • Once per operative per turning point. Optional. Not an activation.

Phase ends when every operative on both sides is expended.

Common beginner mistakes

MistakeReality
Treating phases like 40K’s fiveTwo phases; move / shoot / fight all happen in one activation
Declaring the whole activation up frontDecide one action at a time
Forgetting to set an orderEvery activation starts with Conceal or Engage
Assuming Conceal is always safeConceal only blocks valid target while also in cover
Skipping CounteractCheck every time the opponent activates after you are out of ready ops
Both players always +1 CPAfter TP1, no-initiative player gets +2
