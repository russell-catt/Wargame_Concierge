Faction rule — Throat Slittas

Kommandos (not Bomb Squig) can Charge while Conceal. Stay sneaky, then charge without flipping Engage first.

Ploy cheat strip (paraphrase — PDF for exact text)

Strategy: Dakka! Dakka! Dakka! (ranged Punishing) · Sssshhhh! (free Dash if not targetable / Conceal >6" — not TP1) · Waaagh! (melee Balanced) · Skulk About (Conceal: free defence success vs shooting).

Firefight: Just a Scratch (ignore Normal Dmg die; not Squig/Grot) · Shake It Off (ignore APL changes) · Krump ’Em! (end of Firefight: free Fight) · Kunnin’ But Brutal (Conceal charge: first normal strike → crit).

Equipment: Choppas · Collapsible Stocks · Dynamite (once/battle) · Harpoon (once/TP).

Shoot / Fight at a glance

Shoot: weapon → valid target → Atk vs Hit → defence vs Save (cover) → damage.

Fight: both roll → alternate strike/block (attacker first).
