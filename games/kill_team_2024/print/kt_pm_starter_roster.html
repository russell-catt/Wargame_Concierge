Starter Roster — Plague Marines

Kill Team picks by operative count and role, not 40K points. Owned Teams PDF: fixed legal size of 6.

Legal list: 1 Plague Marine Champion (required) + 5 more from Bombardier, Fighter, Heavy Gunner, Icon Bearer, Malignant Plaguecaster, Warrior — each once only. That is 6 on the table. Leave one optional role home each game.
