Crit/Kill/Tac Op: keep one operative free to score; Bomb Squig is Expendable for Kill Op math.

How should this team be played on Volkus?

  • Strongholds/ruins = approach lanes. Stay Conceal; Skulk About; Sssshhhh! Dash (not TP1).
  • Breacha Boy opens Accessible cuts; watch Door Fight (Volkus extra).
  • One gun on Vantage (Snipa/Dakka); rest on floor for Charge lanes.
  • Throat Slittas: Conceal → Dash → Charge from Conceal → fight (Kunnin’ / Waaagh!).
  • Bomb Squig: no Charge-from-Conceal. Grot: climb, always Conceal, contest markers.

How to win vs elite teams

Examples: Plague Marines, Angels of Death, Deathwatch, Murderwing.

DoDon’t
Trade Squig/Boy into a fight you already wonSend Slasha into a fresh Champion alone
Just a Scratch on finishing Normal DmgStack in one stronghold for their torrent

One elite per TP. Rokkit/Burna/Dynamite for clusters; else isolate with Breach + Charge.

How to win vs balanced teams

Examples: Canoptek Circle, Hierotek Circle, Kommandos (mirror).

DoDon’t
Skulk About every TP you are shot on ConcealMirror their Engage mid-board brawl
Breach their stronghold approachIgnore objective runners
Comms / Get It Dun! on the scorerBoom! into your own cluster

1" control on markers beats an empty wipe.

Vs Plague Marines (Saturday)

  • Six elites: Astartes double actions, Poison chips, Disgustingly Resilient.
  • Do not fair-fight Champion brick — soften with Squig/Rokkit/knives, then Conceal Charge.
  • Poison chip on activation — Just a Scratch only ignores attack Normal Dmg dice.
  • They counteract on Conceal — assume an extra action after you finish.
  • Cloud of Flies: use Door Fight / Seek (Burna deluge) not staring into their marker.
  • Grot+Boy hold; Nob+Slasha+Breacha take one fight lane per TP.
