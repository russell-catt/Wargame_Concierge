Volkus + terrain — table aid

Shared killzone habits for a first Volkus game. Teaching paraphrase — confirm Door Fight vs your owned Volkus / update-log sheet.

Volkus setup (quick)

#Do

4Initiative per pack sequence
5Tokens ready: order, CP, TP dial

Board is a standard 30" × 22" killzone. Strongholds + ruins = cityfight. Upper floors are often Ceiling + Vantage.

Terrain is parts, not one object

Agree every part’s type out loud before anyone moves. Ignoring “Light” means ignoring Light parts, not the whole ruin.

TypeWhat it does
HeavyCan make a target Obscured
LightContributes to Cover; pairs with Vantage
ExposedNo Cover / Obscured from that part
InsignificantIgnored for climbing / dropping (identify before battle)
AccessibleMove through (e.g. door); extra 1"; only base centre must pass
VantageAlso Light; you can finish a move / set up on it
CeilingSmall bases can pass underneath (often with Vantage on Volkus)

Cover vs Obscured

CoverObscured
FromTerrain in 1" control range (ops >2" apart)Heavy between shooter and target
Conceal + in coverNot a valid target—
Engage + in coverValid; cover save (collect 3, keep one normal success)—
If Obscured—Attacker discards one success; crits become normal

Same feature cannot give both Cover and Obscured — defender picks one. Within 1" of Heavy ignores only that part.

Volkus-only: Door Fight

Door Fight (cityfight extra — not on the core valid-target tree): when selecting an enemy, you may pick one on the killzone floor, within 2" of, and on the other side of, a door your active operative is touching.
  • Large ruin upper level: often Ceiling + Vantage; door often Accessible + Heavy.
  • Ignoring a door for 1" control range does not let you Shoot an invisible target.
  • Fire step does not ignore the rest of a stronghold for Obscured.

Movement habits

ActionHabit
ClimbMin 2" of move, even for a short step
DropFirst 2" of a drop free; excess costs move
JumpFrom high Vantage: up to 4" horizontal, then drop/climb
Accessible / door+1" to pass; that inch counts against the 2" counteract cap

Layout that plays well

  • Heavy near each drop zone edge — somewhere to hide immediately.
  • Keep Vantage out of drop zones when you can.
  • Concentrate terrain between drop zones to make real lanes.
  • Agree Barred windows / Blocking vents before deployment.

Vantage tip

One gun on a high floor is usually enough. Most of the team stays on the floor for Charge lanes and Door Fight. Accurate vs Engage targets below is a SEQUENCE rule — check Core when it matters.
