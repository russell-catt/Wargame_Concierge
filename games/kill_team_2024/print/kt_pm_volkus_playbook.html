Volkus Playbook — Plague Marines | Page 1 of 2

First Volkus game. You bring 6 operatives. Slow and tough. Win by holding ground and spreading Poison, not by racing.

How to play on Volkus

Cityfight board: strongholds with Vantage, ruins and doors that block sight.

DODON’T
Walk mid-board behind coverSprint into open lanes
Put Poison on enemies you can seeClimb every roof on turn one
Hold markers with Icon Bearer + WarriorLeave Heavy Gunner with no targets

Use doors and ruin corners so they come to you. Door Fight: fight someone on the other side of a door you are touching (Volkus sheet). Save it for doorway campers.

Astartes: two Shoots OR two Fights — not mixed. Pick one plan each activation.

Vs elite teams

Examples: Plague Marines, Angels of Death, Deathwatch, Murderwing. Few models; each hurts a lot.

  • Do tag with Poison early. Trade one-for-one only when a marker or key gun is worth it.
  • Do treat Disgustingly Resilient as a bonus, not a plan.
  • Don’t stand in an open shoot-out. Don’t charge their best fighter alone.
  • Focus fire: remove one elite before you chip three.

Vs horde teams

Examples: Death Korps, Vespid, Celestian Insidiants. Many models; they swamp markers.

  • Do use Heavy Gunner / Fighter when they bunch. Poison who activates next. Sit on scoring markers.
  • Don’t chase every model into alleys. Don’t split so thin that three weak hits finish you.
  • Hold the middle. Cover doors and lanes.

Vs balanced teams

Examples: Canoptek Circle, Hierotek Circle, Kommandos.

  • Do keep your six close enough to support each other.
  • Do Contagion / Cloud of Flies when the table gets messy (check ploy text on PDF).
  • Do counteract even on Conceal.
  • Don’t over-extend one model for a kill. Don’t forget pre-game equipment.

Play the mission. Poison the threats. Hold what scores.

Vs Kommandos (event mirror)

Kommandos are sneaky and like Charge while Concealed (Throat Slittas). They want close fights and tricks.

DODON’T
Keep Poison on his key boysLeave a lone PM in the open for a Charge
Watch doors and ruin gapsIgnore Bomb Squig / Grot tricks — ask the list
Use Fighter + Champion when adjacent
Counteract after a scary activation

You are tougher per model. Make him pay for every Charge. Hold markers while Poison ticks.
