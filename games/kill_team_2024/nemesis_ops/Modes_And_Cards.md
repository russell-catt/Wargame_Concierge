# Modes and physical cards

**Confidence: draft.**

## Play modes

| Mode | Teaching paraphrase |
|------|---------------------|
| Solo / co-op Joint Ops | Players share one kill team vs NPO Nemesis threats |
| Head-to-head with Nemesis | PvP with Nemesis involvement |
| **Nemesis Ops** (dossier wording) | Pack framing alongside Joint Ops for Nemesis-supported games |
| **Adversary Ops** (WarCom / Shadowhunt usage) | Often used for PvP-with-NPO-assist; Archivist’s Negotiation is labelled Adversary Ops on WarCom |

**Naming conflict (honest):** Dossier OCR uses **Nemesis Ops**; WarCom Sunday Preview labels Archivist’s second mission **Adversary Ops**. Prefer the term printed on the mission you are playing; do not force a single rename without physical confirmation of every card title.

## Cards (qualitative)

Secondary sources + dossier packaging describe a large card pack (trait / faction reference cards, Ambull- and Archivist-specific cards) plus blank dry-erase Nemesis datacards. Exact counts belong to the owned product listing / box — verify on your copy.
