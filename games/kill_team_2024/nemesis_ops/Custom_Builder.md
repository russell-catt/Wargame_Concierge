# Nemesis Custom Builder — axes reference

**Confidence: verified** for the **five-step pipeline** (vision spot-check 2026-08-17). Companion to [`How_To_Create_A_Nemesis_Operative.md`](How_To_Create_A_Nemesis_Operative.md). No weapon/trait numeric tables from the dossier.

## Builder pipeline (summary)

| Step | Axis | Player note |
|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Allegiance keyword | Faction family keyword + allegiance trait |
| 2 | Size S / M / L | Power vs footprint vs how many allies share the team |
| 3 | Behaviour (NPO) | Action-priority loop for non-player Nemesis |
| 4 | Weapons | Slot budget from size; unused slots → extra traits |
| 5 | Nemesis traits | Narrative differentiation; no duplicates |

## Core Nemesis feel (qualitative)

Dossier teaching pages emphasise high durability, multiple activations per turning point, and careful target priority — a Nemesis ignored mid-board will punish you. **Control** is called out as a Nemesis-specific control/APL-related stat used for markers and comparisons (exact values: owned book only).
