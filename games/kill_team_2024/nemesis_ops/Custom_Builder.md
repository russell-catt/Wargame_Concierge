# Nemesis Custom Builder — axes reference

**Confidence: verified** for the **five-step pipeline** (vision spot-check 2026-08-17). Companion to [`How_To_Create_A_Nemesis_Operative.md`](How_To_Create_A_Nemesis_Operative.md). No weapon/trait numeric tables from the dossier.

**Rules currency: Kill Team quarterly balance — August 2026** (Core / update logs + team online rules) · teaching paraphrase · verify owned PDFs · confidence `draft`.

## Builder pipeline (summary)

| Step | Axis | Player note |
|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Allegiance keyword | Faction family keyword + allegiance trait |
| 2 | Size S / M / L | Power vs footprint vs how many allies share the team |
| 3 | Behaviour (NPO) | Action-priority loop for non-player Nemesis |
| 4 | Weapons | Slot budget from size; unused slots → extra traits |
| 5 | Nemesis traits | Narrative differentiation; no duplicates |

## Core Nemesis feel (qualitative)

Dossier teaching pages emphasise high durability, multiple activations per turning point, and careful target priority — a Nemesis ignored mid-board will punish you. **Control** is called out as a Nemesis-specific control/APL-related stat used for markers and comparisons (exact values: owned book only).

## Step 2 addendum — keep Towering Size (August 2026 commentary)

Paraphrase from `eng_mission_packs_update_log-51t6hsixc0-buxngu8xav.pdf` (staging, read 2026-08-27). This is a rules-commentary Q&A, not a text change to the Nemesis rules themselves — it clarifies how to use the **Size** axis (step 2 above) when building a custom Nemesis:

- Custom Nemesis operatives should **almost always keep the Towering Size rule.** Removing it is the exception, not a default option — the named exceptions that *do* remove it (Ambull, Archivist) are ready-made NPOs from the official mission packs, not a general licence to drop it on your own build.
- Even for a **Small** custom Nemesis where you're tempted to drop Towering Size, the commentary still recommends keeping the two practical effects it grants: the operative **cannot be given a Conceal order**, and it **can be selected as a valid target even within another operative's control range.**

**Teaching takeaway:** when in doubt at step 2, leave Towering Size on. Only skip it if you're deliberately building something that plays like Ambull or the Archivist — a small, sneakable Nemesis — and even then, keep the no-Conceal / always-a-valid-target behaviour.
