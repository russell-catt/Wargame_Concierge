# Killzone: Shadowhunt (Descent) - boards and tokens owned, gated on Tomb World

**Status: Boards + tokens owned.** Playable in a limited way now; its headline feature needs [Tomb World](tomb_world.md) assembled first.

---

## What it is

**Kill Team: Shadowhunt** is a boxed expansion bringing two new teams - **Murderwing** and **Celestian Insidiants** (both tracked as stubs in [`../../teams/README.md`](../../teams/README.md)) - and a new way to play called **Descent**. Rather than plastic terrain, the box ships with **two half-size, double-sided cardboard game boards** designed to be used together: one side of each pairs with Killzone: Volkus (surface fighting), the other with Killzone: Tomb World (underground close-quarters), so a Descent mission stacks an "upper" and "lower" killzone and lets operatives move between them at marked **entryways**.

What the household owns from this box, per the ownership lock: **the two boards and the token sheets** - not necessarily the two teams or the full dossier. Confirm against the physical box before planning a Murderwing-vs-Celestian-Insidiants session.

---

## Why it's gated on Tomb World

Descent missions are built to combine **Killzone: Volkus** terrain (upper board) with **Killzone: Tomb World** terrain (lower board). The Shadowhunt boards give you the *map*, but you still need the actual terrain pieces from both killzones to build the table. Since [Killzone: Tomb World is currently unassembled](tomb_world.md), full Descent play isn't available yet.

This creates the slightly odd-looking order in the track priority list - **Volkus and 3e Starter → Shadowhunt → Tomb World (after assembly)** - even though Shadowhunt's flagship mode needs Tomb World. Read it as: get comfortable with the core game on Volkus/Starter first, then treat Shadowhunt's boards as ready to go **the moment** Tomb World terrain is built, rather than needing a separate slice of setup work at that point.

---

## What's usable right now

- The Volkus side of the Descent boards can, at a pinch, stand in for a folded/half-sized Volkus layout if you want a smaller, faster table - using them purely as a single-level Volkus surface, not for a full Descent mission.
- The token sheets are usable in any KT24 game that needs generic tokens, regardless of killzone.
- Full Descent missions (the actual point of the box) wait on Tomb World terrain being assembled.

---

## Setting it up (once Tomb World is assembled)

1. Fold/arrange the Volkus terrain to fit the half-size upper board; set the Tomb World terrain up on the half-size lower board per the Shadowhunt dossier's Descent map.
2. Place entryway markers as the mission specifies - these are what let operatives move between the two boards.
3. Play a Descent mission from the dossier, or one of its single-level Descent-style maps if you'd rather ease in with just one board in play.
