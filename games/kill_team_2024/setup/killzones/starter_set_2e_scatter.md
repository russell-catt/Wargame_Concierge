# 2e Starter Scatter - filler pieces, not a killzone

**Status: Filler terrain only.** Useful as an add-on to a real killzone board; cannot stand in for one.

---

## What it is

This is the leftover terrain from the **2022 (2e) Kill Team Starter Set** - a much lighter box than a full boxed killzone. That set shipped with:

- **"Killzone Essentials"**: three combat gauges, six Kill Team barricades, and a sheet of counters
- **One sprue of Ork scatter terrain** (small ruined pieces, no full structures)
- A **paper battle mat**, not a cardboard or MDF board

There's no board here worth using on its own, and the scatter pieces are a fraction of what a boxed killzone like Volkus gives you - by design, the 2022 Starter Set was built to be *cheaper* than a full killzone box, at the cost of a much thinner table.

---

## Why it can't substitute for a real killzone

- **No board.** The paper mat that came with it isn't a substitute for the double-sided boards used elsewhere in this collection.
- **No structures.** No strongholds, ruins, or anything with a Vantage level - a table built only from these pieces has nowhere to fight for high ground.
- **Too few pieces.** A single sprue of scatter terrain and six barricades doesn't reach the "enough Dense/Heavy terrain to break fire lanes" bar described in [`../Terrain_Basics.md`](../Terrain_Basics.md).

## Edition-drift caution

These pieces originate from the **2021 edition (2e)** product line. Kill Team's terrain-type wording changed between 2e and 3e (KT24) - a barricade's exact terrain type(s) should be re-confirmed against the current [`../Terrain_Basics.md`](../Terrain_Basics.md) definitions rather than assumed from 2e habits. Per the track lock, **never mix 2e rules into `games/kill_team_2024/`** - use these as physical props only, categorised under current KT24 terrain types.

---

## How to actually use it

- Drop a barricade or two into a Volkus or Starter Set 3e layout to add extra Light cover in an open lane - not as the layout's backbone.
- Good for **Join Ops** casual sessions where table dressing matters more than tournament-strict terrain counts.
- Not recommended as the sole terrain for anything but the most casual, rules-light game.

---

## What to check before using these pieces

- The physical piece count actually owned (this page assumes the standard 2022 box contents; verify against what's on the shelf)
- Current KT24 terrain type for each barricade/scatter piece, agreed with your opponent before the game
