## Cityfight callouts (Volkus-only)

| Rule | Remember |
|------|----------|
| **Condensed Stronghold** | Shooting with **Blast**, **Torrent**, and/or **x" Devastating** also gets **Lethal 5+** if **that target** is **wholly within** a stronghold **and** on the **killzone floor or a fire step**. Check **each** secondary target separately — primary inside does **not** gift Lethal to a secondary outside. |
| **Door Fight** | Select Enemy Operative: enemy on the **killzone floor**, within **2"** of, and on the **other side of**, a door the active operative is **touching**. |
| **Fire step + obscured** | Being on a fire step does **not** let you ignore the rest of the stronghold for **obscured**. |
| **Garrisoned Stronghold** | Friendly wholly within a stronghold **retaliating** vs an enemy that isn’t: **defender resolves first**. |
