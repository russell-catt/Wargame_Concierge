## Why this order

- **Volkus and the 3e Starter Set** need no assembly beyond MDF slot-together terrain (Starter Set) or terrain already available (Volkus), and both come with beginner-friendly missions - the honest play-now pair for two people learning together.
- **Shadowhunt** is listed next because its boards and tokens are ready to use in a limited way today, and it becomes fully playable **the moment** Tomb World terrain exists - no separate setup slice needed later.
- **Tomb World** needs bench time (25 terrain pieces, 74 components) before its Close Quarters missions - and Shadowhunt's Descent missions - are available.
- **2e starter scatter** is a small prop kit from an older, cheaper starter box - useful for dressing a table, never a substitute for a real killzone.
