# 3e Starter Set - the built-in first game

**Status: READY.** This is the other play-now recommendation alongside [Volkus](volkus.md), and it's arguably the *better* first choice: everything needed for a first game ships in one box.

---

## What it is

The **Kill Team Starter Set** released in November 2024 for the new (3rd) edition. It contains two complete kill teams - **Angels of Death** (Space Marines) and **Plague Marines** (Death Guard) - plus everything two brand-new players need:

- A 64-page **Starter Handbook** with simplified rules and a series of introductory scenarios
- Reference sheets (one per team)
- A **double-sided game board**
- **Three sheets of slot-together MDF terrain** - no glue, no paint required to play
- Tokens (including measurement aids) and ten dice

Because the two included teams are **Angels of Death** and **Plague Marines** - both on this project's priority team list (see [`../../teams/README.md`](../../teams/README.md)) - this box also doubles as the starting point for those full team guides once written.

---

## What makes it a good first killzone

- **The board and terrain are sized for the intro missions in the handbook** - you don't have to make a single layout decision for your very first game.
- **MDF terrain slides together without glue** and comes apart again for storage, so there's zero build barrier between opening the box and playing.
- **The handbook's scenarios are simplified on purpose** - expect a shorter, more linear game sequence than the full matched-play sequence in [`../Board_Setup.md`](../Board_Setup.md). Use it exactly as written for game one, then graduate to the full sequence.

---

## Setting it up

1. Build the MDF terrain sheets per the handbook's diagrams (no assembly beyond slotting pieces together).
2. Lay out the board and terrain for whichever introductory scenario you're playing, following the handbook's own map.
3. Use the handbook's own simplified game sequence for your first couple of games - it's deliberately easier than the full Approved Ops sequence.
4. Once comfortable, move to the full sequence in [`../Board_Setup.md`](../Board_Setup.md) and either keep using this board or switch to [Volkus](volkus.md).
