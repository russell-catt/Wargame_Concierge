Warhammer 40,000 — System Quick Reference

Shortened teaching paraphrase. Bracket IDs = Core Rules. Active player = whose turn it is [01.03]. No faction rules on this sheet.

6End of TurnNon-mission triggers; score mission VP; fix coherency

Command [08]

  • Core CP [08.02]: both players gain +1 CP.
  • Battle-shock [08.03]: roll for already battle-shocked units and units at/below half-strength (2D6 vs Ld).
  • While battle-shocked: OC = 0; cannot be targeted by stratagems; cannot perform actions.
  • Then Command abilities / army rules that trigger here; End of Command.

System Quick Reference — Charge, Fight & Attacks

Page 2 of 2 · Core IDs in brackets · Teaching paraphrase

Charge phase [11]

2D6 first [11.02]. Roll charge distance before locking targets. The roll caps both eligible enemies and how far you may move. You cannot switch to a closer foe that was outside the rolled distance.
  • Eligible: on the battlefield; within 12" of an enemy; unengaged; did not Advance or Fall Back this turn.
  • Nominate targets within 12" and within the rolled distance; move; end engaged (typically within 1" of a target).
  • If you cannot engage all nominated targets, the charge fails — nothing moves.
  • A unit that completes a charge has Fights First for the rest of the turn.
  • Opponent may answer with Heroic Intervention (1CP) at end of your Charge phase.

Fight phase [12]

  1. Pile In [12.02] — eligible units, up to 3". Active player first, then opponent. Models already in base contact stay put.
  2. Fight [12.04] — both players alternate. Resolve all Fights First units first (starting with the active player), then everyone else. Each model uses one melee weapon vs engaged targets. You must fight with every unit that can.
  3. Consolidate [12.07] — up to 3"; active then opponent. Stay engaged if already; else engage within 3"; else move onto an objective within 3" when able.

Pile-in and consolidate are separate steps. Objective consolidation is a scoring tool, not an afterthought.

Attack sequence [05]

Select weapons → select targets → resolve [04]. Same four rolls for shooting and melee.

StepRule
Hit [05.01]D6 ≥ BS/WS. Unmod 1 fails. Unmod 6 = critical hit.
Wound [05.02]S vs T (bands below). Unmod 1 fails. Unmod 6 = critical wound.
Save [05.03]Defender: armour (AP applies) or invulnerable (no AP). Unmod 1 fails.
Damage [05.04]Unsaved attacks cost D wounds. Excess does not spill to the next model.

Allocation: defender groups by W/Sv/InSv; already-wounded group first; characters last (unless [PRECISION]).

OC reminder [14]

  • Terrain footprint is usually the objective — models inside the area count.
  • Sum OC at end of every phase; higher controls; tie = nobody.
  • Battle-shocked units contribute OC 0.
