Attack sequence recap (05)

  1. Hit (05.01) — D6 vs BS/WS. Unmod 1 fails; unmod 6 = critical hit.
  2. Wound (05.02) — use matrix on page 1.
  3. Save (05.03) — defender: armour (AP applies) or invulnerable (no AP). Unmod 1 fails.
  4. Damage (05.04) — D wounds per unsaved hit; excess does not spill.

Allocation: defender groups by W/Sv/InSv; characters last; already-wounded group first.

Objective control reminder (14)

  • Terrain footprint is the objective — model inside the area counts.
  • Sum OC at end of every phase; higher total controls; tie = nobody.
  • Battle-shocked units have OC 0.
