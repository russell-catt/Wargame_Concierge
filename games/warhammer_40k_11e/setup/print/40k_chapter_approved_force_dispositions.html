Chapter Approved — Force Dispositions

Write your Force Disposition on the list at Muster. Detachment tags (MFM) end with the Disposition name — e.g. CRYPTEK CONCLAVE - 2DP - PRIORITY ASSETS. Caps: Primary 45VP (max 15/round) · Secondary 45VP · Battle Ready 10VP.

How matching works

  1. Each player picks one Force Disposition available from their detachments.
  2. On your Disposition card, find the opponent’s symbol → that line is your Primary.
  3. Unless both pick the same Disposition, Primaries are usually different. Read your card.

Pickup: may change Disposition each battle. Events: lock one Disposition for the whole event (five possible Primaries).

Twists (optional)

Twist cards can change core rules or Primaries. Kitchen table: optional if both agree. Events: usually off unless the TO says otherwise. Do not invent a Twist mid-game.

Starter-event checklist

  • ☐ Detachment named
  • ☐ Force Disposition in BIG letters
  • ☐ Opponent Disposition known before opening Primary text
  • ☐ Reading your Primary, not theirs
  • ☐ Layout A/B/C agreed · Twist: yes/no (default no)
  • ☐ Caps: 15VP this round / 45VP Primary game
