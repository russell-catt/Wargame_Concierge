# CHAPTER APPROVED — FORCE DISPOSITIONS | Page 1 of 2

**Why this laminate exists:** your detachment does not just give stratagems — it tags a **Force Disposition**. That tag decides which **Primary Mission** you play once you know your opponent’s tag. Forgetting it at the starter event breaks the whole mission step.

**Caps (events):** Primary **45VP** (max **15VP**/battle round) · Secondary **45VP** (max **15VP**/round) · Battle Ready **10VP**.

---

## The five Force Dispositions

| Disposition | What it usually asks you to do |
|-------------|-------------------------------|
| **Take and Hold** | Hold objectives. Fewest Actions. Classic “stand on flags.” |
| **Purge the Foe** | Kill enemy units **and** still touch objectives. |
| **Disruption** | Hit-and-run: Actions, terrain, denying the opponent’s plan. |
| **Reconnaissance** | Spread out / table quarters / Actions in key spots. |
| **Priority Assets** | **Action-heavy** on objectives (often Central / enemy half), plus hold Expansion. |

Your detachment’s MFM line looks like: `CRYPTEK CONCLAVE - 2DP - PRIORITY ASSETS`. The **last words** are the Force Disposition. **Write that name on your army list.** What `DP` expands to is **not** defined in owned docs — ignore the number for learning games; the Disposition name is load-bearing.

| This project’s detachments | Force Disposition |
|----------------------------|-------------------|
| Cryptek Conclave | **Priority Assets** (2DP) |
| Canoptek Court | **Take and Hold** (3DP) |
| Gladius Task Force | **Priority Assets** (3DP) |
| 1st Company Task Force | **Priority Assets** (2DP)* |
| Anvil Siege Force | **Take and Hold** (2DP)* |

\*Community/MFM pattern — confirm on your Marines MFM before an event.

---

## How matching works (the important bit)

1. **You** pick **one** Force Disposition available from your list’s detachments.
2. **Opponent** picks theirs the same way.
3. On **your** Force Disposition card, find the **opponent’s** disposition symbol.
4. The Primary Mission listed under that symbol is **your** Primary. They do the same on their card.

**Unless both players pick the same Disposition, you and your opponent usually have different Primaries.** You can both score 45VP on different plans. Read **your** card every game — do not assume you share the same “hold three objectives” text.

**Pickup / kitchen table:** you may change Disposition each battle (if your detachments allow more than one).  
**Events (Event Companion):** lock **one** Disposition when you submit the list — it stays for the whole event. That locks you into a set of **five** possible Primaries (one per opponent Disposition).

## Pre-game sequence (mission-relevant steps)

Full fourteen steps: [`Board_Setup.md`](Board_Setup.md). Mission spine:

| # | Step | Force Disposition angle |
|---|------|-------------------------|
| 1 | **Muster Armies** | Build list · **pick & write Force Disposition** |
| 2 | **Determine Mission** | Match Disposition cards → each player’s Primary |
| 3 | **Determine a Layout** | Each pairing has layouts **A / B / C** (organizer picks or roll) |
| 4 | **Create the Battlefield** | 44"×60" events; place terrain per layout |
| 5 | **Attacker / Defender** | Agree edges to layout; roll; winner picks role |
| 6 | **Secondaries** | Fixed or Tactical (secret, then reveal) |
| 7–11 | Formations → deploy → redeploy → first turn → pre-battle | Same as Board_Setup |
| 12–14 | Play · end · total VP | Score **your** Primary + Secondaries |

> **Deploying second is not a punishment.** Defender deploys first; Attacker sees the board. First turn is a **separate** roll-off.

## Twists (optional)

**Twist** cards are an optional Chapter Approved layer. They change the game (examples from WarCom previews: whole army gets Hidden; swap Primaries; move through terrain like Infantry).

| Format | Twists |
|--------|--------|
| **Kitchen table / casual** | Optional — agree before step 2, draw one if you want chaos |
| **Event Companion events** | Usually **off** unless the TO says otherwise |

Do **not** invent a Twist mid-game. If you are not using the physical deck, skip Twists entirely for learning games.

**Optional Mirrored World-style Twists** (when present on your deck) can make both players use the **same** Primary — useful for teaching, rare at events.

## Starter-event checklist (tape this to the list)

- [ ] Detachment named on the list
- [ ] **Force Disposition written in BIG letters** (e.g. PRIORITY ASSETS)
- [ ] Opponent’s Disposition known before you open Primary text
- [ ] You are reading **your** Primary card, not theirs
- [ ] Layout letter (A/B/C) agreed
- [ ] Twist: yes / no (default **no** for first events)
- [ ] Primary cap remembered: **15VP this round / 45VP game**

---

## Common mistakes (from the table)

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| List has detachment but **no Force Disposition** | Write it at Muster (step 1). No Disposition = cannot Determine Mission |
| Both players read **one** Primary and share it | Match cards — usually **two** Primaries |
| Playing Court list but scoring like Conclave Actions | Court is **Take and Hold** — hold flags; Conclave is **Priority Assets** — Actions |
| Ignoring layout letter | Same Disposition pair, three different boards |
| Forgetting round / game VP caps | Bank early; you cannot dump 30VP in round 5 |
