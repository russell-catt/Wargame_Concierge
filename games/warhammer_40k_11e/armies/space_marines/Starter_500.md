# Starter list - 500 points

500 points is the first size that feels like a real game of Warhammer 40,000. You get a character worth protecting, a gun worth aiming, and enough units that the [Combat Doctrines](Gladius_Task_Force.md) start mattering.

**Every points value below was read from the owned Munitorum Field Manual (Marines) v1.2 on 2026-08-16.**

| **Lieutenant** (Support) | 1 | **45** | `TBD` | **new** |

| **Assault Intercessor Squad** | 5 | **75** | `TBD` | from 250 |
| **Hellblaster Squad** | 5 | **110** | `TBD` | **new** |
| **Infernus Squad** | 5 | **85** | `TBD` | **new** |
| | | **500** | | |

**How to attach:** Captain with **Fire Discipline** leads the **Hellblasters**; Lieutenant supports the **Assault Intercessors**.

That Captain placement is the whole point of the list. Fire Discipline gives his squad `[SUSTAINED HITS 1]`, so the Hellblasters - already your best gun - generate extra hits every single Shooting phase without you having to remember a trigger. Combine it with an [Oath of Moment](Oath_of_Moment.md) target and one squad is doing most of your damage. That is a good thing to learn early: **Space Marines win by concentrating, not by spreading.**

## Path B - Primaris with a walker: 495 / 500

Same core, but the fifth slot buys a vehicle instead of two more infantry squads. Take this one if the audit turns up a Dreadnought.

| Unit | Models | MFM Marines v1.2 | Ownership | New? |
|------|--------|------------------|-----------|------|
| **Captain** (Leader) | 1 | **80** | `TBD` | from 250 |
| **Intercessor Squad** | 5 | **80** | `TBD` | from 250 |
| **Assault Intercessor Squad** | 5 | **75** | `TBD` | from 250 |
| **Hellblaster Squad** | 5 | **110** | `TBD` | **new** |
| **Ballistus Dreadnought** | 1 | **150** | `TBD` | **new** |
| | | **495** | | |

**Why a Dreadnought is a good teaching model:** it is a single big target with high Toughness and a heavy gun, so it teaches you how armour saves, AP and Damage interact without the bookkeeping of a ten-model squad. It also cannot be attached to anything, so there is one less rule to remember.

**Costed alternatives if the box holds a different walker:** Redemptor Dreadnought **195**, Brutalis Dreadnought **150**, plain Dreadnought **135**. Swapping in the plain Dreadnought brings the list to **480** and leaves room for **The Honour Vehement** (15) on the Captain, for exactly 495.

| | | **500** | | |

The Lieutenant from the 250 list steps out and a Captain steps in, because the Captain is a Leader and can carry an enhancement onto the front line. If you would rather keep both characters, drop the Rhino and the enhancement: Captain 80 + Lieutenant 45 + Tactical 140 + Scouts 65 + Dreadnought 135 = **465**.

**The Rhino is the lesson here.** Transports change how a game feels - your slow ten-marine squad suddenly threatens the far objective on turn one. Check the transport capacity on your own datasheet before you plan around it, and remember that a destroyed transport dumps its passengers where it died.

## What the second 250 points actually buys

Not "more models" - four specific capabilities you did not have at 250.

| Capability | Which addition provides it | Why it matters |
|------------|---------------------------|----------------|
| **A gun that scares things** | Hellblasters, or the Dreadnought | At 250 you have nothing that reliably threatens a tough target. Now you do |
| **A second attachment** | The Lieutenant | Two buffed squads instead of one, and a lesson in Leader vs Support |
| **A reason to use enhancements** | Fire Discipline / The Honour Vehement | 15-25 points is a real fraction of 250 but a sensible one of 500 |
| **Doctrine decisions that bite** | Five units instead of three | With three units, a doctrine helps one of them. With five, spending it wrong genuinely costs you |

**Enhancements cost points, not money.** Fire Discipline and The Honour Vehement are written on your list, not bought in a box.

**A warning about buying more than one of something:** several Space Marine datasheets charge more for later copies of the same unit. A second Ballistus Dreadnought is **160**, not 150. A third Intercessor-style squad, a second Desolation Squad, a second Whirlwind - all price up. Check the "your 1st to 2nd units cost / your 3rd + unit costs" lines in the Munitorum Field Manual before you assume two of a thing costs double.
