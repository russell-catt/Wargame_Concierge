# Starter list - 250 points

A first game should be small, short, and mostly about learning the phase order. 250 points is roughly three or four units a side and finishes in an evening.

**Every points value below was read from the owned Munitorum Field Manual (Marines) v1.2 on 2026-08-16.** Note that this is a **separate file** from the main Munitorum Field Manual used for the Necron lists.

Attach the **Captain to the Intercessors**. That puts your best character with the squad that will be standing on an objective shooting all game, which is where you want him for a learning game.

Fifteen points spare. Leave them - spare points cost nothing and no enhancement is worth taking at this size.

## Path B - older Firstborn collection: 250 / 250 exactly

If the collection is a shelf of pre-Primaris marines, this is the list. All three entries are confirmed still matched-play legal in MFM Marines v1.2.

| Unit | Models | MFM Marines v1.2 | Ownership |
|------|--------|------------------|-----------|
| **Lieutenant** (Support) | 1 | **45** | `TBD` |
| **Tactical Squad** | 10 | **140** | `TBD` |
| **Scout Squad** | 5 | **65** | `TBD` |
| | | **250** | |

Attach the **Lieutenant to the Tactical Squad**. A Lieutenant is a **Support** unit in 11th Edition, not a Leader - and a Support unit can attach to a bodyguard squad on its own, without a Leader. Confirmed in the owned Core Rules, read 2026-08-16.

**Watch the Scouts.** The old Scouts box builds two different things: a **Scout Squad** (legal, 65 points) or a **Scout Sniper Squad** (**Legends**, not matched-play legal). Look at what the models are holding before you write them down.

## Older-kit and Firstborn swap notes

If the audit turns up a mix, these are the substitutions that keep a list legal and roughly the same size. Costs are MFM Marines v1.2, 2026-08-16.

| If you have this older kit | Use it as | MFM v1.2 | Swaps for |
|----------------------------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Tactical Squad (10) | Tactical Squad | **140** | An Intercessor Squad plus most of a second squad |
| Devastator Squad (5) | Devastator Squad | **120** | Your shooting unit, at a premium |
| Scout Squad (5) | Scout Squad | **65** | Cheapest legal body count in the faction |
| Old plastic Dreadnought | Dreadnought | **135** | Over half your 250 - better saved for the 500 list |
| Rhino | Rhino | **65** | Nothing at 250. Transports are a round-two lesson |
| Terminator Squad (5) | Terminator Squad | **160** | Too expensive for a 250-point game |
| Sternguard Veterans (5) | Sternguard Veteran Squad | **100** | A straight upgrade over an Intercessor Squad |
| Vanguard Veterans w/ jump packs (5) | Vanguard Veteran Squad with Jump Packs | **105** | Your melee squad, if you want a faster one |

**Kits that will not work in a matched-play list**, confirmed against the Legends section of the owned faction pack v1.1: Assault Squad (with or without jump packs), Command Squad, Bike Squad, Attack Bike Squad, Scout Bike Squad, Scout Sniper Squad, Land Speeder Tornado and Typhoon, Thunderfire Cannon, Venerable Dreadnought, Mortis Dreadnought, Relic Terminator Squad.

**They are not useless.** Agree with your opponent before the game that a Legends unit is standing in for something, write down what it counts as, and play. For a father-and-son learning game that is entirely reasonable. Just never let it into a list that claims to be legal.

## What this list is teaching you

Three or four units is enough to practise everything that matters and few enough to finish a turn in five minutes.

| Unit | Its job in the learning game |
|------|------------------------------|
| **Intercessors** (or Tactical Squad) | Stand on an objective and shoot. Practise counting Objective Control and the four-step attack sequence |
| **Assault Intercessors** (or Infernus) | Walk at the middle objective and fight for it. Practise charges, pile-in and consolidate |
| **Scouts** | Practise pre-game movement and screening - cheap bodies in the right place |
| **Captain / Lieutenant** | Practise attaching a character before the game and remembering he is part of the squad |

**The two rules you must not forget**, one per phase, both at the start of your Command phase:

1. **Pick your Oath of Moment target.** One enemy unit, re-roll your hits against it all round. See [`Oath_of_Moment.md`](Oath_of_Moment.md).
2. **Decide whether to spend a Combat Doctrine** - and remember each one is once per battle. See [`Gladius_Task_Force.md`](Gladius_Task_Force.md).

Put two tokens on the table edge. One goes on the oathed enemy unit; one sits on your army list next to the doctrine you have spent.
