# How your army works — 1000 Gladius

> **FORCE DISPOSITION: PRIORITY ASSETS**  
> List: [`Army_List_1000_Matched_Statted.md`](Army_List_1000_Matched_Statted.md) · System cheat: [`print/40k_11e_cheat_sheet_wounds.html`](print/40k_11e_cheat_sheet_wounds.html)

**One-sentence plan:** Build a turn-one firing base with the shooting Terminators on the table, hold objectives with Captain + Tacticals, improve the Whirlwind with the Techmarine, then Deep Strike the Chaplain + Assault Terminators into the fight that matters.

## Your six battlefield pieces

| Piece | Job | Default action | Avoid |
|-------|-----|----------------|-------|
| **Captain + Tacticals** | OC / Actions / midboard | Claim Expansion or Central; make mission Actions; counter-charge | Chasing a fast unit away from scoring |
| **Devastators** | Direct anti-armour | Remain Stationary for Signum; shoot Oath | Hiding so well they have no lane |
| **Shooting Terminators** | Durable turn-one fire | Start on table; occupy a lane / objective; storm-bolter and heavy-weapon pressure | Reserving them and losing turn-one shots |
| **Chaplain + Assault Terminators** | Deep Strike melee hammer | Arrive turn 2–3 near an objective or safe charge setup | Dropping in the open for a desperate long charge |
| **Techmarine** | Whirlwind support | Stay independent within 3"; Blessing every Command phase | Attaching and losing vehicle-side Lone Operative |
| **Whirlwind** | Indirect pressure | Hide; shoot scoring Infantry; force Battle-shock | Treating it as a front-line tank |

## Deployment map

| Area | Put here | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| Backfield obscuring | Whirlwind + Techmarine within 3" | Vehicle survives; Techmarine repairs / buffs |
| Best direct-fire lane | Devastators in cover | Signum and long-range guns |
| Safe lane toward a non-home objective | Shooting Terminators | Contribute on turn one, then become a durable blocker |
| Route to Expansion / Central | Captain + Tacticals | OC20 before the Captain; main Action unit |
| Deep Strike | Chaplain + Assault Terminators only | **235 pts**, safely below the reserves cap |

## Command-phase script

1. Gain CP.
2. **Name the Oath target.**
3. Techmarine uses **Blessing of the Omnissiah** on Whirlwind.
4. Decide whether a once-per-battle doctrine is actually needed.
5. From round 2 onward, decide whether the Assault brick arrives now or stays hidden one more round.

## Oath of Moment

Name one enemy at the start of your Command phase. Against it, re-roll Hits; this pure Codex Space Marines list also gets +1 Wound.

**Best Oath target:** a unit you can attack with at least two pieces, a unit contesting your scoring objective, or the unit the Assault brick will fight. Do not choose the scariest enemy if most of your army cannot reach it.

## Gladius doctrines

| Doctrine | Permission | This list's use |
|----------|------------|-----------------|
| **Devastator** | Advance and still shoot | Reposition the firing line without losing a whole Shooting phase |
| **Tactical** | Fall Back, then shoot and charge | Free Tacticals or shooting Terminators from a bad tie-up |
| **Assault** | Advance and charge | Give the Captain brick or a landed Assault brick extra reach |

Each is once/battle. Skip a round when no doctrine helps.

## Priority Assets Primaries — Secure Asset, Vital Link, Sabotage

**Confidence: draft teaching shape. Your physical Chapter Approved card wins.** Caps are normally 15VP per round and 45VP per game; confirm on the mission.

### 1. Secure Asset — vs Take and Hold

| Mission ask | Your answer |
|-------------|-------------|
| Action on a non-home objective | Captain + Tacticals are the reliable Action brick; shooting Terminators can substitute |
| Destroy a unit on a Central | Oath it; combine Devastators, shooting Terminators and Whirlwind |
| Hold several non-home objectives | Captain brick holds one; shooting Terminators hold another; Assault brick steals / contests late |
| Protect home late | Whirlwind has OC3 but should not fight; rotate shooting Terminators back if the enemy threatens home |

**Win condition:** bank the Action points and deny Hold More. Do not abandon two objectives to chase one damaged enemy.

### 2. Vital Link — vs Purge the Foe

| Mission ask | Your answer |
|-------------|-------------|
| Repeated Central Action / markers | Captain + Tacticals establish the link |
| Hold Central and another non-home | Shooting Terminators support Central or Expansion |
| Avoid feeding Kill More | Keep Techmarine and Whirlwind screened; Deep Strike brick arrives where it survives |
| Threaten enemy home late | Assault Terminators are the late-game threat, not a turn-two suicide unit |

**Win condition:** make the Captain brick hard to shift while the gunline removes one threat at a time.

### 3. Sabotage — vs Priority Assets

| Mission ask | Your answer |
|-------------|-------------|
| Multiple non-home Actions | Tacticals take the safest; shooting Terminators take the second; Assault brick can Action after landing if no charge is needed |
| Bonus in enemy territory / Central | Use the Deep Strike brick for a high-value location after enemy screens thin |
| Deny enemy Action units | Whirlwind pressures hidden Infantry; direct guns cover Central lanes |
| Hold one non-home from round 2 | Shooting Terminators or Captain brick must remain assigned to that objective |

**Win condition:** at 1,000 points you have enough durable units to split jobs. Do not make the Whirlwind or Techmarine expose themselves for an early low-value Action.

### 5. Vanguard Operation — vs Reconnaissance

| Mission ask | Your answer |
|-------------|-------------|
| Action in enemy-territory terrain | Assault Terminators are ideal after Deep Strike if the area is clear |
| Kill at least one unit | Oath a reachable scoring unit; finish it rather than splitting fire |
| Hold a non-home objective | Captain brick or shooting Terminators |
| Enemy-home control at end game | Assault brick only after it has banked safer points |
| Keep a model in enemy territory | Surviving claw Terminator is enough; do not expose the Chaplain needlessly |

**Win condition:** use Deep Strike for position first and damage second. An Action that wins the mission can be better than a speculative charge.

# How your army works — 1000 Gladius

## The two Terminator jobs

| Shooting Terminators | Assault Terminators + Chaplain |
|----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Start on table | Start in Deep Strike |
| Shoot from turn one | No meaningful ranged profile |
| Hold a lane / objective | Threaten a late objective or decisive fight |
| Fury of the First helps vs Oath | Chaplain adds +1 Wound in melee |
| Chainfists deter vehicles | Claws clear Infantry; one hammer threatens elites |

Do not swap these roles mid-plan without a mission reason. The shooters already paid for guns; the melee unit loses nothing by waiting off-board.

## Arrival choices

| Tool | Timing | Position | Best use |
|------|--------|----------|----------|
| Normal Deep Strike | Your Movement, round 2+ | Anywhere more than 8" from enemies | Take a safe objective, Action location, or supported charge lane |
| Rapid Ingress — 1CP | End of opponent Movement, not round 1 | Deep Strike position, more than 8" away | Hide behind terrain, then move and charge on your turn |
| Teleport Homer Ingress — 0CP once | Same Ingress timing | Near token and more than 8" away | Free arrival if the token stayed useful |

**Best default:** Rapid Ingress behind obscuring terrain. On your turn, the unit moves 5" before charging. This is safer than appearing in your Movement phase and depending on a long charge.

## Assault brick sequence

1. Choose a landing spot that cannot be erased by the opponent's remaining guns.
2. Oath the intended fight target on your next Command phase.
3. Move toward the objective / target.
4. Charge only if success leaves the unit somewhere useful.
5. Fight with the right profile: claws into bodies; hammer into a hard target; Chaplain adds reliable damage.
6. Consolidate onto the objective or into cover.

## Turn-one shooting plan

1. Bless the Whirlwind.
2. Keep Devastators stationary if they already have a lane.
3. Move shooting Terminators only enough to gain a target or objective.
4. Fire the weapons most likely to expose / weaken the Oath target.
5. Use high-damage shots before low-value cleanup if the target has limited wounds.
6. Whirlwind finishes hidden / damaged Infantry or pressures a scoring unit.

## Whirlwind: “spotters” and indirect fire

There is no dedicated spotter rule or spotting Action. A **spotter** is simply any friendly unit that can currently see the target; that unit gives up nothing to do so.

| Shot | Hit floor | What applies |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| Whirlwind itself sees target; use Normal Shooting | Normal BS (usually 3+; Tech blessing can improve it) | No indirect penalties; Oath Hit re-roll works |
| Target hidden; Whirlwind Remained Stationary; any friendly sees target | Unmodified **4+** | Target gets Cover; no Hit re-rolls; Oath +1 Wound still works |
| Target hidden and Whirlwind moved, or nobody friendly sees target | Unmodified **6** only | Usually choose another target |

Captain + Tacticals and the shooting Terminators are natural midboard spotters. Devastators can spot down their firing lane; the Assault brick can spot after arrival. Check visibility when the Whirlwind shoots.

Keep the Techmarine independent within 3". Blessing repairs and adds 1 to Hit, but cannot override indirect's **unmodified** 4+/6 floor. It is strongest when the Whirlwind sees its own target and uses Normal Shooting. Hide the artillery cell from direct anti-tank and screen enemy Deep Strike.

## Battle-shock

Every failed test gives the unit OC0, prevents your stratagems targeting it, prevents it starting or completing Actions, and makes Falling Back a risky Desperate Escape. Battle-shock does **not** normally stop basic movement, shooting, charging or fighting.

| Unit | What failure stops / costs | What still works |
|------|---------------------------|------------------|
| Captain + Tacticals | Objective control, Actions, targeted stratagems and Captain-discounted stratagem use | Move, shoot, charge, fight and Oath bonuses |
| Devastators | OC, Actions, Storm of Fire, Overwatch and defensive stratagems | Signum / Cherub and shooting |
| Shooting Terminators | Holding, Actions and all targeted stratagems | Shooting, Fury of the First, charging and fighting |
| Chaplain + Assault Terms | Objective flip, Actions, Honour the Chapter, Armour of Contempt and targeted re-roll | Move, charge, fight and Chaplain Litany |
| Techmarine | OC, Actions and stratagem protection | Bless / repair, shoot and fight |
| Whirlwind | OC and targeted defensive stratagems | Shoot, Pinning Bombardment and receive Tech blessing |

## Major weapon keywords

| Keyword | Meaning / use in this army |
|---------|----------------------------|
| `[HEAVY]` | +1 Hit if unengaged, not set up this turn and no model moved over 3"; Devastators like planted lanes |
| `[RAPID FIRE 2]` | +2 attacks within half range; Terminator storm bolters improve inside 12" |
| `[BLAST]` | +1 attack per five target models; cannot target engaged units |
| `[INDIRECT FIRE]` | Can target unseen units; forced Cover, no Hit re-roll, and the 4+/6 floor above |
| `[TORRENT]` / `[IGNORES COVER]` | Flamer auto-hits and denies Cover; excellent for Overwatch |
| `[SUSTAINED HITS 1]` | Critical Hit adds one extra hit; native on heavy bolter |
| `[MELTA 2]` | +2 Damage inside half range; multi-melta wants 9" |
| `[HAZARDOUS]` | Supercharged plasma makes a hazard roll after attacking; failure causes a mortal wound |
| `[DEVASTATING WOUNDS]` | Critical Wound becomes mortal damage; assault cannon / hammer |
| `[TWIN-LINKED]` | Re-roll Wounds, not Hits; Assault Terminator claws |
| `[ANTI-VEHICLE N+]` | Unmodified N+ against a Vehicle is critical; chainfist 3+, grav-pistol 2+ |
| `[ONE SHOT]` / `[EXTRA ATTACKS]` | Hunter-killer fires once; Tech servo-arm attacks in addition |


# How your army works — 1000 Gladius

## CP spends — list-specific card

Both players gain +1CP in each Command phase. Captain's **Rites of Battle** can discount one eligible stratagem targeting his attached unit once per round; follow the current datasheet wording.

### Highest priority

| Spend | CP | Timing | Use it when… |
|-------|----|--------|-------------|
| **Rapid Ingress** | 1 | End opponent Movement; not BR1 | Assault brick can land hidden, then move and charge next turn; Homer may make it free but restricts position |
| **Armour of Contempt** | 1 | Enemy attacks one unit | Real AP threatens Terms or Captain brick; do not spend against AP0 |
| **Command Re-roll** | 1 | Immediately after eligible roll | A decisive charge, key save or high-damage roll changes the turn |
| **Adaptive Strategy** | 1 | Your Command | One unit needs a doctrine the army skipped or already spent |

### On your turn

| Spend | CP | Phase | Best user / reminder |
|-------|----|-------|----------------------|
| **Storm of Fire** | 1 | Shooting | Terms / Devs gain `[IGNORES COVER]`; +1 AP under Devastator. It can cancel indirect's forced Cover but cannot improve the Whirlwind's Hit floor |
| **Honour the Chapter** | 1 | Fight | Assault brick gains `[LANCE]`; +1 AP under Assault. Best melee damage spend |
| **Epic Challenge** | 1 | Fight | Captain / Chaplain gains `[PRECISION]` when killing an attached Character matters |
| **Insane Bravery** | 1 | Command | Once/battle; preserve OC only when control changes scoring |
| **Only in Death Does Duty End** | 2 | Fight | Use only when destroyed, unswung Terminators will remove a key unit or flip an objective |

### On the opponent's turn

| Spend | CP | Timing | Best user / reminder |
|-------|----|--------|----------------------|
| **Squad Tactics** | 1 | Enemy finishes a move within 8" | Reactive D6" move (6" under Tactical): score, break a lane, or lengthen a charge |
| **Fire Overwatch** | 1 | Eligible enemy move / charge | Flamer Tacticals are best because `[TORRENT]` auto-hits; others usually hit only on unmodified 6 |
| **Heroic Intervention** | 1 | End of enemy Charge | Protect an objective-holder or pull the Assault brick into a nearby fight |
| **Counter-offensive** | 2 | Fight sequence | Interrupt only when waiting would lose a valuable unit before it fights |
| **Smokescreen** | 1 | Eligible ranged targeting | Whirlwind only if its current keywords satisfy the Core card |

## CP budget by round

| Round | Default |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Save at least 1CP for defence; no Rapid Ingress yet |
| 2 | Reserve 1CP for Rapid Ingress unless the Homer makes it free |
| 3 | Spend on the Assault brick's damage / defence if it has committed |
| 4–5 | Spend to preserve OC and complete mission Actions, not for flashy cleanup |

**Default priority:** keep 1CP for Ingress / defence; protect scoring units; improve the decisive Assault-brick fight; spend leftovers on damage. Battle-shocked units cannot be targeted by these stratagems.

## Megabattle — joint war plan with the Necrons + table etiquette

Team game: your 1000 Blood Ravens + Dad's 945 Necrons on one side. Source: megabattle prep notes at `raw/Megabattle_prep/` (2026-09-25), rules-checked against this pack. **confidence: draft.**

**The team plan in one line:** a shared **backline firebase** (your Whirlwind + Techmarine behind Dad's Doomstalker and Warrior Brick B), a shared **midboard push** (your Captain + Tacticals + Devastators beside his Brick A + Immortals), and a **pocket hammer** (his Scarabs set the trap; your Chaplain + Assault Terminators spring it). His bricks give you a wall that regrows; your Marines give his bricks the killing power they lack.

| Sector | Your Marine job | Dad's Necron job | Why it works |
|--------|-----------------|------------------|--------------|
| **1 · Backline firebase** (team corner) | Whirlwind hidden behind the Necron screen with a lane to the midboard; Techmarine within 3" and **Blessing every Command phase** (+1 to Hit, repair). Whirlwind shoots the scoring Infantry his Doomstalker cannot see — any friendly unit that sees the target unlocks the 4+ indirect floor when the Whirlwind stayed still. | Doomstalker never moves (3+ Heavy, 48"). Warrior Brick B + Technomancer stand in front of both guns on `[HEAVY]`; the Technomancer heals what gets shot. | Fast melee cannot reach your Whirlwind without going through 10 Warriors that reanimate. Anything that charges the zone eats Doomstalker Overwatch on **5+**. |
| **2 · Midboard push** (one shared flank) | Captain + Tacticals claim the objective; Devastators plant on the lane and Remain Stationary; shooting Terminators hold the near flag. Name **Oath** on the unit the whole team is shooting; Rites of Battle discounts one stratagem on the Captain's brick. | Brick A (30" / 18" guns) sits a step behind your Tacticals in cover with `[IGNORES COVER]`; Immortals take `[ANTI-INFANTRY 3+]`, or the Atomic anti-Vehicle pick if a war machine pushes the lane so you can keep shooting infantry. | Two OC-heavy blocks on Central and the Oath target dying every turn. **Oath only buffs your Marines** — Dad just points his guns at the same unit. |
| **3 · Pocket hammer** (turn 2–3 strike) | Turn 2+: Chaplain + Assault Terminators arrive by **Rapid Ingress** (1CP, end of enemy Movement, not BR1) or the Homer, landing behind the Scarab shield. Next turn: Oath the target, charge, Honour the Chapter if the fight decides the game. Claws (Twin-linked) into bodies, hammer into the hard target, Chaplain +1 to Wound on everything. | Turn 1: Scarabs sprint into enemy territory and sit in the face of the scariest unit — tarpit, −1 OC, and a screen for your landing. | His cheap bugs buy the safe landing; your 235 points of Terminators delete the scoring threat. Agree the target during *their* turn. |

**Rules checks on the prep notes:** Rapid Ingress cannot be used in battle round 1. Blessing follows the current Techmarine datasheet wording. The Chaplain's modeled lightning claw counts as a **relic shield** — say so before any model touches the table. Two Necron Warrior units of 10, not one of 20.

### Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Pre-measure during the enemy turn — know the Captain's move and the Terminators' landing spot before your turn starts | Argue a weird interaction — ask an organiser or roll off and keep moving |
| Roll simultaneously with Dad when you shoot different targets | Flip through books — use the CP spends card in this guide and your two or three favourite spends |
| Show proxies before deployment: Chaplain's claw = relic shield; which Assault Terminator is the hammer | Reach into another player's lane without asking — coordinate flanks through the team commanders |
| Announce targets out loud: "Devastators fire into *that* unit behind the ruin — Oath target" | Drop the Terminators into open ground for a long-odds charge |
| Name Oath in every Command phase, before anything else | Reserve the shooting Terminators — they start on the table |

### Turn-by-turn team beats

| Round | You | Together |
|-------|-----|----------|
| BR1 | Firebase set; Captain + Tacticals and shooting Terms step onto the shared flank; Bless the Whirlwind; Oath a reachable target | Agree the turn-2 hammer target while the enemy moves |
| BR2 | Rapid Ingress the Assault brick behind the Scarabs (hold 1CP for it); Storm of Fire on the decisive volley | Oath on the hammer target — Dad's guns add to it; he Syphons a brick in their Command |
| BR3 | Hammer charges: Honour the Chapter; Armour of Contempt when they answer | Midboard claims 3 flags; firebase Overwatch ready |
| BR4–5 | Hold flags; Squad Tactics onto scoring; Actions over kills | Do not overextend into a sector you did not agree |
