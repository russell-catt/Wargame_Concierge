# 1st Company Task Force - elite brick night

> **FORCE DISPOSITION — write on the list before you deploy**
> **Priority Assets** · MFM pattern: `1ST COMPANY TASK FORCE - 2DP - PRIORITY ASSETS` *(confirm on your Marines MFM)*
> How matching works: [`../../setup/Chapter_Approved_Force_Dispositions.md`](../../setup/Chapter_Approved_Force_Dispositions.md)

This is the **best alternate detachment for your owned Blood Ravens shelf** after Gladius. You already own what it wants: two Terminator Squads, Assault Terminators, and a Chaplain in Terminator Armour.

**MFM tag pattern (confirm on your Marines MFM before play):** community builders list it as **2DP · Priority Assets** (retrieved 2026-08-22). Gladius is 3DP Priority Assets — same mission family, cheaper DP sticker if that matters for your event’s detachment budget.

**Confidence:** `draft`. Gladius was cross-checked against your owned pack’s Gladius errata. This page has **not** had that owned-PDF pass yet — verify wording before a tournament.

## Why this fits *your* box

| Owned unit | Why 1st Company cares |
|------------|------------------------|
| **Terminator Squad** ×2 (5+5) | Core “veteran / Terminator” stratagem targets |
| **Terminator Assault Squad** (5) | Same veteran lane (watch mixed loadouts honesty) |
| **Chaplain in Terminator Armour** | Terminator Leader; claw counts as **Storm Shield** in game |
| Captain / Tacticals / Devastators | Still legal glue — the detachment does **not** ban Midboard OC |

Deathwatch veterans stay **out**. Primaris incompletes stay optional. Bikes / Servitors stay **Casual / Legends** only.

## The detachment rule, in plain terms

**Extremis-level Threat** (once per battle):

1. In **your Command phase**, you may flip this ability on.
2. Until the **start of your next Command phase**, every model in your army that has **Oath of Moment**, when attacking your **current Oath target**, may **re-roll Wound rolls** as well (on top of whatever Oath already gives you for hits / wound bonus — confirm stacking on your pack; teaching rule of thumb: this is the “make sure that one unit dies” turn).

You get **one** big turn. The skill is not “remember the rule exists” — it is **which battle round** you spend it.

### When to flip Extremis

| Flip when | Hold when |
|-----------|-----------|
| Your bricks can all see / reach the Oath target **this** turn | The Oath target is a chaff screen you will delete anyway |
| You need one enemy brick / vehicle / character **gone** before they activate | You have already spent Oath on something soft and cannot retarget usefully |
| Assault Terminators (or both Term squads) are about to swing or shoot into that target | Early round one with half the army still off the board |

**Default learning plan:** hold Extremis for the turn you commit both Terminator threats into the same Oath target (often round 2–3). Write on the list: `[ ] Extremis used on round ____`.

## Stratagems worth knowing on day one

Six stratagems. For a first 1st Company game, learn these four; skim the rest.

| Stratagem (short name) | CP | Player-facing use |
|------------------------|----|-------------------|
| **Heroes of the Chapter** | 1 | One Terminator / Bladeguard / Sternguard / Vanguard Veteran unit: **+1 to Hit**; if Below Half-strength, **+1 to Wound** as well |
| **Legendary Fortitude** | 1 | Veteran unit that was charged: reduce Damage of attacks by 1 (opponent’s Fight phase) — brick survival |
| **Orbital Teleportarium** | 1 | After opponent’s Fight phase: pick up a **Terminator** unit into Strategic Reserves; it returns next Movement phase via Deep Strike — reposition / re-threaten |
| **Armour of Contempt** | 1 | Worsen AP of attacks targeting your unit by 1 (shared SM defensive staple) |
| Duty and Honour | 1 | Objective sticky after a veteran unit leaves (scoring tech) |
| Terrifying Proficiency | 1 | Veteran unit charges and kills something → nearby enemies Battle-shock |

**Owned-shelf tip:** Heroes + Extremis on the same Oath target is the “delete that brick” combo. Orbital Teleportarium is how a slow Terminator unit stays relevant after the midboard locks up.

## Enhancements that match your models

Points below are from public 11e references (retrieved **2026-08-22**) — **re-check MFM Marines** before costing a list.

| Enhancement | Pts (draft) | Restriction | Why you care |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Rites of War** | **10** | Terminator model | +1 OC on the bearer; once/battle, +1 OC on the rest of the unit for a phase — flip objectives with a Term brick |
| **Iron Resolve** | **15** | Terminator model | Bearer FNP 5+; once/battle, whole unit FNP 5+ for a phase — natural on Term Chaplain or a Term-capable character |
| The Imperium’s Sword | ~25 | Character | Melee swing once/battle — less urgent for learning |
| Fear Made Manifest | ~30 | Character | Battle-shock tax — skip until comfortable |

**Learning pick:** **Rites of War** on the Terminator Chaplain if you want OC tricks; **Iron Resolve** if you want the brick to live through the Extremis turn.

## Gladius vs 1st Company (same models)

| | Gladius | 1st Company |
|--|---------|-------------|
| Skill test | When to spend three movement doctrines | When to spend **one** wound-reroll turn |
| Rewards | Whole army mobility | Terminator / veteran packages |
| Best when | Learning phase order | You want the Terminator shelf to be the star |

Stay on Gladius until doctrines feel automatic. Switch to 1st Company for a “Terminator night.”
