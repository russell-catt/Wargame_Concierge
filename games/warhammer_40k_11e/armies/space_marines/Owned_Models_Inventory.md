## Step 1 - Firstborn or Primaris?

| Tell | Firstborn (older, pre-2017 kits) | Primaris (newer) |
|------|----------------------------------|------------------|
| Height | Noticeably shorter | Roughly a head taller |
| Base | 32 mm, or **25 mm** on genuinely old models | 32 mm, larger for Gravis and heavier armour |
| Basic rifle | Boltgun - short, boxy | Bolt rifle - longer, with a magazine ahead of the grip |
| Build | Plain armour plates, softer detail | Sharper, more armoured, more panel lines |

**Photo ID 2026-08-21:** Blood Ravens folder is **Firstborn** (Mk VI / classic Terminator / bikes). Angels of Death Pics + used Intercessors are **Primaris**. Both play together under Codex SM — different datasheets.

## Chapter and mixing note

**Blood Ravens:** paint/lore only; **Codex Space Marines** rules (Gladius / Oath OK).

**Used Intercessors:** Black Templars paint — **not** played as BT.

**Deathwatch:** separate identity. If any **Deathwatch** units are ever mixed into a Codex SM army, Oath of Moment loses +1 to Wound — see [`Oath_of_Moment.md`](Oath_of_Moment.md). Do not mix casually into learning Gladius lists.

**AoD Pics cosmetics** (green Captain, yellow Assault, DW silver arms on some bodies): still dual-use as **Codex SM** per owner, distinct from the Deathwatch *team* folder.

## Inventory rules

1. **Game-ready** = assembled, based, and fieldable under current edition rules.
2. **Build before play** = purchased but not yet assembled.
3. **Legends** = owned and playable in friendly games by agreement, not matched-play costed.
4. **Shopping lists** must not double-count anything marked owned above.
5. Record the **datasheet name**, not the box name.
6. Prefer **Faction Pack + MFM** over 10e Codex PDFs for points/legality; Codex at `C:\Personal\40K\codexes\` is path-pointer / kit-ID only (paid, no large quotes, rotates).
