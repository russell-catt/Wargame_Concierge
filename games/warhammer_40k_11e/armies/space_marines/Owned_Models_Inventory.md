# Space Marine Owned Models Inventory

**Status:** Worksheet - **still not filled in.** This is the one blocker standing between the son and a legal list; everything else in this folder is ready and waiting.

Record assembly and paint state, not just counts. A box on the sprue is not a unit you can field this weekend.

---

## How to do the audit in one sitting

1. **Lay everything out**, including boxes still on sprue and anything half-painted in a drawer.
2. **Sort into three piles:** assembled, on sprue, and "no idea what this is".
3. **Work the "no idea" pile with the Firstborn/Primaris test below**, then the Legends check.
4. **Fill in the tables.** Then open [`Starter_250.md`](Starter_250.md), which is already costed against the most likely contents.

Photograph anything you cannot name. A photo of the sprue or the sergeant is usually enough to identify a kit.

---

## Step 1 - Firstborn or Primaris?

This is the first fork and it changes which datasheet a model uses. It is a size and proportion test, not a paint test.

| Tell | Firstborn (older, pre-2017 kits) | Primaris (newer) |
|------|----------------------------------|------------------|
| Height | Noticeably shorter | Roughly a head taller |
| Base | 32 mm, or **25 mm** on genuinely old models | 32 mm, larger for Gravis and heavier armour |
| Basic rifle | Boltgun - short, boxy | Bolt rifle - longer, with a magazine ahead of the grip |
| Build | Plain armour plates, softer detail | Sharper, more armoured, more panel lines |

**If both are in the collection, that is fine.** They are all Adeptus Astartes and they play together in the same army. They just use different datasheets, and one of the two piles may turn out to be Legends.

---

## Step 2 - is it Legends?

Some older kits still have a full matched-play datasheet. Others were moved to **Warhammer Legends** and are no longer legal in a matched-play list - they are fine in a friendly game if your opponent agrees, but they cannot be costed into a tournament-legal army.

**This list is not exhaustive.** If a kit is not named above, check the Legends section of your own faction pack or the Warhammer 40,000 app before costing it.

## Inventory rules

1. **Game-ready** = assembled, based, and fieldable under current edition rules.
2. **Build before play** = purchased but not yet assembled. Never list it in a "play tonight" list.
3. **Legends** = owned and playable in friendly games by agreement, but never costed into a matched-play list.
4. **Shopping lists** must not double-count anything marked owned above.
5. Record the **datasheet name**, not the box name. Boxes get renamed; datasheets are what you build a list from.
