# Anvil Siege Force - plant feet, shoot hard

> **FORCE DISPOSITION — write on the list before you deploy**
> **Take and Hold** · MFM pattern: `ANVIL SIEGE FORCE - 2DP - TAKE AND HOLD` *(confirm on your Marines MFM)*
> How matching works: [`../../setup/Chapter_Approved_Force_Dispositions.md`](../../setup/Chapter_Approved_Force_Dispositions.md)

This is the **second-best alternate detachment for your owned Blood Ravens shelf** after Gladius (and after **1st Company** if Terminators are the star). Anvil rewards the **gun line**: Devastators, Whirlwind, and Tacticals that are willing to stop moving.

**MFM tag pattern (confirm on your Marines MFM before play):** community builders list it as **2DP · Take and Hold** (retrieved 2026-08-22).

**Confidence:** `draft`. Not yet line-checked against your owned PDFs for this detachment.

**Table habit:** Anvil wants planted guns on terrain **footprints** (`14.01`) — OC is the sum of models **inside** the objective’s terrain area (`14.02`), not “near the marker.” Wound chart: [`../../rules/Wound_Roll_Reference.md`](../../rules/Wound_Roll_Reference.md).

---

## Why this fits *your* box

| Owned unit | Why Anvil cares |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Devastator Squad** (5; optional pad to 10) | Already-[HEAVY] guns get the **big** Anvil reward if they Remain Stationary |
| **Whirlwind** | Indirect artillery that wants a parking spot |
| **Tactical Squads** | Bolters / flamer / missile gain [HEAVY]; sit on objectives and shoot |
| Terminators / Chaplain | Still legal — just not what Anvil is “about” |
| Techmarine | Babysits Devs / Whirlwind while they stay put |

**Not Anvil’s story:** Bike Squad / Attack Bike (Legends, and they want to move), incomplete Primaris rush builds, Deathwatch.

## The detachment rule, in plain terms

**Shield of the Imperium:**

1. Ranged weapons on your Adeptus Astartes models gain the **[HEAVY]** ability.
2. If a weapon **already** had [HEAVY], and the model’s unit **Remained Stationary** this turn, attacks with that weapon get **+1 to the Wound roll**.

Teaching translation:

| Weapon type | If you Remained Stationary |
|-------------|----------------------------|
| Normal bolter / etc. (no Heavy before) | Now counts as Heavy → usually **+1 to Hit** when stationary (Core [HEAVY] behaviour — confirm on your Core / glossary) |
| Devastator lascannon / HB / MM / plasma cannon (already Heavy) | **+1 to Wound** on top of sitting still |

### The Oath trap (read this)

Community writeups (retrieved 2026-08-22) flag that Anvil’s **+1 to Wound** on already-Heavy weapons **does not stack** with Oath of Moment’s +1 to Wound (when that bonus applies). Practical teaching:

- Oath still helps **hits** / target priority.
- For Devastators sitting still into the Oath target, do not assume two separate +1 Wound bonuses — pick the plan that your pack’s stacking rules actually allow.
- **Verify on your Faction Pack / Core** before a serious game.

### When to Remain Stationary

| Sit | Move |
|-----|------|
| Devs and Whirlwind have lanes and the midboard is already contested by Terminators / Tacticals | Opening round — walk onto objectives first, *then* plant |
| Opponent must come to you | You are losing Primary because nobody is on the marker |
| You just used a stratagem that also wants “stationary” | Bikes night / Casual Legends flanking (wrong detachment for that) |

**Default learning plan:** round one move onto markers; from round two, Devastators and Whirlwind try to **never move again** unless the lane dies.

## Stratagems worth knowing on day one

| Stratagem (short name) | CP | Player-facing use |
|------------------------|----|-------------------|
| **Battle Drill Recall** | 1 | Shooting: [SUSTAINED HITS 1]; if you Remained Stationary, Crit Hits on **unmodified 5+** — the Anvil “I am a gunline” button |
| **Hail of Vengeance** | 2 | After an enemy shoots and kills a model in your unit: that unit may shoot back at them (not Overwatch) — pairs with sitting still / Heavy |
| **No Threat Too Great** | 2 | Ranged attacks into Monster/Vehicle: re-roll Wounds |
| **Not One Backwards Step** | 1 | Double OC this turn, but you must Remain Stationary — hold a marker hard |
| Rigid Discipline | 1 | Fall Back restricted / objective-linked (scoring / disentangle — confirm exact limits on pack) |
| **Armour of Contempt** | 1 | Worsen AP vs your unit by 1 |

**Owned-shelf tip:** Battle Drill Recall on stationary Devastators is the first Anvil habit to learn. Hail of Vengeance on a Tactical blob that got shot off a marker is the second.

## Enhancements (draft points)

Public refs (retrieved **2026-08-22**) — re-check MFM before costing.

| Enhancement | Pts (draft) | Notes for your shelf |
|-------------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Architect of War** | ~25 | Ignore Cover on the bearer’s unit — strong on a Captain with Tacticals or near Devs |
| Stoic Defender | ~15 | Unit FNP 6+ / Battle-shock OC softness — durability on a planted squad |
| Fleet Commander | ~15 | Captain; once-per-battle mortal-wound line trick — skip for first Anvil game |
| Indomitable Fury | ~20 | Gravis-only revive — you own a Gravis Captain (40K-only model); optional later |

**Learning pick:** none at 250; at 500+ consider **Architect of War** if you want one clear “guns ignore Cover” lever.

## Gladius vs Anvil (same models)

| | Gladius | Anvil |
|--|---------|-------|
| Skill test | Spend three movement doctrines | When to **stop** moving |
| Rewards | Whole-army flexibility | Stationary shooting |
| Feels wrong when | You never need to Advance | You keep repositioning the gun line every turn |

If the son is still learning Advance / Fall Back / charge timing, stay on Gladius. Switch to Anvil for a “gunline vs Necrons” evening once doctrines are boring.
