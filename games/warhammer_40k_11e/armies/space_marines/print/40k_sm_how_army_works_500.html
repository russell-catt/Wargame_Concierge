How your army works — 500 Gladius (Blood Ravens)

FORCE DISPOSITION: PRIORITY ASSETS
MFM GLADIUS TASK FORCE - 3DP - PRIORITY ASSETS · Page 1 of 4 · Companion to Army List 500 Matched (statted)
One-sentence plan: Park Devastators on a lane, push Captain + Tacticals onto the mid objective, drop Terminators where the Oath target is weakest. You always play your Priority Assets Primary — opponent’s Disposition picks which of the five. Caps: 15VP/round · 45VP/game.

Your four pieces

UnitJobDo thisDon’t
Captain + TacticalsMidboard OC / ActionsClaim an objective and stay. Flamer clears screens; missile pokes. Captain fights what touches you. Main Action body when the Primary asks.Chase the whole board alone
DevastatorsGunlineRemain Stationary for Signum (Ignores Cover). Oath target first. LC/MM for big; HB/plasma for packs.Advance every turn “just because”
TerminatorsHammerStart in reserves (Deep Strike). Arrive turn 2+ near Oath, free objective, or Action spot.Walk on from your edge turn 1 (unless board is tiny)

Oath of Moment (army rule)

When: start of your Command phase — free. What: name one enemy unit. Until your next Command phase (covers their turn too): (1) Re-roll Hit rolls against that unit; (2) +1 to Wound (Codex SM detachment + no BA/DA/DW/SW — your list qualifies).

Legal (sounds weird)

  • Name a unit in a transport
  • Name a unit still in reserves
  • No LOS / range needed to name it

Habit

  • Say the Oath target out loud every Command phase
  • Shoot it first with whoever can see it
  • Splitting fire throws the free buff away

Each once per battle. Skipping a round is fine. Adaptive Strategy (1CP) = one unit into any doctrine (even a used one).

Terminators — reserves toolkit

Page 4 of 4 · Deep Strike changes where they arrive, not when

Three arrival tools (don’t mix the inches)

ToolWhenWhereNotes
Strategic ReservesYour Movement, from battle round 2Usually within 6″ of a battlefield edge, and >8″ from enemiesReserves ≤ 50% list points. Still off board end of round 3 → destroyed
Deep StrikeSame timing windowAnywhere more than 8″ from enemiesWhy Terminators feel scary
Rapid Ingress (1CP Core)End of opponent’s MovementSame setup as normal arrival (Deep Strike → >8″ anywhere)Not in BR1. Arrive before your turn — then shoot/charge on your turn
Distance triad: coherency 2″/9″ · Ingress edge 6″ · Deep Strike / reserves gap >8″. Different jobs — don’t call the 6″ “coherency.”

Default Terminator plan

  1. Declare them in reserves / Deep Strike before the game.
  2. Turn 1: play Captain brick + Devastators; pick Oath.
  3. Turn 2: Deep Strike onto Oath / free objective / Action spot — or Rapid Ingress if they overextended.
  4. After landing: shoot (Fury of the First vs Oath), then charge if the math is good. Chainfists for vehicles.

Your turn checklist

  1. Command — +CP; name Oath; optional Doctrine
  2. Movement — every unit; bring Terms in; start Actions if Primary needs them
  3. Shooting — Oath first; Devs prefer Stay Still
  4. Charge — roll 2D6 first
  5. Fight — pile in → fight → consolidate
  6. End — score your Primary VP

First three games — success

  • Named an Oath target every turn
  • Devastators sat still ≥2 times for Signum
  • Terminators arrived turn 2 (or Rapid Ingress) without dying to “set up at 7″”
  • Captain + Tacticals spent more time on an objective / finishing an Action than chasing models
  • Knew which of the five Primaries before turn 1
