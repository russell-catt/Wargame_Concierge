Assault sequence

  1. Land where return fire cannot erase them.
  2. Next Command: Oath intended target.
  3. Move toward target / objective.
  4. Charge only if the ending position helps.
  5. Claws into bodies; hammer into hard target.
  6. Consolidate onto scoring / cover.

Turn-one fire

  1. Bless Whirlwind.
  2. Keep Devs still if lane is good.
  3. Move shooting Terms only as needed.
  4. Focus reachable Oath target.
  5. Use Whirlwind on hidden / damaged Infantry.

Whirlwind: “spotters” and indirect fire

No dedicated spotter rule or action exists. A “spotter” is simply any friendly unit that can currently see the target. It does not give up shooting, charging, or an Action to spot.

ShotHit floorWhat applies
Whirlwind itself sees target; use Normal ShootingNormal BS (usually 3+; Tech blessing can improve)No indirect penalties; Oath Hit re-roll works
Target hidden; Whirlwind Remained Stationary; any friendly sees targetUnmodified 4+Target gets Cover; no Hit re-rolls; Oath +1 Wound still works
Target hidden and Whirlwind moved, or nobody friendly sees targetUnmodified 6 onlyUsually shoot a different target unless this one must die

Best spotters: Captain + Tacticals and shooting Terminators naturally see midboard targets. Devastators can spot down their lane. The Assault brick can spot after it arrives. Check visibility when the Whirlwind shoots.

Techmarine: keep him independent within 3″; Blessing repairs the Whirlwind and adds 1 to Hit. That bonus cannot override the indirect unmodified 4+/6 floor, but is excellent for a visible target fired at normally. Hide both from direct anti-tank and screen enemy Deep Strike.

Battle-shock + weapon keyword card

Battle-shock does not normally stop basic movement, shooting, charging or fighting

Every failed test does four things: OC becomes 0 · your stratagems cannot target that unit · it cannot start or complete Actions · Falling Back becomes Desperate Escape and risks casualties. Abilities still work unless they say otherwise.

Megabattle — joint war plan with the Necrons + table etiquette

Team game: your 1000 Blood Ravens + Dad's 945 Necrons on one side · Source: megabattle prep notes (2026-09-25), rules-checked against this pack · draft

The team plan in one line: a shared backline firebase (your Whirlwind + Techmarine behind Dad's Doomstalker and Warrior Brick B), a shared midboard push (your Captain + Tacticals + Devastators beside his Brick A + Immortals), and a pocket hammer (his Scarabs set the trap; your Chaplain + Assault Terminators spring it). His bricks give you a wall that regrows; your Marines give his bricks the killing power they lack.

Rules checks on the prep notes: Rapid Ingress cannot be used in battle round 1. Blessing follows the current Techmarine datasheet wording. The Chaplain's modeled lightning claw counts as a relic shield — say so before any model touches the table. Two Necron Warrior units of 10, not one of 20.
