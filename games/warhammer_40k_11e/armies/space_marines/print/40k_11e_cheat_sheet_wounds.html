Movement types [09]

Remain StationaryNo move; keeps [HEAVY]
NormalUp to M″; start & end unengaged

Reserves / IngressArrive from off-board

Engagement: 2″ horiz / 5″ vert. Coherency [03.03]: 2″ to a squadmate + 9″ to every model in the unit.

Key distances

2″Engagement (horiz.); coherency to a squadmate
6″Ingress: set up within 6″ of a battlefield edge [20.04]
8″Reserves / Deep Strike: more than 8″ from all enemies
9″Coherency span: every model within 9″ of all others [03.03]

Do not mix 6″ edge ingress with 9″ coherency.

Attack sequence [05]

Hit [05.01]D6 ≥ BS/WS. Unmod 1 fails. Unmod 6 = critical hit.
Wound [05.02]S vs T (bands / matrix). Unmod 1 fails. Unmod 6 = critical wound.
Save [05.03]Armour (AP applies) or invulnerable (no AP). Unmod 1 fails.
Damage [05.04]Unsaved attacks cost D wounds. Excess does not spill.

Wound bands [05.02]

CompareNeed
S ≥ 2×T2+
S > T (not 2×)3+
S = T4+
S < T but > ½T5+
S ≤ ½T6+

Examples: bolter S4 vs T4 → 4+. Melta S9 vs T6 → 3+. S4 vs T7 → 5+.

Charge / Fight (pocket)

  • Charge: roll 2D6 first — roll caps targets and distance.
  • Fight: Pile In 3″ → Fight (Fights First first) → Consolidate 3″.
  • Both players fight; alternate activations.
Cover in 11e: Benefit of Cover worsens attacker BS by 1 — it is not a +1 save. Battle-shocked units have OC 0.

Reserves pocket

  • Strategic Reserves: arrive from BR2; usually 6″ of edge + >8″ enemies; ≤50% points; gone if still off end of BR3.
  • Deep Strike: anywhere >8″ from enemies.
  • Rapid Ingress (1CP): end of their Movement; not BR1.

OC pocket [14]

  • Sum OC on the objective footprint.
  • Higher controls; tie = nobody.
  • Battle-shocked = OC 0.
