| Weapon | Range | A | WS | S | AP | D | Keywords |
|--------|-------|---|----|---|----|---|----------|
| Thunder hammer | Melee | 3 | 3+ | 8 | -2 | 2 | `[DEVASTATING WOUNDS]` |
| Twin lightning claws | Melee | 6 | 3+ | 5 | -2 | 1 | `[TWIN-LINKED]` |

**Terminatus Assault:** at the start of the Fight phase, enemy units engaged with this unit take Battle-shock tests.  
**Teleport Homer:** before battle, optionally place its token outside the enemy deployment zone. Once/battle, Rapid Ingress this unit for 0CP near the token and still more than 8" from enemies. If the token lane looks bad, do not place it; use normal Deep Strike / paid Rapid Ingress instead.

### Melee math reminders

| Attacker | Base wound help | Best target |
|----------|-----------------|-------------|
| 4 claw models | 24 attacks; `[TWIN-LINKED]` re-rolls Wounds | Infantry and medium bodies |
| 1 hammer model | 3 attacks; critical wounds become mortal damage | Tough targets / armoured elites |
| Chaplain | 5 attacks at WS2+ | Adds reliable damage |
| Whole attached unit | Chaplain gives **+1 Wound** in melee | Concentrate into the Oath target when possible |

| Forge bolter | 24" | 3 | 2+ | 5 | -1 | 2 | — |

| Omnissian power axe | Melee | 4 | 3+ | 6 | -2 | 2 | — |

**Techmarine:** while independent and within 3" of an Astartes Vehicle, gains Lone Operative.

**Vengeance:** if a friendly Astartes Vehicle is destroyed within 12", the axe becomes 7 attacks for the rest of the battle.

| Storm bolter | 24" | 2 | 3+ | 4 | 0 | 1 | `[RAPID FIRE 2]` |
| Armoured tracks | Melee | 3 | 4+ | 6 | 0 | 1 | — |

**Pinning Bombardment:** after shooting, if the launcher scored a hit on enemy Infantry, that unit takes a Battle-shock test.  
**Techmarine order:** Blessing makes the launcher effectively hit one step better; use the Whirlwind to pressure hidden scoring Infantry, not as your only anti-tank plan.

## Turn-one firing order

1. Command: gain CP; name the Oath target; Techmarine blesses Whirlwind; decide doctrine.
2. Keep Devastators still if their lane is good.
3. Shooting Terminators move only as much as needed to see / reach a useful target.
4. Fire Oath-efficient direct guns first; use the Whirlwind on hidden Infantry or a damaged scoring unit.
5. Keep Chaplain + Assault Terminators off-board until turn 2 or 3.
