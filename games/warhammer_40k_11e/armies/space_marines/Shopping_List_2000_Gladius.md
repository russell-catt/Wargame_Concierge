# Shopping list — ~2,000 pts — Gladius Task Force (Blood Ravens)

> **Baseline today:** [`Starter_1000_Matched.md`](Starter_1000_Matched.md) / [`Son_Play_Pack_1000.md`](Son_Play_Pack_1000.md) ≈ **1,000 / 1,000** owned Matched.  
> **Target:** ~**1,000 more** under Gladius + Oath of Moment.  
> **FORCE DISPOSITION:** **Priority Assets** · `GLADIUS TASK FORCE - 3DP - PRIORITY ASSETS`

**Codex honesty:** WarCom preview (Oct) — **Tactical / Devastator** → future Legendary Proxies; **Whirlwind** → future Legends. Shop **Primaris** for the next 1k so the 2k list ages better. Deathwatch stays a separate identity — do not mix into Gladius shopping.

## Bench first (spend $0)

Paint / finish before shopping hard:

1. Indomitus **Assault Intercessors (10)**  
2. **Bladeguard Veterans (3) + Ancient**  
3. **Outriders (3)**  
4. **Judiciar** + Primaris **Chaplain**  
5. Reattach Tac2 fallen body; optional Devastator pad to 10 from Tac bolters  

Those alone can eat a large chunk of the path to 2k once costed.
