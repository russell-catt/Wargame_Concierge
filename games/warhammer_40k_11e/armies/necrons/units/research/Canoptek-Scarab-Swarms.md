## Abilities (teaching paraphrase)

**Faction — Reanimation Protocols:** as army rule.

**Self-destruction** *(confirmed on Wahapedia 11e, 2026-09-24):*
At the **start of the Fight phase**, if this unit is within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, you **may** pick **one** Scarab model in the unit and destroy it. Then pick one enemy unit in Engagement Range of that model and roll a D6 (add **+1** if that enemy is a **VEHICLE**). On a **2–5**, that enemy takes **D3 mortal wounds**; on a **6+**, it takes **3 mortal wounds**.

Table notes:
- **One model per Scarab unit per Fight phase** — you cannot chain-detonate the whole unit in the same phase.
- Happens at phase start (before Fight activations). It is **not** a Fight activation.
- Destroying your own model for this ability is voluntary; the remaining bases stay in the fight and can still Fight normally later in the phase.

**Chittering swarm:**
- While an enemy unit is within Engagement Range of this unit, that enemy’s models have **−1 OC** (to a minimum of 1).
- While this unit is within **6"** of one or more friendly **CRYPTEK** models, each Scarab model’s OC is **1** (instead of 0).

## How to use them (beginner)

1. **Screen / Action chips** — fast FLY bodies that die cheaply.
2. **Near a Cryptek** — park within 6" so they actually contest objectives (OC 1).
3. **Into melee they will not win** — charge a vehicle or tough brick, then **Self-destruction** one base at Fight start for mortal wounds; leave the rest as tar-pit / OC drain via Chittering swarm.
4. **Court path** — cheap Canoptek Matrix flippers; dying after flipping a region is still a win.
5. **Conclave path** — no gun menu; use for Actions, screens, and the new detonation trick.

## At the table (plain language)
- **Reanimation:** End of your **Command phase** (`08.05`) — roll D3 wounds; heal first, then return models at 1W. Wiped units stay dead. See `Reanimation_Protocols.md`.
- **Objectives:** Default OC 0 — count as “almost nothing” for holding flags unless a Cryptek is within 6" (then OC 1 each). Enemy units locked with you also lose 1 OC per model (min 1).
- **Fight start:** Decide whether to blow **one** base for mortal wounds before anyone Fights.
