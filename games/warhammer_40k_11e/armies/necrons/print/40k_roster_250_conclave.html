How to play (plain language)

  1. Before the game: Attach Geomancer to Warriors (19.01). Write it on your list — cannot change mid-battle.
  2. Every your turn: End of Command (08.05) — roll D3 reanimation for each unit. Token on the table.
  3. Warriors: Hold an objective (14.02). In Shooting (10.02), say Conclave pick out loud — [IGNORES COVER] (13.08) is the usual beginner pick in terrain.
  4. Crawlers: Walk to the middle. Shoot without the menu; still reanimate.
  5. Scarabs: Run wide and annoy.
