How your army works — 500 Cryptek Conclave

FORCE DISPOSITION: PRIORITY ASSETS
MFM CRYPTEK CONCLAVE - 2DP - PRIORITY ASSETS · Page 1 of 5 · App backup · Companion to Army List 500 V1
One-sentence plan: Two Cryptek shooting bricks hold Expansion/Central, Scarabs Action and screen, refuse to die — Reanimation every Command + Potentiality Syphon off-turn. Caps: 15VP/round · 45VP/game.

Your pieces

UnitJobDo thisDon’t

Immortals + Techno/PlasmancerGun brickSecond Conclave menu pick; hold/support WarriorsLeave Cryptek unattached
Scarabs (6)Screen / ActionsAdvance wide, start Actions, die cheaplyExpect Conclave gun buffs

Attachments: Warriors ← Warden (Leader) + Geomancer (Support). Immortals ← Technomancer or Plasmancer proxy. Crypteks are Support, not Leader.

Reanimation Protocols

When: end of your Command — free. Every eligible unit rolls D3. Heal survivors first → return models at 1W. Wiped = no RP.

Extra on this listEffect
Their Number is Legion (Warriors)Re-roll the D3 whenever this unit’s RP activates (incl. Syphon)
Potentiality Syphon (1CP)Their Command: unit on objective activates RP; +1 wound if Cryptek unit

Technosorcerous Augmentations

Cryptek guns get [ASSAULT]. Each Cryptek unit that shoots picks one: [ANTI-INFANTRY 3+] · [IGNORES COVER] · [ASSAULT] · [HEAVY] · [ANTI-MOUNTED 4+]. Two bricks = two picks. Say them out loud. Atomic Disintegrators not on this budget list.

Your turn checklist

  1. Command — +CP; Battle-shock; REANIMATE every unit (Warriors re-roll D3)
  2. Movement — bricks usually Stay Still on flags; Scarabs Advance/Action; Geomancer pin
  3. Shooting — announce menu pick per Cryptek brick
  4. Charge/Fight — rare; don’t gift Kill More
  5. End — score your Primary
  6. Their Command — consider Potentiality Syphon
