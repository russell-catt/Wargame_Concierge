Battle Formations (declare before deploy): Warriors A ← Royal Warden (Leader) + Geomancer (Support) · Warriors B ← Technomancer (Support) · Immortals ← Plasmancer (Support). Crypteks are Support, not Leader; one Leader + one Support per bodyguard. Doomstalker, Scarabs, Crawlers, Macrocytes run independently and get no Conclave menu. Optional: Tomb Crawlers’ Canoptek Retinue may join a Cryptek-led unit — check the datasheet wording.
Why two Warrior 10s, not one 20: three Cryptek Supports need three bodyguards. Two Warrior units + Immortals = three legal seats, three menu picks, three Battle-shock tests at the character’s Ld 6+. A 20-brick leaves one Cryptek with nowhere to attach. If owned MFM says Doomstalker 145: add 5 to every total (980 / 965 / 940).

Turn-one firing order (one glance)

  1. Doomstalker (stationary, 3+): biggest VEHICLE / MONSTER in the 48″ lane; else the densest Infantry for Blast.
  2. Immortals + Plasmancer: Living Lightning first (free mortals), then 20 shots with [ANTI-INFANTRY 3+] or Atomic anti-Vehicle.
  3. Warriors A: 30″ / 18″ Lethal Hits volley with [IGNORES COVER]; Geomancer beam at anything T8+ within 12″ for Melta.
  4. Warriors B: [HEAVY] from home at whatever wandered into 24″.
  5. Macrocytes / Crawlers: chip shots only; their job is position.
