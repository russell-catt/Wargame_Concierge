Battle round frame

A battle round (07) is: start-of-round rules, then both players take a turn, then end-of-round rules. Your turn: Start of Turn → five phases → End of Turn.

Visibility (06.01): Targets must normally be visible, in range, and unengaged. Enemy Monsters and Vehicles are the exception — you can shoot those while they are engaged, at −1 to hit. Terrain may block or obscure LOS — see setup/terrain aid.

Charge & Fight (short)

  • Charge (11.02): within 12", not engaged, did not Advance/Fall Back; roll 2D6; must end engaged with all nominated targets or the charge fails.
  • Fight: pile in 3" (12.02) → fight (12.04, Fights First first) → consolidate 3" (12.07). Objective consolidation is free scoring movement.

Attack sequence (hit → wound → save → damage)

  1. Hit (05.01) — D6 vs BS (shoot) or WS (melee). Unmodified 1 always fails; unmodified 6 is a critical hit.
  2. Wound (05.02) — D6, Strength vs Toughness. Unmodified 1 fails; unmodified 6 is a critical wound.
  3. Save (05.03) — Defender rolls: armour save modified by AP, or invulnerable save (ignores AP). Unmodified 1 fails.
  4. Damage (05.04) — Each unsaved attack costs D wounds. Excess damage does not spill to the next model.

Allocation: Defender chooses order; already-wounded group first; characters cannot be put in front of ordinary models.

Objective Control (OC) vs Battle-shock

OC — how you win (14.02)

  1. At the end of every phase and turn, add OC of models in range of the objective.
  2. Higher total controls it. Tie = nobody.
  • Control flips constantly — re-checked every phase.
  • Bodies beat quality: ten OC 1 out-hold one OC 3 tank.
  • A battle-shocked unit has no OC.

Battle-shock (08.03, 01.07)

When: Command phase — units already battle-shocked or at/below half-strength. Roll 2D6 vs Leadership.

While battle-shocked: OC = nothing · cannot be targeted by your stratagems · cannot start/complete actions · Fall Back uses Desperate Escape.
