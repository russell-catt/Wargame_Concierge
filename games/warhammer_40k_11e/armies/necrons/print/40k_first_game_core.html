Your turn, in order

Battle round (07): both players take a turn. Your turn = Start → five phases → End.

Shooting / Charge / Fight

Shoot: Normal | Assault (advanced) | Close-quarters (engaged) | Indirect (unseen; gives cover 13.08). Visibility (06.01): usually visible + in range + unengaged; Monsters/Vehicles while engaged at −1 to hit.

Charge (11.02): within 12″, not engaged, no Advance/Fall Back; 2D6; must end engaged with nominated targets. Fight: pile in 3″ → fight (Fights First first) → consolidate 3″.

Attack sequence + S vs T

  1. Hit (05.01) — vs BS/WS; unmodified 1 fails; 6 = critical hit.
  2. Wound (05.02) — S vs T: S≥2×T → 2+ · S>T → 3+ · S=T → 4+ · S<T → 5+ · S≤½T → 6+. Full grid: 40k_wound_roll_reference.
  3. Save (05.03) — armour (−AP) or invuln. Damage (05.04) — excess does not spill.
  4. Allocation: defender order; already-wounded first; characters cannot be put in front.

Cover (13.08): worsens attacker BS by 1 — not a save bonus.

OC, attach, battle-shock

14.01 / 14.02: Objective = terrain footprint; sum OC inside each phase/turn; higher controls; tie = nobody; score when mission card says. Battle-shocked = 0 OC.

19.01 / 03.03: one Leader + one Support per bodyguard → one unit; deploy as one 2″/9″ coherency blob. Cannot attach mid-game. Ingress 6″ edge / >8″ enemies is a different job.

Battle-shock (08.03): Command phase — already shocked or ≤ half strength; 2D6 vs Ld. While shocked: no OC · no your stratagems · no actions.

Force Disposition / Mission 38: Write Disposition on the list at Muster. Matching sets each Primary (Chapter Approved Force Dispositions 2-pager). Mission 38 Converging Ambition (40k_wd527_mission) replaces Determine Mission / Layout when in use; Disposition still picks your Primary block.
