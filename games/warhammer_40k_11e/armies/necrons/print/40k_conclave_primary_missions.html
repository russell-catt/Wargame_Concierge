1. Secure Asset — vs Take and Hold

Action Secure Asset on non-home · kill on Central · BR2+ hold non-home / hold 3+. Opponent: hold 3+ / Hold More / steal your home late.

Keys: Scarabs Action; Warriors hold; never gift Hold More; defend Expansion.

2. Vital Link — vs Purge the Foe

Stack Operation Markers on Central via Action · BR2+ hold non-home (bonus Central) · end-game their home. Opponent: kills / Kill More + holds.

Keys: Warriors tarpit Central and Action every turn. Don’t feed Scarab suicides.

3. Sabotage — vs Priority Assets (mirror)

Sabotage Action per non-home (bonus in enemy territory; Central counts) · BR2+ hold non-home. Mirror scoring.

Keys: Max Action volume; stop opponent turn-1 mega score; quantity of cheap units.

4. Extract Relic — vs Disruption

Opponent places Operation Markers on terrain outside their DZ. Sensor Sweep on Central removes a marker (you score for Sweeps). Also: kill on-objective units · final-marker control · hold non-home. Opponent Sweeps too (denies you) and contests final marker.

Keys: Be the Sweeper early on their-half markers; lock last marker on your Expansion terrain; block their Central Sweep.

5. Vanguard Operation — vs Reconnaissance

Once/turn Action in terrain in enemy territory (Central counts) if cleared · kill 1+ · BR2+ hold non-home · end-game their home. Opponent: Centrals · kills your terrain units · multi non-home · deny your presence in their half late.

Keys: Action without donating free kills; hold Expansion; end with a model in their half when you can.
