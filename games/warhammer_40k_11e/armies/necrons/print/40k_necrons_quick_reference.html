Army rule — Reanimation Protocols

End of YOUR Command phase (08.05). Every unit on the battlefield. Roll D3 wounds. Free. (Necron army rule — faction pack.)
  1. Heal wounded survivors first.
  2. Then return destroyed models at 1 wound.
  3. At Starting Strength / full wounds: stop. Wiped unit = never returns.

Consequence: keep the last model alive; stand on the footprint; chip damage is often wasted.

Detachment snapshot

Court — Power Matrix: re-check start of every phase (07.02). Your DZ always; NML / enemy DZ when you control ≥ half their objectives (14.02). Cryptek/Canoptek: re-roll 1s anywhere; re-roll hits wholly within Matrix.

Conclave — Technosorcerous Augmentations: each Cryptek unit shooting picks one: [ANTI-INFANTRY 3+] [ANTI-MOUNTED 4+] [ASSAULT] [HEAVY] [IGNORES COVER]. Attach Cryptek (19.01) so the brick counts.

Combat sequence + S vs T

  1. Hit (05.01) — vs BS/WS; unmodified 1 fails; 6 = critical hit.
  2. Wound (05.02) — S vs T: S≥2×T → 2+ · S>T → 3+ · S=T → 4+ · S<T → 5+ · S≤½T → 6+. Full grid: 40k_wound_roll_reference.
  3. Save (05.03) — armour (−AP) or invuln. Damage (05.04) — excess does not spill.
  4. Cover (13.08) worsens attacker BS by 1 — not a save bonus.

WD527 habit: one Leader + one Support per bodyguard (Geomancer = Support). Coherency is 2″/9″ — not the Ingress 6″ edge.
