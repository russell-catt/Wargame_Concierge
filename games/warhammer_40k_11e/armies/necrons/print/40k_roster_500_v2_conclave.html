How to play (one glance)

  1. Macrocytes Scout 8″ before turn 1 — park the −1-to-Hit aura over whichever brick gets shot first; keep the nanoscarab model within 3″ of a reanimating unit.
  2. Warriors + Geomancer hold home/near objective — 20 OC. Reanimate end of Command, re-roll the D3. Default [IGNORES COVER]; 30″/18″ guns with the Gauntlet.
  3. Immortals + Technomancer take midfield — T5 / 3+ / FNP 5+. Menu [ANTI-INFANTRY 3+], or Atomic Disintegrators anti-Vehicle / anti-Monster vs armour.
  4. Tomb Crawlers screen the Immortals or hold a flank — inside 12″ they ignore cover and hit modifiers. S6 claws beat chaff.
  5. Scarabs run wide: Actions and charge-blocking only. OC0.
  6. Potentiality Syphon (1CP) on the opponent's Command phase when a Cryptek brick is on an objective — extra reanimation every round.
  7. Two menu picks to declare out loud every Shooting phase. Forgetting them is the #1 way to under-play this list.
