**Points arithmetic:** 75 + 80 + 60 + 50 + 85 + 85 + 140 + 80 + 50 + 70 + 140 = **915** body · + 20 + 10 = **945**.

**Why two Warrior 10s (not one 20):** you own **three** Cryptek Supports (Geomancer, Technomancer, Plasmancer proxy) plus a Warden. Each Support needs a bodyguard. Two Warrior squads + Immortals = three legal seats — three menu picks, three Battle-shock tests at the character's Ld 6+, three Syphon targets. A single 20-Warrior brick can only take Warden + one Cryptek, which leaves a Cryptek with nowhere legal to attach.

### Enhancements — locked (app allows two)

| Enhancement | Bearer | Pts |
|-------------|--------|-----|
| **Gauntlet of Compression** | Geomancer (Warriors A) | 20 |
| **Atomic Disintegrators** | Plasmancer (Immortals) | 10 |

Gravitic Bolas and Quantum Abacus are off the list. The Technomancer and the Royal Warden carry no enhancement.

**If owned MFM lists Doomstalker at 145:** body **920** · list **950**. The official app currently totals **945** at 140.

## Attachments (declare before deploy)

| Bodyguard | Leader | Support |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Necron Warriors (10) brick A | Royal Warden (Despotek proxy) | Cryptek Geomancer *(Warlord)* |
| Necron Warriors (10) brick B | — | Technomancer |
| Immortals (10) | — | Plasmancer (Apprentek proxy) |

- **Doomstalker, Macrocytes, Tomb Crawlers, Scarabs:** no Cryptek attached — they do **not** get the Conclave shooting menu. Synergistic Empowerment (1CP) can lend CRYPTEK to Crawlers or Macrocytes for one phase; never to the Doomstalker (Vehicle).
- **Immortals loadout:** field as one unit of 10 — pick **gauss blasters** or **tesla carbines** for the whole unit if bodies allow (Guardians + 1st box are gauss; 2nd box is tesla). If you must mix to reach 10, agree it with your opponent and track which model carries which.
- **Swap option:** Technomancer ↔ Plasmancer proxy between brick B and Immortals if you want FNP 5+ on Immortals instead — but then the Technomancer's D3 heal leaves the Doomstalker's neighbourhood.

### Who gets the Conclave shooting menu

## How this list plays (plain language)

**One-sentence plan:** Three Conclave gun bricks (two Warrior 10s + Immortals) hold Expansion / Central, Scarabs Action and screen, Macrocytes/Crawlers contest, and the **Doomstalker** deletes vehicles from your backline.

1. **Warriors A + Warden + Geomancer** — walk to Expansion; every Shooting phase say your Conclave menu pick out loud (`[IGNORES COVER]` is the beginner default in terrain). 30" / 18" guns with the Gauntlet.
2. **Warriors B + Technomancer** — home brick next to the Doomstalker; `[HEAVY]` because it never moves; Technomancer heals the walker D3 at end of Movement.
3. **Immortals + Plasmancer proxy** — Central; Living Lightning first, then 20 shots with `[ANTI-INFANTRY 3+]` or Atomic anti-Vehicle.
4. **Doomstalker** — Remain Stationary (Heavy → 3+). Shoot the scariest VEHICLE / MONSTER; Blast into 10-model squads. Overwatch hits on **5+** (Sentinel Construct) — budget 1CP when Terminators are near your backfield.
5. **Scarabs** — Actions / screens; OC 1 near a Cryptek; −1 OC to whatever they touch; optional **Self-destruction** one base at Fight start.
6. **Macrocytes** — Scout 8" before turn 1; park the −1-to-Hit aura on the brick that gets shot first. **Tomb Crawlers** — screen the Doomstalker against Deep Strike.

**Reanimation:** every unit at end of your Command phase (`08.05`). **Potentiality Syphon (1CP)** when a Cryptek brick sits on an objective in the opponent's Command phase.

### Turn-one firing order

1. **Doomstalker** (stationary, 3+): biggest VEHICLE / MONSTER in the 48" lane; else the densest Infantry for Blast.
2. **Immortals + Plasmancer**: Living Lightning, then the volley with `[ANTI-INFANTRY 3+]` or Atomic anti-Vehicle.
3. **Warriors A**: 30" / 18" Lethal Hits volley with `[IGNORES COVER]`; Geomancer beam at anything T8+ within 12" for Melta.
4. **Warriors B**: `[HEAVY]` from home at whatever wandered into 24".
5. **Macrocytes / Crawlers**: chip shots only; their job is position.
