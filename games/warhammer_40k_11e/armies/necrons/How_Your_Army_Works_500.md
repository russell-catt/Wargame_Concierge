# How your army works — 500 Cryptek Conclave | Page 1 of 5

> **FORCE DISPOSITION: PRIORITY ASSETS**  
> MFM: `CRYPTEK CONCLAVE - 2DP - PRIORITY ASSETS`  
> List: [`Army_List_500_V1_Conclave.md`](Army_List_500_V1_Conclave.md) · Math companion: [`Reference_Guide_500_V1_Conclave.md`](Reference_Guide_500_V1_Conclave.md) · Matching: [`../../setup/Chapter_Approved_Force_Dispositions.md`](../../setup/Chapter_Approved_Force_Dispositions.md)

**One-sentence plan:** Two Cryptek shooting bricks hold Expansion / Central, Scarabs Action and screen, and you refuse to die — Reanimation every Command, plus **Potentiality Syphon** off-turn when a brick sits on a flag.

You always play **your** Priority Assets Primary. Opponent’s Disposition picks **which** of the five below. Caps: **15VP**/round · **45VP**/game.

---

## Your pieces (500 V1)

| Unit | Job | Do this | Don’t do this |
|------|-----|---------|---------------|
| **Warriors + Warden + Geomancer** | Home / Expansion **OC** brick | Park on a non-home flag. Reanimate. Default shoot pick **[IGNORES COVER]**. Geomancer pins a threat | Chase the whole board; wipe yourself by overextending |
| **Immortals + Techno or Plasmancer proxy** | Midboard gun brick | Same Conclave menu rules; stronger guns. Hold / support Warriors | Leave the Cryptek unattached |
| **Scarabs (6)** | Screen / Action / kamikaze chip | Advance wide, start Actions, stay near a Cryptek for OC 1; optional **Self-destruction** (one base) at Fight start | Expect Conclave gun buffs — they don’t get them; do not chain-detonate the whole unit in one Fight phase |
| **Spare Cryptek note** | Support slot | Crypteks are **Support**, not Leader — Warden is the Leader on Warriors | Forget Battle Formations before deploy |

**Attachments (declare before deploy):** Warriors ← Warden (Leader) + Geomancer (Support). Immortals ← Technomancer **or** Plasmancer proxy.

---

## Reanimation Protocols (army rule)

**When:** End of **your** Command phase — free. Every eligible Necron unit still on the board rolls **D3** wounds returned.  
**Order:** heal wounded survivors first → then return destroyed models at **1W**. **Wiped unit = no reanimation.**

| On this list | Extra |
|--------------|-------|
| **Warriors — Their Number is Legion** | Re-roll the D3 whenever this unit’s RP activates (including Syphon) |
| **Potentiality Syphon** (1CP Conclave) | Opponent’s Command: one unit on an objective activates RP; **+1 wound** if it’s a Cryptek unit |

Full math: [`Reference_Guide_500_V1_Conclave.md`](Reference_Guide_500_V1_Conclave.md) · [`Reanimation_Protocols.md`](Reanimation_Protocols.md).

---

## Technosorcerous Augmentations (detachment)

1. Cryptek models’ guns gain **[ASSAULT]** (shoot after Advance).  
2. Each time a **Cryptek unit** shoots in your Shooting phase, pick **one** menu ability for **all** ranged weapons in that unit until end of phase:

| Pick | When |
|------|------|
| **[ANTI-INFANTRY 3+]** | Infantry in the open |
| **[IGNORES COVER]** | Default in terrain |
| **[ASSAULT]** | You Advanced and still need the volley |
| **[HEAVY]** | You Remained Stationary and want +hit |
| **[ANTI-MOUNTED 4+]** | Mounted keyword targets |

Two bricks = **two picks** per Shooting phase (can differ). Say each pick out loud. Atomic Disintegrators **not** on this budget list.

## The five Primaries (you = Priority Assets)

**confidence: draft.** Teaching shape — same Priority Assets pairings as [`Cryptek_Conclave_Primary_Missions.md`](Cryptek_Conclave_Primary_Missions.md). **Your Chapter Approved card wins.**

### 1. Secure Asset — vs **Take and Hold**

| Your scoring shape | Keys for this 500 Conclave |
|--------------------|----------------------------|
| EOT: **Action** Secure Asset on a non-home | Scarabs Action; Warriors hold |
| EOT: bonus destroy a unit on **Central** | Immortals / Geomancer guns |
| BR2+ Command: hold non-home; bonus hold **3+** | Expansion + home + one more |
| Opponent: hold 3+ / Hold More / steal your home late | Stop Hold More; guard home |

**Success:** Steady Actions + never gift Hold More.

### 2. Vital Link — vs **Purge the Foe**

| Your scoring shape | Keys for this 500 Conclave |
|--------------------|----------------------------|
| EOT: hold Central + Operation Markers via Action | **Warriors on Central** Action repeatedly — tarpit mission |
| BR2+ Command: hold non-home; bonus if Central | Prefer Central for big ticks |
| End game: opponent’s home | Optional hail Mary — not plan A |
| Opponent: Kill More + Hold More | Do **not** feed free Scarab suicides |

**Success:** One durable brick Actions Central every turn. Reanimation makes the tarpit honest.

### 3. Sabotage — vs **Priority Assets** (mirror)

| Your scoring shape | Keys for this 500 Conclave |
|--------------------|----------------------------|
| EOT: **Sabotage Action** on each non-home (bonus enemy territory; Central counts) | Scarabs are gold — max Action pieces |
| BR2+ Command: hold 1+ non-home | Keep Expansion locked while Actions run |
| Opponent: identical | Deny turn-1 mega scores |

**Success:** Early Action volume wins. Quantity of cheap units > one elite blob.

### 4. Extract Relic — vs **Disruption**

| Your scoring shape | Keys for this 500 Conclave |
|--------------------|----------------------------|
| Disruption places Operation Markers on terrain outside their DZ | Know markers before round 1 |
| Once/turn **Sensor Sweep** Action on a Central you control | Be the one Sweeping — Warriors / Scarabs on Central |
| EOT: Sweep · kill on-objective unit · final-marker bonus | Immortals for kills when safe |
| BR2+ Command: hold non-home | Expansion still matters |
| Opponent also Sweeps | Engagement-block Centrals after enough Sweeps |

**Success:** Trade Sweeps early in **their** half; lock the last marker on **your** Expansion.

### 5. Vanguard Operation — vs **Reconnaissance**

| Your scoring shape | Keys for this 500 Conclave |
|--------------------|----------------------------|
| Once/turn **Vanguard Operation** Action in enemy-territory terrain | Send Scarabs / tough Action unit into enemy-half terrain |
| EOT: kill 1+ enemy units | Pick off chips; don’t donate the Action unit |
| BR2+ Command: hold 1+ non-home | Warriors on Expansion |
| End game: control their home | Late push only if Actions banked |

**Success:** Action in enemy territory without feeding terrain-kill points.

## CP spends (stratagems / “ploys”) — paper backup

40K **Stratagems** = app “ploys.” Both players **+1 CP** each Command phase.  
**Rules:** same stratagem once/phase · usually one stratagem per unit per phase · battle-shocked units cannot be targeted by your stratagems.  
**confidence: draft** — Conclave names from community/Wahapedia shape (retrieved **2026-09-19**); **owned Faction Pack wins** on wording. Core from project glossary.

### On Necron turn

| Spend | CP | When | What it does (short) | Tip |
|-------|----|------|----------------------|-----|
| **Molecular Targeting** *(Conclave)* | 1 | Your Shooting **or** Fight | Ignore Hit / BS / WS modifiers; if **CRYPTEK** unit, ignore Wound modifiers too | When the brick is -1 to Hit in cover fights |
| **Untapped Power** *(Conclave)* | 1 | Your Shooting (before that Cryptek unit shoots) | That Cryptek unit picks **two** menu abilities this phase | Assault + Ignores Cover on the move-and-shoot turn |
| **Synergistic Empowerment** *(Conclave)* | 1 | Start of your Shooting | One nearby Necron model (not Monster/Vehicle) gains **CRYPTEK** until end of phase | Rare on this list — no Tomb Blades / Praetorians yet |
| **Explosives** *(Core)* | 1 | Your Shooting | Grenade / explosives profile if the datasheet has it | Skip if sheet has none |
| **Epic Challenge** *(Core)* | 1 | Your Fight | Character melee gains **[PRECISION]** | Rare — Conclave is a gun army |
| **Insane Bravery** *(Core)* | 1 | Your Command | Auto-pass one Battle-shock (**once/battle**) | Keep OC on the flag |

### On opponent turn

| Spend | CP | When | What it does (short) | Tip |
|-------|----|------|----------------------|-----|
| **Potentiality Syphon** *(Conclave)* | 1 | **Their Command** | One Necron unit on an objective activates **RP**; **+1 wound** if Cryptek unit | Default off-turn spend — Warriors brick |
| **Microscarab Swarm** *(Conclave)* | 1 | Your unit is targeted by attacks | Warriors get a soft invuln; Immortals a stronger one (verify pack: often 5+ / 4+) | When they try to wipe a brick in one activation |
| **Animus Curse** *(Conclave)* | 1 | After a Cryptek is destroyed (verify trigger) | Punish the killer (verify pack effect) | Insurance when they snipe the Geomancer |
| **Fire Overwatch** *(Core)* | 1 | Enemy moves / Advances / Falls Back / Charges in range | Your unit shoots (snap / Overwatch — usually unmodified 6s) | Immortals on a gun lane |
| **Smokescreen** *(Core)* | 1 | Your unit chosen as ranged target | Smoke / cover benefit (verify Core) | Soften a Devastator volley |
| **Heroic Intervention** *(Core)* | 1 | End of their Charge | One unit charges back | Protect the flag |
| **Counter-offensive** *(Core)* | 2 | Their Fight | Fights First / interrupt (verify Core) | Premium — rare for this list |
| **Rapid Ingress** *(Core)* | 1 | End of their Movement | Bring reserves in early (**>8″** if Deep Strike). Not BR1 | N/A unless you put something in reserves |

### Other

| Spend / note | CP | When | What it does (short) | Tip |
|--------------|----|------|----------------------|-----|
| **Command Re-roll** *(Core)* | 1 | After **you** make a roll | Re-roll that Hit / Wound / Save / Charge / Advance / Battle-shock (follow Core card) | Either turn — key RP D3 or a failed charge |
| **Snap Shooting** *(Core)* | 1 | See Core card | Restricted shooting mode — read the card | Core wins over laminate |
| **Crushing Impact** *(Core)* | 1 | Vehicle/Monster charge tricks | **N/A this list** | Ignore |
| **Technosorcerous menu** | **0** | Your Shooting | Free per Cryptek unit — not a stratagem | Untapped Power is the CP “second pick” |
| **Reanimation Protocols** | **0** | End of your Command | Free D3 per unit | Syphon is the CP off-turn version |

**Default first-game CP plan:** bank **Potentiality Syphon** · **Microscarab Swarm** when they try to wipe a brick · **Command Re-roll** on a key roll · **Molecular Targeting** if the brick is buried in modifiers.

## Turn checklist (your turn)

1. **Command** — +CP; Battle-shock; **REANIMATE** every unit (say the number); Warriors re-roll D3 (Legion).  
2. **Movement** — bricks usually Remain Stationary on flags; Scarabs Advance / Action; Geomancer pin.  
3. **Shooting** — announce menu pick per Cryptek brick; Oath-equivalent = whoever’s on your Action objective.  
4. **Charge / Fight** — usually rare; don’t gift Kill More.  
5. **End** — score **your** Primary.  
6. **Their Command** — consider **Potentiality Syphon** on an objective Cryptek brick.
