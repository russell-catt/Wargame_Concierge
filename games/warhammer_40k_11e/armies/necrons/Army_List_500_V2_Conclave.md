### Battle Formations (declare before deployment)

| Bodyguard | Support | Leader |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Necron Warriors (10) | **Cryptek Geomancer** (Gauntlet of Compression) | — none in this list |
| Immortals (5) | **Technomancer** (Atomic Disintegrators) | — none in this list |

Tomb Crawlers, Macrocytes, and Scarabs all operate **independently**.

> **No Leader on this list, and that is legal.** Crypteks are **Support** (`19.01`), not Leaders. You simply have no Overlord or Royal Warden to add, so each brick runs one Support character. The Warlord only needs to be a Character — the Geomancer qualifies.

> **Optional: Canoptek Retinue.** Tomb Crawlers have a rule letting them join a Cryptek-led unit at Battle Formations, raising that bodyguard's Starting Strength. Check the wording on your own datasheet before using it — if it works with a Support-only brick, the Crawlers can ride with the Warriors instead of screening alone.

## Which units get the Conclave menu

This is the decision that matters most, and this list splits 2 / 3.

| Unit | Cryptek keyword? | Gets the shooting menu? |
|------|------------------|--------------------------|
| Warriors + Geomancer | **Yes** (character confers it) | **Yes** — one pick per Shooting phase |
| Immortals + Technomancer | **Yes** | **Yes** — one pick per Shooting phase |
| Canoptek Tomb Crawlers | No | No |
| Canoptek Macrocytes | No | No |
| Canoptek Scarab Swarms | No | No |

Three of your five non-character units buy **board control and bodies**, not buffed guns. That is the trade V2 makes versus V1.

> **Synergistic Empowerment (1CP)** grants **CRYPTEK** to one nearby non-Monster/Vehicle Necron model until end of phase — the stratagem that temporarily pulls a Canoptek unit into the menu. Name and exact wording are `draft`; verify on the owned Faction Pack.

## How to play this list (plain language)

V2 is **two buffed shooting bricks plus a Canoptek board-control package**. You have far more bodies than V1 and far fewer wasted points.

1. **Warriors + Geomancer** hold your home or nearest objective (`14.02`). Reanimate at end of **Command** (`08.05`), re-rolling the D3 via Their Number is Legion. With the Gauntlet, the whole squad threatens **30" / 18"** — you no longer have to walk reapers into danger. Default menu pick: `[IGNORES COVER]` (`13.08`).
2. **Immortals + Technomancer** take the midfield. T5 / 3+ / FNP 5+ means they survive being shot at. Menu pick `[ANTI-INFANTRY 3+]` into bodies, or use **Atomic Disintegrators** anti-Vehicle / anti-Monster when armour shows up.
3. **Macrocytes Scout 8"** before turn 1. Park them where their **−1 to Hit** aura covers whichever brick will be shot at first, and keep the nanoscarab model within 3" of a unit that will reanimate.
4. **Tomb Crawlers** screen the Immortals or hold a flank alone. Inside 12" they ignore modifiers — they are your answer to an opponent hiding in cover. Their S6 claws will win a fight against most chaff.
5. **Scarabs** run wide for Actions and charge-blocking. Do not expect them to hold anything — **OC 0**.
6. **Spend 1CP** (`15.01`) on **Potentiality Syphon** during the opponent's **Command phase** (`08.01`) when a Cryptek brick sits on an objective — a second helping of reanimation every round.

**The one habit to build:** say your menu pick out loud before rolling, for each of the two Cryptek bricks, every Shooting phase. Forgetting it is the most common way to under-play this detachment.
