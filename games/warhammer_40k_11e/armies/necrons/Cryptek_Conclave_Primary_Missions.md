# CRYPTEK CONCLAVE — PRIORITY ASSETS PRIMARIES | Page 1 of 2

> **FORCE DISPOSITION:** **Priority Assets**  
> MFM: `CRYPTEK CONCLAVE - 2DP - PRIORITY ASSETS`  
> How matching works: [`../../setup/Chapter_Approved_Force_Dispositions.md`](../../setup/Chapter_Approved_Force_Dispositions.md)

You always play **your** Priority Assets Primary. Opponent’s Disposition picks **which** of the five below. Caps: **15VP**/round · **45VP**/game. Round 5 objective scoring often moves to **end of turn** if you go second — check the card.

**Conclave plan in one line:** Warrior brick **holds** Expansion / non-home OC; Scarabs / Crawlers / spare bodies **Action**; Cryptek menu keeps the brick shooting; Reanimation + Syphon punish anyone who chips you on a flag (`14.02`, `08.05`).

---

## Universal keys (every Priority Assets game)

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Write **PRIORITY ASSETS** on the list | Assume you share the opponent’s Primary text |
| Keep **one** durable OC brick on Expansion | Send the whole Warrior unit Actioning into the middle alone |
| Bring **spare Action pieces** (Scarabs, Crawlers, 5-model chips) | Run out of units that can still start Actions |
| Re-check OC on footprints every phase (`14.02`) | Forget battle-shocked = **0 OC** |
| Say Conclave pick out loud each Shooting phase | Forget end-of-Command reanimation |

## The five Primaries (you = Priority Assets)

### 1. Secure Asset — vs **Take and Hold**

| Your scoring shape (paraphrase — verify card) | Keys for Conclave |
|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| End of turn: **Action** Secure Asset on a non-home objective | Scarabs / Crawlers Action; Warriors hold |
| End of turn: bonus if you **destroy** a unit on a **Central** | Keep a gun unit that can reach Central |
| From BR2 Command (R5 EOT): hold non-home; bonus for holding **3+** | Expansion + home + one more — Warriors excel |
| Opponent: end-of-turn hold **3+**; Command hold **2+** / hold **more**; end-game your home | Stop **Hold More**; guard your home late |

**Success:** Steady Actions + never gift Hold More. On maps where both sides sit on three easily, defence in depth on Expansion beats over-aggression.

### 2. Vital Link — vs **Purge the Foe**

| Your scoring shape | Keys for Conclave |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| End of turn: hold Central + **Operation Markers** you place via Action (stacking VP) | **Warriors on Central** Action repeatedly — this is the tarpit mission |
| BR2+ Command: hold non-home; bonus if that is Central | Prefer Central for the 8VP-style ticks |
| End game: opponent’s home (big VP) | Optional hail Mary — not plan A |
| Opponent: kill units / Kill More; also hold non-home / Hold More | Do **not** feed free kills with Scarab suicides |

**Success:** One durable brick that Actions Central every turn. Conclave loves this — Reanimation makes the tarpit honest. If blocked once, pivot to holding one safe non-home and staging for late home steal.

### 3. Sabotage — vs **Priority Assets** (mirror)

| Your scoring shape | Keys for Conclave |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| End of turn: **Sabotage Action** on each non-home (bonus in **enemy territory**, Central counts) | Max Action pieces — Scarabs are gold |
| BR2+ Command: hold 1+ non-home | Keep Expansion locked while Actions run |
| Opponent: identical | Deny first-turn mega scores; Overwatch / engage the small Central |

**Success:** Early Action volume wins. Deploy to stop opponent’s turn-1 triple Sabotage. Quantity of cheap units > one elite blob.

### 4. Extract Relic — vs **Disruption**

| Your scoring shape | Keys for Conclave |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| Disruption places **Operation Markers** on terrain outside their DZ | Know which markers exist before round 1 |
| Once/turn **Sensor Sweep** Action on a Central (while you control it) removes a marker; you score for completing Sweeps | Be the one Sweeping — Warriors or Crawlers on Central |
| End of turn: Sweep done · kill a unit that started on an objective · final-marker control bonus | Kill on-objective units when safe; contest final marker terrain |
| BR2+ Command: hold non-home | Expansion still matters |
| Opponent also Sweeps (denies your Sweep VP) and scores kills / final marker | Engagement-block Centrals after you have enough Sweeps |

**Success:** Trade Sweeps early on markers in **their** half; then lock the last marker on **your** Expansion terrain. Durable multi-model units block their Sweep.

### 5. Vanguard Operation — vs **Reconnaissance**

| Your scoring shape | Keys for Conclave |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| Once/turn **Vanguard Operation** Action: start in Shooting while in a terrain area in **enemy territory** (Central counts); completes if no enemies in that area | Send disposable / tough Action unit into enemy-half terrain |
| End of turn: kill 1+ enemy units | Pick off chips; don’t donate your Action unit for free |
| BR2+ Command: hold 1+ non-home | Warriors on Expansion |
| End game: control their **home** (big VP) | Late push only if Actions already banked |
| Opponent: hold Centrals · kill your units that started in terrain · hold multiple non-homes · end-game deny your presence in their territory | Action units that finish and leave, or tarpit that won’t die |

**Success:** Action in enemy territory **without** feeding terrain-kill points. Hold Expansion every Command. End with a model in their half to deny their end-game bonus when possible.

## Conclave toolkit vs mission asks

| Tool | Use it when |
|------|-------------|
| **Warriors + Cryptek** | Vital Link tarpit · Expansion hold · Syphon target |
| **Scarabs** | Sabotage / Secure Asset Actions · wide OC |
| **Tomb Crawlers** | Midboard Action + shoot; not Cryptek menu |
| **Potentiality Syphon** | Unit already on objective, opponent’s Command — buy another RP tick |
| **Atomic Disintegrators** | Vehicle/Monster menus when Actions are done |
