# Gamefound postmortem — campaign ended 2026-09-18

**`confidence: draft`** — historical campaign analysis, checked **2026-09-24**. Platform records and publisher statements establish the sequence; they do not independently establish why the campaign was cancelled.

## What happened

| Date | Event | Evidence |
|------|-------|----------|
| **2026-09-15** | The mixed digital-and-physical campaign launched. | [Gamefound launch update](https://gamefound.com/en/projects/redmakers/the-warcode/updates/1), timestamped 11:15 AM |
| **2026-09-17** | RedMakers publicly stated that it was cancelling the campaign and that pledges would not be charged. | [Cancellation update](https://gamefound.com/en/projects/redmakers/the-warcode/updates/2), timestamped 10:56 AM |
| **2026-09-18** | Gamefound records the platform end date and says the creator cancelled the project; no funds were collected. | [Final Gamefound page](https://gamefound.com/en/projects/redmakers/the-warcode) |

The September 17 statement and September 18 platform end are separate events. Both dates matter.

## Final platform display

Gamefound's final display, **retrieved 2026-09-24**, showed:

- **63 backers**
- **€7,894 pledged toward €45,000**
- **17.54%**
- **2,800 followers**
- **No funds collected**

These are closed Gamefound counters, not general audience metrics. They do not show why followers did or did not pledge, whether notifications reached them, or which traffic converted.

## What the explanations establish

### Publisher diagnosis

RedMakers said the game was ready but the launch was not, and identified **“reach”** as what was not ready. That establishes RedMakers' diagnosis. It does **not** independently prove that reach was the sole or primary cause.

### Owner testimony

The project owner separately described the launch as having **“insufficient buzz”** in testimony supplied on 2026-09-24. This is useful first-hand context, but it is not RedMakers' wording and is not independent proof of causation.

### Independent coverage

[Brückenkopf Online](https://www.brueckenkopf-online.com/2026/the-warcode-gamefound-laeuft/) reported the campaign as live on September 17, corroborating public visibility during its short run. Its article mainly repeated campaign material and did not investigate the cancellation.

[Tabletop Sentinel](https://www.tabletopsentinel.com/columns/crowdfund-quest/crowdfund-quest-weekly-19-09-26-new-project-launches-over-the-last-week) listed the project among that week's launches. The listing appeared after the cancellation window, repeated campaign copy, and did not establish current status or cause.

## Offer transition

The cancelled Gamefound offer included digital STL/Print & Play paths and physical products. RedMakers' cancellation statement said a physical edition was intended to follow a digital relaunch. That is deferred publisher intent with no confirmed date or guarantee.

The later Kickstarter is a separate, digital-only offer. Physical boxes are **not** Kickstarter rewards. See [`Kickstarter_and_Community_2026-09-24.md`](Kickstarter_and_Community_2026-09-24.md).

## What remains unknown

- Whether reach or buzz was the primary cause of cancellation
- Follower quality, notification delivery, traffic sources, and conversion behaviour
- Why Kickstarter was selected for the digital relaunch
- Timing, contents, price, platform, and fulfilment plan for any physical edition
