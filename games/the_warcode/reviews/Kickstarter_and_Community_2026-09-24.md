# Kickstarter and community analysis — 2026-09-24

**`confidence: draft`** — live campaign snapshot retrieved **2026-09-24**. Campaign terms and public coverage can change.

## Current campaign

The [Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/redmakers/the-warcode-stl-campaign) launched **2026-09-23** and is scheduled to run through **2026-10-23**. It is an all-or-nothing, **digital-only STL and Print & Play campaign**:

- rewards are delivered digitally;
- STL tiers cover miniatures, terrain, and tokens in different combinations;
- Print & Play files are included by tier;
- no physical manufacturing, packaging, or shipping is included.

Physical boxes belonged to the cancelled Gamefound proposition. RedMakers has stated an intention to pursue a physical edition later, but no date, platform, contents, price, or delivery commitment is established. Physical boxes are **not campaign rewards here**.

Live pledge totals, backer counts, percentages, comments, and reward distribution are intentionally omitted. They are volatile and belong in timestamped research, not durable shipping analysis. This page makes no claim about momentum or likely final performance.

## Try-before-backing routes

The campaign FAQ points readers to the free rulebook and a public [Steam Workshop item](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3776386741). The Workshop listing describes the game as playable, but visibly labels its current rules **v0.8.7**. The current free PDF mechanics baseline is **v0.8.9-F**.

Treat the public mod as an accessible preview with visible version drift. Its completeness, scripting quality, and mechanical equivalence have not been audited.

## Community coverage found by 2026-09-24

Public third-party coverage was sparse. No clearly independent review of the digital-only Kickstarter offer was found one day after launch.

### Guerrilla Miniature Games first look

- **Disclosure label:** **Preview copy / supplied product**
- **What was disclosed:** the hosts said they received the game early and thanked RedMakers for supplying miniatures and terrain.
- **What was not established:** payment, sponsorship, affiliate terms, editorial control, or the exact rules build.

The long-form first look included a rules explanation and one played game. The hosts found the core presentation approachable after learning the icons and highlighted ammunition, reloading, terrain interaction, objectives, and catch-up contracts as meaningful choices. They also raised an activation-economy concern after casualties, wanted more equipment and faction customization, and thought additional maps or scenarios would improve replayability.

Important limits: the hosts identified a melee rules error in their own game; the material was a pre-release preview; the video discussed the former physical/Gamefound-era proposition rather than reviewing the later Kickstarter offer. It cannot establish balance, long-term replayability, production quality, file quality, fulfilment, or Kickstarter value.

### Other identifiable discussion

- **TheTabletopEngineer newsletter — independent/unknown:** expressed cautious pre-launch interest while worrying whether a new game could build a usable local player base. No hands-on test; pre-dates both campaigns.
- **Brückenkopf preview and launch pages — disclosure unknown:** primarily repeated promotional material. Self-selected commenters raised visual-originality, adoption, and campaign-fatigue concerns; these comments are not a representative sample.
- **Community forum post — independent for the visible post:** showed individual interest alongside questions about aesthetics, price, and rules. Only limited indexed text was available.
- **Tabletop Sentinel roundup — disclosure unknown:** listed the Gamefound launch but offered no hands-on assessment or cancellation analysis.
- **RedMakers videos and selected testimonials — publisher-owned promotional:** useful for understanding what RedMakers markets, not independent validation. Publisher-selected audience figures and testimonials do not establish backing intent or conversion.

“No disclosure found” means no disclosure was visible in the reviewed material. It does not prove that no relationship existed.

## Defensible reading

The strongest third-party evidence available is a supplied-preview playthrough that was broadly positive about approachability and tactical choices while flagging preview incompleteness and possible activation snowballing. Announcement-page communities also raised originality, local-adoption, and campaign-fatigue concerns.

This evidence does not prove broad demand, conversion, campaign momentum, balance, or delivery quality. The timing of favourable coverage does not establish that it caused any campaign result.
