## Commander Rickman (Leader)

> Source: The Warcode Rulebook V.0.8.7-F.pdf — p.33 — PROTAGEN MARINES TEAM LIST (via OCR)

**Core:** HP 9 · A 3 · M 6 · Armour 4

**Ranged — Pistol:** AP 1 to shoot · AP 1 to reload · Pen +1 · Damage 1 / 2 · Shots 3

**Melee — Volt Sword:** AP 1 · Pen re-roll 1s on armour penetration until higher

> LEADER: The player gains 2 re-roll points at the start of each round.

Also shown on p.5 as the rules example character.
