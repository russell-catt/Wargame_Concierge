# Protagen Marines

**Engineering faction in heavy suits** — trade mobility for resilience, take a position and hold it. **`confidence: draft`**

**Unofficial personal notes.** The Warcode is property of RedMakers. Not endorsed by RedMakers or Gamefound.

---

## Identity (teaching paraphrase)

Pre-launch marketing tags: **Durable**, **Methodical**, **Punishing**. Stated difficulty: mid / beginner-friendly.

On the published beta roster:

- **Armour** sits a step above Ulfari across the board; two units are **slow** (lower Movement Range).
- **Shotgun-heavy** ranged profiles push advance-and-clear play rather than long-range trades.
- **Smasher** punishes approach with forced **Melee Lock** within 1 inch even without base contact.
- **Blade** carries the squad's highest melee attack volume (**Combat Claws**).

Tension to know: Protagen also fields the hardest-hitting melee weapon profile and high melee volume — do not assume Ulfari always win the scrap.

---

## Lore and doctrine

**Academy frame:** Protagen began as an Erdan corporation and became a state by uniting Abyron Belt stations. Distance, dispersed and off-ecliptic infrastructure, and guerrilla warfare provide strategic depth. Protagen also maintains bases on some Cassini moons. (`The-Warcode-Lorebook-Tactical-Doctrine-Field-Edition.pdf` — PDF p.25 — Section 5.)

**Named scholarship:** a Meridian Institute monograph places Protagen resistance at the opening of the Blackout War. This is attributed historical analysis, not academy connective prose. (Lorebook PDF p.20 — Section 3.6, “The Blackout War: From Insurrection to Equilibrium”.)

**Faction-authored position:** a Protagen executive presents scientific progress, engineering, individual liberty, open communication, and expansion beyond the system as core values. The same source calls the long conflict the **Silence War**. This is a corporate press-service statement, not neutral academy assessment. (Lorebook PDF p.26 — Section 5, “Our Right to the Galaxy”.)

**Internal tension:** an MDR critic portrays station autonomy as conditional on Protagen's absolute collective-security power and access to necessities. A Protagen scientist separately praises Marine bioengineering while acknowledging that its resources could improve basic health elsewhere. One is hostile commentary and one is faction-affiliated testimony; together they expose tensions without proving every station shares one social model. (Lorebook PDF pp.26, 28 — Section 5.)

**Marine doctrine bridge:** the lore supports heavily invested biological and suit engineering built for deep-space survival. It does not provide game effects; resilience, movement, weapons, and abilities come from **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf** and [`Squad_Datasheet.md`](Squad_Datasheet.md).

Shared geography and terminology: [`../../lore/Theatre_of_Operations.md`](../../lore/Theatre_of_Operations.md) and [`../../lore/Historical_Timeline.md`](../../lore/Historical_Timeline.md).

## How they win

Hold VP tokens with bodies that do not die easily. Advance through partial cover, overwatch lanes, and use **Smasher** as an anchor enemy assault elements must respect. Protect **Commander Rickman** — killing him removes **2 re-roll points per round**.
