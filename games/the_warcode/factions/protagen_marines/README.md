# Protagen Marines

**Engineering faction in heavy suits** — trade mobility for resilience, take a position and hold it. **`confidence: draft`**

**Unofficial personal notes.** The Warcode is property of RedMakers. Not endorsed by RedMakers or Gamefound.

---

## Identity (teaching paraphrase)

Pre-launch marketing tags: **Durable**, **Methodical**, **Punishing**. Stated difficulty: mid / beginner-friendly.

On the published beta roster:

- **Armour** sits a step above Ulfari across the board; two units are **slow** (lower Movement Range).
- **Shotgun-heavy** ranged profiles push advance-and-clear play rather than long-range trades.
- **Smasher** punishes approach with forced **Melee Lock** within 1 inch even without base contact.
- **Blade** carries the squad's highest melee attack volume (**Combat Claws**).

Tension to know: Protagen also fields the hardest-hitting melee weapon profile and high melee volume — do not assume Ulfari always win the scrap.

## How they win

Hold VP tokens with bodies that do not die easily. Advance through partial cover, overwatch lanes, and use **Smasher** as an anchor enemy assault elements must respect. Protect **Commander Rickman** — killing him removes **2 re-roll points per round**.
