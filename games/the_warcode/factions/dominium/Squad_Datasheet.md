# Custodia Silens — squad datasheet

**`confidence: verified`** against **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf**, PDF pages **39–40**, section **“CUSTODIA SILENS — TEAM LIST.”**

**Profile key:** ranged = Shots · Range · Ammo · Shoot AP · Reload AP · Pen · Damage/Critical. Melee = Strength · Range · AP · Pen · Damage/Critical.

## Justiciar Julius

**Core:** HP 9 · A 3 · M 6 · Armour 3
**Large-Caliber Pistol:** 5 · 6" · 2 · 1 AP · 1 AP · −1 · 1/2
**Combat Knife:** 2 · 1" · 1 AP · 0 · 2/3.

- **Leader:** gain 2 re-roll points at the start of each round.
- **Justiciar’s Influence:** enemies within 6" and line of sight reduce Agility by 1.

Source: **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf — p.39 — “CUSTODIA SILENS — TEAM LIST” (Justiciar Julius).**

**Name note:** contract 4186 spells this target **Justicar Julius**; the roster and aura use **Justiciar Julius**. They are treated as the same character, without silently correcting either source surface.

## Assassin

**Core:** HP 8 · A 3 · M 7 · Armour 3
**Weapons:** Large-Caliber Pistol; Combat Knife.

Starts with **2 grenades** and cannot take other equipment.

Source: **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf — p.39 — “CUSTODIA SILENS — TEAM LIST” (Assassin).**

## Punisher

**Core:** HP 10 · A 2 · M 5 · Armour 4
**Heavy Weapon:** 4 · 8" · 1 · 2 AP · 1 AP · −2 · 2/3
**Combat Maul:** 3 · 1" · 1 AP · 0 · 2/3.

Cannot be healed by medkits and cannot carry equipment.

Source: **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf — p.39 — “CUSTODIA SILENS — TEAM LIST” (Punisher).**

## Cremator

**Core:** HP 8 · A 3 · M 6 · Armour 3
**Hand Flamethrower:** 2 · 5" · ammo 3 · 1 AP · reload AP — · Pen 0 · 2/4
**Volt Sword:** 4 · 1" · 1 AP · 0 · 2/3; re-roll armour-penetration dice showing 1 until higher.

The flamethrower ignores Agility and partial cover, never needs reloading, and applies Burning to the target and **all units** within 2". Burning deals 1 damage at round end and then clears. A medkit removes Burning; a unit cannot have more than one Burning effect.

Source: **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf — p.39 — “CUSTODIA SILENS — TEAM LIST” (Cremator).**

**Source gap:** the profile prints ammo 3 and no reload cost while the ability says it never needs reloading. The book does not define whether this means infinite ammunition, automatic replenishment, or three lifetime shots.

## Confessor

**Core:** HP 7 · A 2 · M 6 · Armour 3
**Fist:** 1 · 1" · 1 AP · +1 · 1/2.

Cannot be healed by medkits and cannot carry equipment.

**Inspiration:** spend 1 AP on a friendly unit within 8" and line of sight that already activated; spend both AP to choose two different units. Each receives a later 1-AP activation after all ordinary activations on both sides. Cannot be used in melee.

Source: **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf — p.40 — “CUSTODIA SILENS — TEAM LIST” (Confessor).**

**Timing gaps:** the book does not order Inspired units between players or within a side, state whether one unit can be Inspired repeatedly, or explain what happens if it dies before the delayed activation.

## Tormentor

**Core:** HP 7 · A 3 · M 6 · Armour 2
**Directed Energy:** 3 · 8" · ammo — · 1 AP · reload — · Pen — · 2/3
**Fist:** 1 · 1" · 1 AP · +1 · 1/2.

- Directed Energy ignores armour, is a ranged weapon usable once per round, and cannot be used for Overwatch.
- Each critical hit deals 1 damage to its user; this sentence calls the weapon **Focused Energy**.
- **Tormentor’s Influence:** enemies within 5" take 1 damage at round end; no line of sight required.
- **Choke:** for 1 AP, mark an enemy within 8" and line of sight. It takes 1 damage each round end regardless of later distance or line of sight, ending only when Tormentor is destroyed. A unit can receive Choke once; at most two units can be affected, and a dead target frees a slot. Cannot be used in melee.

Source: **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf — p.40 — “CUSTODIA SILENS — TEAM LIST” (Tormentor).**

**Source gaps:** no separate Focused Energy profile exists. Because Directed Energy ignores armour, the book does not identify which roll supplies its critical hits. “Can receive Choke once” is also not defined as once at a time, per Tormentor, or per game.

## Executor

**Core:** HP 8 · A 3 · M 6 · Armour 3
**Shotgun:** 2 · 5" · 2 · 1 AP · 1 AP · −1 · 3/4
**Combat Knife:** 2 · 1" · 1 AP · 0 · 2/3.

Source: **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf — p.40 — “CUSTODIA SILENS — TEAM LIST” (Executor).**

## Lancer

**Core:** HP 8 · A 3 · M 6 · Armour 3
**Combat Spear:** 4 · 1" · 1 AP · −1 · 2/3.

Enemies within 1" count as Melee Locked even when bases do not touch.

Source: **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf — p.40 — “CUSTODIA SILENS — TEAM LIST” (Lancer).**

**Source gap:** apply the printed lock exception, but the general multi-lock escape procedure still assumes touching bases and does not supply new movement geometry.

## Shared unresolved interactions

- No minimum or stacking order is stated for Agility reductions.
- Round-end order among Burning, Tormentor’s Influence, Choke, and healing is not stated.
- It is unclear whether a medkit can remove Burning from Punisher or Confessor, or whether one medkit use both heals and removes Burning.
- Burning does not exempt allies or the Cremator.
- AP-costed abilities are absent from the p.11 Overwatch trigger list despite the broader p.10 “takes action” wording.
