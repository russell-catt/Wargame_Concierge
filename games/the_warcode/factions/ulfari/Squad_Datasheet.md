## Reaper

> Source: The Warcode Rulebook V.0.8.7-F.pdf — p.36 — ULFARI TEAM LIST (via OCR)

**Core:** HP 8 · A **5** (agility cap) · M **7** · Armour 3

**Melee — Razor Blade only:** AP 1 · Pen −1 · Damage 2 / 3

**No ranged weapon** — cannot shoot or Overwatch. At agility 5, cover and friendly-screen bonuses to hit may not apply (see rules pp.11–12).

## Shade

> Source: The Warcode Rulebook V.0.8.7-F.pdf — p.36 — ULFARI TEAM LIST (via OCR)

**Core:** HP 8 · A 4 · M 6 · Armour 3

**Ranged — Rifle:** AP 1 · AP 1 · Pen 0 · Damage 2 / 3 · Shots 2

**Melee — Combat Knife:** AP 1

> SPECIAL ABILITY: Sniper — When this unit shoots, the Target's agility is reduced by 1.

Contract worked example on p.22 names **Shade** as a valid elimination target when Player B fields Ulfari.
