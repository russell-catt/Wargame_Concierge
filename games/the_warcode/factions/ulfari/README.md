# Ulfari

**Outside-system aliens** — close distance fast, hit before the opponent reacts. **`confidence: draft`**

**Unofficial personal notes.** The Warcode is property of RedMakers. Not endorsed by RedMakers or Gamefound.

---

## Identity (teaching paraphrase)

Pre-launch marketing tags: **Aggressive**, **Fast**, **Melee-focused**. Stated difficulty: **beginner-friendly**.

On the published beta roster:

- **Agility** a step above Protagen; **armour** a step below — the older “average armour” marketing line was already generous in the original two-roster comparison and is not a current four-squad benchmark.
- **Phantom** and **Reaper** exceed baseline **Movement Range** (M 7).
- **Reaper** is **melee-only** — no ranged weapon, therefore **no Overwatch**.
- **Shade** strips **1 Agility** from shoot targets (Sniper).
- **Soul Eater** leads the squad and carries the Leader re-roll rule.

The unit names read like imposed codenames, but neither the rulebook nor lorebook confirms their origin. Treat that as project interpretation, not faction testimony.

---

## Lore and doctrine

**Academy frame:** Arrival begins in year 2026 with confirmed external intelligence identified as Ulfari. The dossier contains no clearly Ulfari-authored source, so their own name, political aims, reason for entering the system, and account of first contact remain unknown. (`The-Warcode-Lorebook-Tactical-Doctrine-Field-Edition.pdf` — PDF pp.5, 35–39 — Sections 3.1 and 7.)

**Contested first contact:** an older pre-launch publisher summary was previously rendered here as sudden Ulfari destruction of a station. That wording lacks a captured primary excerpt in this track and is no longer presented as fact. The later Dominium internal file instead records four Protagen crew and one non-human entity aboard the Erebus-7 carrier in non-hostile postures with no raised weapons. (Lorebook PDF p.35 — Section 7, “Internal Analysis — First Contact Event,” part I.)

The same file then records Dominium plans to suppress the evidence and orders Custodia Silens to eliminate witnesses and records, with forced hostility as a contingency. Those are documented **orders**, not proof of completion. They do not establish that every later clash was engineered or that all later Ulfari violence was fabricated. (Lorebook PDF pp.36–37 — Section 7, “Internal Analysis — First Contact Event,” parts III–V.)

**Hostile-source combat assessment:** a later Dominium military briefing describes observed Ulfari fighters as individually skilled, aggressive, mobile in low or zero gravity, inclined to close to melee, and capable of cooperation despite apparent competition. This is an enemy assessment after Dominium ordered maximum hostility—not neutral species psychology. (Lorebook PDF p.38 — Section 7, “Ulfari Combat Profile”.)

**Project interpretation:** first contact is unresolved, Ulfari intent is unknown, and later violence is real enough to generate military reporting without revealing who initiated the wider conflict. Mechanics still come only from the current rulebook.

Full comparison and sourcing method: [`../../lore/Historical_Timeline.md`](../../lore/Historical_Timeline.md) and [`../../lore/Source_Methodology.md`](../../lore/Source_Methodology.md).

## How they win

Close distance using **Phantom** / **Reaper**, trade shots where agility wins, and force melee with **Razor Blades**. Use **Shade** to soften high-agility targets before the squad commits. Accept lower armour — positioning and first activation advantage matter more than trading in the open.
