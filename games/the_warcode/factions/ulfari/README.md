# Ulfari

**Outside-system aliens** — close distance fast, hit before the opponent reacts. **`confidence: draft`**

**Unofficial personal notes.** The Warcode is property of RedMakers. Not endorsed by RedMakers or Gamefound.

---

## Identity (teaching paraphrase)

Pre-launch marketing tags: **Aggressive**, **Fast**, **Melee-focused**. Stated difficulty: **beginner-friendly**.

On the published beta roster:

- **Agility** a step above Protagen; **armour** a step below — "average armour" in marketing is generous with only two rosters public.
- **Phantom** and **Reaper** exceed baseline **Movement Range** (M 7).
- **Reaper** is **melee-only** — no ranged weapon, therefore **no Overwatch**.
- **Shade** strips **1 Agility** from shoot targets (Sniper).
- **Soul Eater** leads the squad and carries the Leader re-roll rule.

Lore hook (marketing): Ulfari destroyed a station without warning — catalyst for the sealed-system war.

Unit names (Ravener, Wraith, Phantom, etc.) read as **imposed codenames**, not native language — unstated in rules text.

## How they win

Close distance using **Phantom** / **Reaper**, trade shots where agility wins, and force melee with **Razor Blades**. Use **Shade** to soften high-agility targets before the squad commits. Accept lower armour — positioning and first activation advantage matter more than trading in the open.
