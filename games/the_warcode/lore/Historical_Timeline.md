# Historical Timeline

The academy organizes Erdan history around Year 0, the appearance of Dominium. The chronology is useful, but its evidence includes academy summaries, faction narratives, hostile analysis, and records selected by an unknown compiler.

**Unofficial personal notes.** The Warcode is property of RedMakers. Not endorsed by RedMakers or Gamefound.

---

## Calendar

- 28 hours per day
- 39 days per month
- 12 months per year
- 468 days per year
- negative years before Dominium; five named epochs after Year 0

These are academy structural statements. (`The-Warcode-Lorebook-Tactical-Doctrine-Field-Edition.pdf` — PDF p.5 — Section 3.1, “Chronological Framework”.)

## Epochs

### Epoch 0 — Industrial Era, pre-zero

Erda held many industrial nation-states using analog-mechanical and electromechanical systems. The academy calls the surviving record fragmentary and gives no duration. (Lorebook PDF pp.5–6 — Sections 3.1–3.2.)

### Epoch 1 — Foundation, years 0–238

Dominium appeared as a technologically superior city-state of unknown origin. It secured itself through trade and allied buffer states while restricting outside access. The academy warns that contemporary outsiders underestimated its technological advantage. (Lorebook PDF pp.5, 7 — Sections 3.1 and 3.3.)

### Epoch 2 — The Burning, years 238–380

Nuclear war among Erda's states began in 238. Dominium halted nuclear production by force, issued its Manifest in 248, and eventually consolidated control. Universal submission was officially announced in 303; the last regional conflict ended in 380. (Lorebook PDF pp.8–16 — Section 3.4.)

> **Duration warning:** the academy says the Burning lasted 142 years, but lists phases of 9, 56, and 86 years, totaling 151. Its phase ranges—238–247, 247–303, and 303–380—yield 9, 56, and 77 years, totaling 142. The printed 86-year attrition duration is therefore an unresolved internal arithmetic inconsistency, likely a typo; it is not silently corrected here. (Lorebook PDF p.8 — Section 3.4, “The Burning”.)

The shared endpoint years 238 and 380 are best read as transition years unless a day-level source specifies otherwise.

### Epoch 3 — Golden Age, years 380–1648

The academy frames this as the longest peaceful period, lasting 1,268 years, while Dominium governed Erda and humanity expanded throughout the system. Inserted sources disagree over whether this was prosperity, corporate domination, or oligarchic control. Those judgments belong to their speakers; the dossier does not reconcile them into one neutral verdict. (Lorebook PDF p.5 — Section 3.1; pp.17–19 — Section 3.5.)

### Epoch 4 — Blackout, years 1648–2026

Dominium imposed external isolation in 1648. Protagen resistance, outer-system war, renewed conflict on Erda, and the eventual formation of MDR produced the modern balance of power. The war never formally ended, although it had diminished to local engagements by 2026. (Lorebook PDF pp.20–22 — Sections 3.6 and 4.)

MDR's founding congress met in year 1689 aboard **Mars, a Kirkwood Belt settlement**. This is separate from the planet **Huoxing**, which became MDR's main planetary base. The founding details come from an encyclopedia extract, not academy narration. (Lorebook PDF p.30 — Section 6, “Founding of the MDR”.)

### Epoch 5 — Arrival, year 2026 / present

The academy marks confirmed Ulfari contact as the start of Arrival. The consequences are unresolved. (Lorebook PDF p.5 — Section 3.1, “Chronological Framework”.)

## Three related war terms

- **Blackout** is Dominium's isolation policy, the year-1648 initiating event, and the academy's name for the 1648–2026 epoch.
- **Blackout War** is the label used in a Meridian Institute historical monograph excerpt for the conflict that followed.
- **Silence War** appears across interested sources: a Protagen press statement, a Protagen officer's account, an MDR address, and a Dominium military briefing. It concerns the same long conflict, but it is not a neutral synonym for either Blackout or the Blackout War.

Do not use the three terms as neutral synonyms. Preserve the speaker and intended meaning. (Lorebook PDF pp.5, 20, 22, 26, 30–31, 34 — Sections 3.1, 3.6, and 4–6.)

## Contested first contact at Erebus-7

An older pre-launch publisher summary was previously rendered in this project as a claim that Ulfari suddenly destroyed a station. The exact wording is not preserved in the track's captured primary evidence, so that claim is historical marketing context—not an established event.

The later lorebook supplies a materially different Dominium internal record:

1. Erebus-7 is called a decommissioned Protagen deep-range carrier.
2. An authenticated image reportedly shows four Protagen crew and one non-human entity in non-hostile postures, with no raised weapons.
3. Dominium analysts assess the entity as extra-system and fear the disclosure would undermine Blackout doctrine.
4. Dominium offices propose information noise, witness elimination, evidence destruction, and forced hostility if neutralization fails.

(Lorebook PDF pp.35–37 — Section 7, “Dominium Security Chancellery — Internal Analysis — First Contact Event,” parts I–V.)

### What the evidence supports

- A Dominium internal file records an initially non-hostile encounter.
- The same file records suppression and escalation **orders**.
- Later hostile-source reporting describes violent Ulfari combat.

### What the evidence does not support

- It does not confirm that the orders were completed.
- It does not establish who destroyed any station or whether Erebus-7 was destroyed.
- It does not prove who initiated every later clash.
- It does not prove all later Ulfari violence was fabricated.

The project interpretation is therefore **contested first contact, unknown Ulfari intent**. See [`Source_Methodology.md`](Source_Methodology.md) for why an internal hostile record can be probative without becoming omniscient truth.
