# Theatre of Operations

Read the theatre from the inner system outward:

**Erda → Kepler / Hydron / Huoxing → Kirkwood Belt / Mars → Cassini and its moons → Abyron Belt / off-ecliptic sites**

The academy's summary is the key constraint: no faction has enough force to control the whole theatre. (`The-Warcode-Lorebook-Tactical-Doctrine-Field-Edition.pdf` — PDF p.5 — Section 2, “Summary”.)

**Unofficial personal notes.** The Warcode is property of RedMakers. Not endorsed by RedMakers or Gamefound.

## Inner system

### Erda — 1.00 AU

Erda is the only naturally habitable world. The academy places roughly 50 billion people there and describes Dominium as holding the planet, orbital superiority, and access to space. This is academy framing, not a faction speech. (Lorebook PDF p.4 — Section 2, “Erda”.)

Kepler and Hydron are named among the inner planets but receive no further location dossier in this edition. Their governments, environments, and loyalties remain unspecified. (Lorebook PDF p.3 — Section 2, “Inner Orbits”.)

### Huoxing — 1.91 AU

Huoxing is a **planet** under MDR control. Its human population lives in sealed infrastructure within a carbon-dioxide atmosphere. MDR influence extends through nearby space toward Cassini, but the academy calls its control of Cassini's moon system **partial**. (Lorebook PDF p.5 — Section 2, “Huoxing”.)

> **Huoxing is not Mars.** Huoxing is a planet. Mars is a settlement in the Kirkwood Belt where the MDR founding congress met in year 1689. The settlement is identified by the Free Systems Encyclopedia extract, not by academy connective prose. (Lorebook PDF p.30 — Section 6, “Founding of the MDR”.)

## Middle system

### Kirkwood Belt — 2.5–3.8 AU

The Kirkwood Belt is an industrial extraction region contested by multiple factions. The academy assigns no continuously controlling authority. Mars was its largest settlement at the time of the MDR founding congress; that historical description does not establish its present rank or exclusive control. (Lorebook PDF pp.5, 30 — Sections 2 and 6.)

### Cassini — 6.2 AU

Cassini is a gas giant with overlapping faction presence:

- the academy says MDR has **partial control** over the moon system;
- the academy's Protagen introduction says Protagen maintains bases on some Cassini moons.

These claims are compatible. Neither grants exclusive ownership, and the source does not identify particular moons or borders. (Lorebook PDF pp.5, 25 — Sections 2 and 5.)

## Outer system

### Abyron Belt — 18–40 AU

The Abyron Belt is Protagen's main region. Its widely dispersed stations include off-ecliptic sites, turning distance into strategic depth and making detection, targeting, and force projection difficult. (Lorebook PDF p.5 — Section 2, “Abyron Belt”.)

### Erebus-7

The Dominium internal first-contact file identifies Erebus-7 as a decommissioned Protagen deep-range **carrier** in far orbit outside the ecliptic. It does not identify the vessel as a station. The file is strong evidence of what Dominium recorded internally, but remains a selected Dominium primary source. (Lorebook PDF p.35 — Section 7, “Internal Analysis — First Contact Event,” part I.)

---

## Operational reading

- **Dominium:** population center, orbital control, and access monopoly at Erda.
- **MDR:** planetary base at Huoxing, extensive middle-system relationships, incomplete reach at Cassini.
- **Protagen:** dispersed outer-system infrastructure, Abyron depth, and Cassini moon bases.

This is project synthesis from the cited academy geography. It is not a precise map and should not be used to invent travel times, borders, or ownership.

**Ulfari source boundary:** the academy names Arrival as contact with external
intelligence; a Dominium internal analysis separately assesses an extra-system
origin. No source maps Ulfari territory or supplies a self-authored geopolitical
account. (Lorebook PDF p.5 — Section 3.1; PDF p.35 — Section 7, “Dominium
Security Chancellery — Internal Analysis — First Contact Event,” part II.)
