# Lore Field Guide

The Tactical Doctrine Field Edition presents a contested single-star theatre: Dominium holds Erda, MDR centers its power on Huoxing, Protagen occupies the distant Abyron Belt, and Ulfari arrival disrupts a centuries-old armed standoff.

**Unofficial personal notes.** The Warcode is property of RedMakers. Not endorsed by RedMakers or Gamefound.

---

## Read this first

The lorebook is framed as an academy dossier, not an omniscient history. Its connective material supplies the academy's operational frame; speeches, sermons, interviews, intercepted records, and analyses are interested primary sources. These pages label the source voice and keep project interpretation separate. See [`Source_Methodology.md`](Source_Methodology.md).

## Lore path

1. [`Theatre_of_Operations.md`](Theatre_of_Operations.md) — worlds, belts, settlements, and overlapping control.
2. [`Historical_Timeline.md`](Historical_Timeline.md) — Erdan calendar, five post-zero epochs, and contested first contact.
3. [`Source_Methodology.md`](Source_Methodology.md) — how to weigh academy framing, faction testimony, hostile assessments, and project synthesis.
4. Faction overviews — [`Dominium`](../factions/dominium/README.md), [`MDR`](../factions/mdr/README.md), [`Protagen Marines`](../factions/protagen_marines/README.md), and [`Ulfari`](../factions/ulfari/README.md).

The faction pages carry only the lore needed to understand each playable squad. This directory holds the shared setting spine so those pages do not become duplicate dossiers.

## Mechanics firewall

The lorebook's “Tactical Application” section directs practical squad instruction to the accompanying rulebook; it does not add or override mechanics. Use **The-Warcode-Rulebook-V.0.8.9-F.pdf** and the [`rules overview`](../rules/Overview.md) for play.
