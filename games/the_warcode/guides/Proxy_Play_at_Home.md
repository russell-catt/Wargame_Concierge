# Proxy play at home

**`confidence: draft`** — practical setup for learning Warcode with models you already own. **Unofficial; not endorsed by RedMakers or Gamefound.**

Goal: one evening, **eight models per side**, **Protagen Marines vs Ulfari**, on a board scaled toward **33" × 24"**, using **Rawmallet 39.876** and **Murder Platoon** collections as stand-ins.

---

## Recommended matchup

| Player | Faction | Why start here |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Parent / experienced skirmish player | **Protagen Marines** | Hold ground, armour advantage, forgiving if you forget a trick |
| Junior / first Warcode game | **Ulfari** | Faster movement on key units, higher agility — simpler plan: close, shoot, stab |

Both rosters are **eight fixed units** in the beta rulebook — no list-building. Full statlines: [`../factions/protagen_marines/Squad_Datasheet.md`](../factions/protagen_marines/Squad_Datasheet.md), [`../factions/ulfari/Squad_Datasheet.md`](../factions/ulfari/Squad_Datasheet.md).

---

## Model count and roles

Each side fields **exactly eight models**, one per roster slot:

### Protagen Marines (proxy suggestions)

| Unit | Role hint | 39.9 / Murder Platoon proxy idea |
|------|-----------|----------------------------------|
| Commander Rickman | Leader, blade + pistol | Space Marine Lieutenant / Sergeant with power sword |
| Blast | Shotgun + locked grenades | Assault Intercessor / breacher operative |
| Hammer | Shotgun | Assault Intercessor |
| Anvil | Shotgun | Assault Intercessor |
| Bastion | Heavy weapon, slow | Heavy Intercessor / gunner operative |
| Blade | Melee claws | Intercessor with power fist / combat specialist |
| Shellshocker | Slow, tough, shotgun | Terminator / heavy armoured operative |
| Smasher | Melee bruiser, forced lock | Terminator / melee bruiser |

### Ulfari (proxy suggestions)

| Unit | Role hint | 39.9 / Murder Platoon proxy idea |
|------|-----------|----------------------------------|
| Soul Eater | Leader | Necron Lord / team leader operative |
| Ravener | Rifle | Necron Warrior / ranger operative |
| Shade | Sniper rifle | Deathmark / sniper operative |
| Stalker | Shotgun | Flayed One / breacher operative |
| Wraith | Blade + pistol | Wraith / melee hybrid operative |
| Phantom | Fast + locked grenades | Flayed One / fast assault operative |
| Doom | Heavy weapon | Immortal with heavy weapon / gunner |
| Reaper | Melee-only, fastest | Flayed One champion / pure melee operative |

**Mark leaders** (Rickman, Soul Eater) with a base ring or token — re-roll economy depends on them.

**Mark special units**: Blast and Phantom (double-grenade loadout); Reaper (no gun — no Overwatch).

## Board and terrain

| Item | Target | Home workaround |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| Playing surface | **33" × 24"** | Poster board, MDF, or mat trimmed to size |
| Terrain source | Partial cover, walls, doors | **Volkus** / sector mechanics boards, Murder Platoon starter terrain, or 39.9 barricades |
| Scaling | Warcode scenarios assume the 33×24 rectangle | If your mat is larger, **tape deployment edges** inward; do not stretch distances |
| VP tokens | Scenario card | Coins, dice, or numbered chits |
| Doors | Interactive | Literal doors from terrain kits or cardboard cutouts |

See [`../setup/Board_Setup.md`](../setup/Board_Setup.md) for deployment order and VP placement (D6 table).

**First scenario:** *Core of the Machine* — one narrative mission with three rooms and a shared countdown. Walkthrough: [`../First_Game_Walkthrough.md`](../First_Game_Walkthrough.md).

## What to print or scribble

- [`../Quick_Reference_Play_Guide.md`](../Quick_Reference_Play_Guide.md) — two-page dense aid between you

- Ammunition counters per ranged weapon
- 4 equipment points tracker per player (grenades / medkits)

Dice: handful of D6.

## Pre-game checklist

- [ ] Board marked **33" × 24"** (or agreed proxy scale with taped bounds)
- [ ] Scenario chosen — *Core of the Machine* for first session
- [ ] **8 Protagen + 8 Ulfari** proxies identified and leader-marked
- [ ] Squad datasheets printed or on tablet
- [ ] VP placement rolled (D6) per scenario
- [ ] Deployment zones agreed; D6 for deploy-first and round-1 initiative
- [ ] All units deployed alternately
- [ ] **4 equipment points** spent per side (unless Blast / Phantom locked loadout)
- [ ] Activation, ammo, overwatch, and grenade blast tokens ready
- [ ] Quick reference and glossary link: [`../rules/Keyword_Glossary.md`](../rules/Keyword_Glossary.md)
- [ ] House rule acknowledged: **proxy models are stand-ins** — statlines come from Warcode PDF, not 39.9 datasheets

---

## During play — keep it fun

1. **One unit at a time** — alternate activations; finish or pass before handing over.
2. **Call overwatch before** the opponent commits a move into line of sight.
3. **Reload costs AP** — track ammo on the sheet.
4. If behind on VP at end of a round, **draw a Contract** (use a homemade card naming one enemy unit).
5. When in doubt, read the cited page in the beta PDF — not the proxy game's rules.
