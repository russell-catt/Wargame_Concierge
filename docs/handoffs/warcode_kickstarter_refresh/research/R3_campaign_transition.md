# R3 — Campaign transition: Gamefound to Kickstarter

- **Track:** `warcode_kickstarter_refresh`
- **Researcher:** R3
- **Date:** 2026-09-24
- **Status:** Complete for Stage 1 gate
- **Scope:** Pre-launch marketing through the live Kickstarter scheduled to end 2026-10-23
- **Retrieval window:** 2026-09-24, approximately 12:56–13:10 EDT unless a row states otherwise

## Finding

The verified sequence is: a mixed digital/physical Gamefound offer launched on
2026-09-15; RedMakers publicly cancelled it on 2026-09-17, while Gamefound
records the campaign as ended on 2026-09-18 with no funds collected; a
digital-only STL/Print & Play Kickstarter then launched on 2026-09-23 and is
scheduled to end on 2026-10-23.

RedMakers publicly diagnosed the failed launch as a problem of **“reach.”**
The project owner's separate phrase **“insufficient buzz”** is useful testimony
about the transition, but it is not publisher wording or independent proof of
causation. Independent coverage located in this pass confirms that the
Gamefound campaign was publicly live, but does not establish why it was
cancelled.

## Evidence classes

- **Platform fact:** status, dates, totals, reward listings, or other data
  displayed by Gamefound, Kickstarter, or Steam.
- **Publisher statement:** a public claim made by RedMakers. It establishes
  what the publisher said, not that the diagnosis is independently proven.
- **Owner testimony:** Russell Catt's first-hand summary supplied to the
  coordinator on 2026-09-24. No public URL; do not attribute it to RedMakers.
- **Independent reporting:** a third-party article or roundup. Campaign copy
  reproduced by a publication is still publisher copy, not independent
  analysis.
- **Unknown:** not established by the sources reviewed.

## Chronology

### Pre-launch: physical and digital were both planned

The pre-launch site and later Gamefound reward list described a broad campaign:
digital STL/PnP tiers alongside resin miniatures, terrain, and physical boxes.
This is the correct historical context, but it must not be allowed to describe
the current Kickstarter offer.

### 2026-09-15: Gamefound launch

Gamefound update #1 is the strongest date source: it is timestamped
2026-09-15 11:15 AM and announces that the campaign is live.

### 2026-09-17/18: creator cancellation

The two dates describe different events and should both be retained:

- **2026-09-17 10:56 AM:** RedMakers posted the cancellation update.
- **2026-09-18:** Gamefound records the campaign's platform end date.

Gamefound explicitly states that no funds were collected. Its final displayed
historical counters are 63 backers, €7,894 pledged against a €45,000 goal, and
2,800 followers. Label all four figures as **Gamefound's final display,
retrieved 2026-09-24**.

### 2026-09-23 through 2026-10-23: digital Kickstarter

Kickstarter metadata records launch at 2026-09-23 11:00:42 EDT and deadline at
2026-10-23 11:00:42 EDT. This campaign is not the previous mixed-format offer:

- every reward is digital;
- STL tiers cover miniatures, terrain, and tokens in varying combinations;
- Print & Play PDFs are included by tier;
- the campaign expressly disclaims physical-reward manufacturing, packaging,
  and shipping risk.

RedMakers' Gamefound cancellation update says a physical edition is intended
to follow. That is a deferred publisher intention, **not** part of this
Kickstarter and not a confirmed release.

## Cause and claim separation

### Platform facts

- Gamefound campaign: cancelled by creator; no funds collected.
- Final Gamefound display: 63 backers; €7,894 / €45,000; 17.54%; 2,800
  followers.
- Kickstarter: launched 2026-09-23; scheduled to end 2026-10-23; fully
  digital rewards.

### RedMakers' public diagnosis

RedMakers said the game was ready but the launch was not, and specifically
identified **reach** as what was not ready. It said it would return when
reviews were available, full matches were on camera, and the right players
knew the game existed.

This supports: “RedMakers attributed the cancellation to inadequate reach.”
It does not support: “The campaign failed because of inadequate reach.”

### Owner testimony

The owner used **“insufficient buzz”** as a summary of the Gamefound problem.
Preserve that as owner testimony only. Do not place it in quotation marks
after “RedMakers said,” and do not treat “buzz” and “reach” as interchangeable
source language.

### Independent reporting

Brückenkopf independently confirms the campaign was visibly live on
2026-09-17. Tabletop Sentinel independently listed it among that week's
launches. Neither source independently investigates cancellation, conversion,
community size, or the publisher's reach diagnosis.

## TTS version drift

The live Kickstarter promotes the full game as playable in Tabletop Simulator
and links to Steam Workshop item `3776386741`. The Workshop description still
labels its current rules **0.8.7**, while this track has established the free
PDF **v0.8.9-F** as the current mechanics baseline.

Shipping may say: “A public TTS version is available, but its listing still
labels rules v0.8.7; verify against v0.8.9-F.” Do not say the mod is updated,
fully accurate, or mechanically equivalent without a play/build audit.

## Unknowns

- Whether insufficient reach was the sole or primary cause of cancellation.
- Gamefound follower quality, notification delivery, pledge conversion
  mechanics, traffic sources, and campaign-day retention.
- Why Kickstarter was selected over a revised Gamefound relaunch.
- Whether reviews and recorded matches caused improved Kickstarter
  performance; temporal sequence alone is not causation.
- Date, platform, contents, pricing, and fulfilment plan for any physical
  edition.
- Whether the public TTS implementation is functionally complete or differs
  from v0.8.9-F beyond its visible version label.
- Final Kickstarter totals and whether the campaign remains funded at close.

## Handling recommendations

### Durable shipping / KB facts

- Keep the 2026-09-15 launch, 2026-09-17 cancellation statement,
  2026-09-18 platform end date, and “no funds collected” as a dated Gamefound
  postmortem.
- Keep final Gamefound metrics only with platform/date attribution; they are
  historical evidence, not a general audience-size claim.
- Describe the current Kickstarter as **digital-only STL + Print & Play**,
  launched 2026-09-23 and scheduled through 2026-10-23.
- State that physical boxes were part of the cancelled Gamefound offer and
  are only deferred publisher intent after cancellation; they are not
  Kickstarter rewards.
- Attribute “reach” to RedMakers and “insufficient buzz” to owner testimony.
- Carry the TTS v0.8.7 drift warning until the Workshop listing and build are
  re-verified.

### Research-only / volatile

- Live Kickstarter backers, pledged amount, percentage, comments, reward
  distribution, and apparent momentum.
- Comparisons between 2,800 Gamefound followers and backer conversion.
- Claims that influencer coverage caused the relaunch to perform better.
- Any Kickstarter total copied without date, time, currency, and platform.

### Do not publish as fact

- “The campaign failed because it lacked buzz/reach.”
- “The Kickstarter has more momentum” without a defined metric and timestamp.
- “Physical boxes are coming” without “RedMakers said/intends” and an explicit
  no-date/no-guarantee caveat.
- “TTS is current” while the public listing remains labelled v0.8.7.
