# Space Marine 1DP fit — owned shelf (MFM v1.5)

- **Track:** `dataslate_0930`
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-01
- **DP source:** live [Munitorum Field Manual — Space Marines](https://mfm.warhammer-community.com/en/space-marines) v1.5. Detachment names and DP stamps only. This note does not copy detachment rules or weapon profiles.
- **Shelf source:** [`Owned_Models_Inventory.md`](../../../../games/warhammer_40k_11e/armies/space_marines/Owned_Models_Inventory.md) and the collection table in [`README.md`](../../../../games/warhammer_40k_11e/armies/space_marines/README.md) (photo audit 2026-08-21; Indomitus half 2026-09-12).
- **Confidence:** `draft`. A 1000-point list spends 2DP. These are 1DP options, so two of them can share a list, or one can stand alone under budget. The owner picks. The 1000 Gladius list was not changed.

## Recommend (at most three)

1. **Terminator Storm Force (1DP).** Best painted shelf. Game-ready: two Terminator Squads, one Terminator Assault Squad, Terminator Chaplain (claw called as a storm shield). That is a force you can put on the table without priming anything.
2. **Tacticus Attack Force (1DP).** Game-ready Intercessors and the primed relic-shield Captain. Assault Intercessors ×10 are owned and assembled, and they are bare plastic, so the close-assault half of this force is not table-ready tonight.
3. **Tacticus Firestorm Force (1DP).** Same Tacticus bodies, plus Heavy Intercessors and an Eliminator squad that the inventory marks dual-legal and in progress. More of a shooting shelf than Attack Force, and less complete than the Terminator shelf.

## The other 1DP detachments

- **Stormlance Task Force.** Three Outriders, assembled and unprimed. Bike Squad and Attack Bike are painted and are absent from the current MFM, so they are not a points source for this detachment.
- **Gravis Linebreaker Force** and **Gravis Siege Force.** The owned Gravis body in the collection table is Heavy Intercessors. No Aggressor or Eradicator squad is listed as owned.
- **Phobos Shadow Force** and **Phobos Shock Force.** Eliminators plus a Lieutenant with combi-weapon and two loose Marines. Thin next to the Terminator shelf.
- **Tactical Brethren, Devastator Brethren, Assault Brethren.** The Firstborn Tacticals (two squads of 10) and Devastators (5) are painted. The live MFM v1.5 does not list Tactical Squad or Devastator Squad. Do not build a 1000 list on those names until the Codex cards are in hand.
- **Ironstorm Spearhead.** Painted Techmarine and one Whirlwind. This MFM places the Whirlwind in Legends (owner paste). That is not a vehicle force.
- **Ironclad Champions** and **Gauntlet Task Force.** The inventory does not name a separate shelf for these. Terminators are already scored under Terminator Storm Force. A Dreadnought is in the README's owned future-Legends set. Without the detachment's keyword rules, neither outranks Terminator Storm Force.
- **Deathwatch Support.** About eleven primed Deathwatch veterans. That army stays its own identity and is off the Blood Ravens recommendation.

## Also inside the 2DP budget, not scored

A single 2DP detachment can be the whole budget: Ceramite Sentinels, Forgefather's Seekers, Medusa's Wrath, Shadowmark Talon, Spearpoint Task Force. Terminator Storm Force already fits a playable painted shelf, so these five are named and not ranked.

**Outside a 1000-point list (3DP):** Blade of Ultramar, Gladius Task Force. The current 1000 list is Gladius.
