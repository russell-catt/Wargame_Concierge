## 2. Effects by team (priority first)

### High impact for how you play today

**Hierotek Circle (game-ready set)**  
Expect weaker same-turn resilience after a revive. Sequence: bring back → opponent can more easily finish the 1-wound operative this turning point → Living Metal next Ready. Don’t build plans around double-dip regen.

**Canoptek Circle (Tomb World / full guide)**  
- vs Nemesis / Red Terror: don’t rely on Dimensional Banishment.  
- On Tomb World: respect teleport “can’t end closer” + pad limits + Breach 2AP floor.
