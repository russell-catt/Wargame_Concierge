## Format identity

- **Skirmish scale**, not army scale: single-model units, 28mm bases referenced throughout for line-of-sight math. `[RB pp.8, 12]` — confidence: verified
- **Alternating activation**, not IGOUGO: players take turns activating one unit each. `[RB p.4]` — confidence: verified
- **Fixed roster, no list-building**: you pick a faction and get its eight units; the only pre-game choice is spending 4 equipment points. `[RB pp.2, 16]` — confidence: verified
- **Indoor / derelict-interior framing**: the beta's only scenario is set inside a wrecked space drifter with rooms and doors. `[RB p.26]` — confidence: verified

## Physical / digital shape

- Three announced Gamefound tiers: **Field Commander** (STL), **Core Box** (physical), **Full Deployment** (all-in). Contents and pricing unrevealed. `[PL §09]` — confidence: draft
