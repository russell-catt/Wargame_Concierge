## The studio's own six differentiators

`[PL §03]` — all confidence **draft** (marketing framing), with a rules check appended:

| Claim | Rules support | Confidence |
|-------|---------------|-----------|
| "New world, no homework" | Fixed 8-unit rosters, 4 equipment points, no list-building `[RB pp.2, 16]` | verified |
| "Ammo runs out. Reload costs" | Per-weapon ammunition, tracked with tokens, reload priced in AP `[RB p.9]` | verified |
| "Overwatch rewards positioning" | 1 AP reactive fire interrupting seven different enemy action types `[RB pp.10, 11]` | verified |
| "Loot the fallen — friend or foe" | Equipment tokens stay where a unit died; any unequipped model picks them up for 0 AP `[RB pp.16, 17]` | verified |
| "Secret assassination targets" | Contracts, drawn face-down by the trailing player `[RB p.22]` | verified |
| "Easy entry, high skill ceiling" | Unfalsifiable from the beta; two of four factions unpublished | unverified |

**Finding:** five of six differentiators are genuinely implemented in the beta, not just copy. That is a better hit rate than the marketing-vs-rules gaps in `10_rules_vs_marketing.md` would suggest.

## Versus That other game (KT24) — where Warcode actually differs

Design-level comparison, teaching paraphrase only. No comparator rules text quoted.

| Axis | The Warcode | That other game | Confidence |
|------|-------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Force construction | Fixed 8 units, no points; only equipment choice `[RB pp.2, 33–36]` | Points/roster selection with meaningful pre-game list decisions | verified (Warcode side) |
| Ammunition | Tracked per weapon; running dry is a real board state `[RB p.9]` | Not a general subsystem | verified (Warcode side) |
| Catch-up mechanic | Contracts pay the **losing** player a secret bounty `[RB p.22]` | No losing-player bounty of this kind | verified (Warcode side) |
| Battlefield loot | Dead models drop usable gear that either side can take `[RB p.17]` | Not a general subsystem | verified (Warcode side) |
| Faction rules layer | Thin — statlines plus one-line weapon riders; Protocol Cards unread `[RB pp.28–32, 33–36]` | Thick — faction packages, ploys, equipment lists | draft |
| Board | 33" × 24" `[RB p.27]` | Comparable small footprint | verified (Warcode side) |
| Reroll economy | Points from a living Leader **and from your own casualties** `[RB p.23]` | Command-resource economy, not casualty-fed | verified (Warcode side) |
| Randomised objectives | D6 picks one of six capture-point layouts before deployment `[RB p.27]` | Mission/objective variation by card and pack | verified (Warcode side) |

**Convergent, not differentiated** — be honest about these in the review:
- Alternating unit activation. Not a Warcode invention; the studio's own community quote frames it as a preference, not a novelty. `[PL §08]` — confidence: verified
- Melee where the **defender rolls dice to block** is close in spirit to That other game's attacker/defender melee exchange. `[RB pp.13, 21]` — confidence: draft
- Small-squad, dense-terrain, interior-fight framing. The beta's derelict-ship scenario lands squarely in the same subgenre. `[RB p.26]` — confidence: verified

## Versus the broader market

- **STL-first distribution as a headline tier**, not a stretch goal — Field Commander leads the tier list, with resin-optimised minis and FDM-optimised terrain, plus terrain STLs as a standalone add-on. `[PL §09, §11]` — confidence: draft
- **$1 refundable co-design pledge.** VIPs vote on a new squad's theme, work with designers on its rules and playstyle, and join balance discussions; the result ships free on both digital and physical tiers. Low-friction community co-design at this price point is genuinely uncommon. `[PL §10]` — confidence: draft
- **Free full beta rulebook before launch.** The complete rules, two playable rosters, a scenario, and the map system are public pre-campaign. That is a real trust signal and it is what makes this review possible at all. `[RB pp.1–37]` — confidence: verified
- **Published faction difficulty ratings pre-launch.** `[PL §02]` — confidence: draft

## Where differentiation is weakest

- **No organized play story at all** — see `08_who_is_this_for.md`. A player embedded in event play has nothing to move toward. — confidence: verified
- **Half the faction roster is marketing-only.** "4 balanced factions" cannot be evaluated, and the two published rosters are the two rated easiest. `[PL §02]` `[RB pp.33–36]` — confidence: verified
- **Thin faction identity layer** relative to the comparator, unless Protocol Cards carry more than expected. — confidence: draft
- **Unproven fulfilment.** First campaign, no prior shipped boxed product located. See `02_who_is_developing.md`. — confidence: unverified
