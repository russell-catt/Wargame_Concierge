## Headline finding

Marketing sells **four balanced factions**. The beta rulebook ships **two playable rosters**. MDR and Dominium exist as prose blurbs only.

| Faction | Marketing blurb | Beta roster | Confidence |

## Protagen Marines — roster

Eight units `[RB pp.33–34]` — confidence: verified

- **Commander Rickman** — Leader; sidearm + energy blade; grants the squad's per-round re-roll income
- **Blast** — shotgunner; locked into a double-grenade loadout, cannot take other equipment
- **Hammer** — shotgunner, baseline
- **Anvil** — shotgunner, baseline
- **Bastion** — heavy-weapon platform; slow; minimal melee
- **Blade** — melee specialist; the squad's highest melee-attack count
- **Shellshocker** — slow, heaviest-armoured, easiest to hit; shotgun
- **Smasher** — melee bruiser; his axe drags anything within 1" into Melee Lock without base contact

**Faction shape (paraphrase):** every unit carries a melee weapon *and* (except by design intent) a gun; the squad sits one step above Ulfari on armour and one step below on agility; two units are slow. Reads as a hold-ground list that wins by not dying.

## Ulfari — roster

Eight units `[RB pp.35–36]` — confidence: verified

- **Soul Eater** — Leader; sidearm + razor blade; highest wounds in the squad
- **Ravener** — rifleman
- **Shade** — rifleman with a sniper rule that strips a point of the target's agility
- **Stalker** — shotgunner
- **Wraith** — sidearm + razor blade
- **Phantom** — fast; locked into a double-grenade loadout, cannot take other equipment
- **Doom** — heavy-weapon platform; the squad's least agile body
- **Reaper** — fastest, hardest to hit, and **melee-only — carries no ranged weapon at all**

**Faction shape (paraphrase):** uniformly lighter armour, uniformly better agility, two units above baseline speed. Reads as a close-fast list that trades durability for the first punch.

## Cross-faction observations

- **Mirror-slot design is visible.** Both squads field a Leader with per-round re-rolls, a locked double-grenade unit, a heavy-weapon platform, and shotgun/rifle bodies. Faction identity comes from stat offsets and one or two rule riders, not from different chassis. `[RB pp.33–36]` — confidence: verified
- **Reaper is the only unit in either list with no gun**, which also means it can never use Overwatch (Overwatch needs ≥1 ammunition `[RB p.10]`). Sharp, deliberate-looking design choice. — confidence: draft (inference from absence)
- **Reaper also sits at the agility cap.** The rules cap agility at 5 and note a base-5 target gains no bonus, so Reaper cannot benefit from cover or friendly-screen agility bonuses at all. `[RB pp.11, 12, 36]` — confidence: draft
- **Smasher's melee-lock rider is the strongest single rule in either list** — it removes the "just walk out of melee" escape that the core rules grant against non-touching models. `[RB pp.15, 34]` — confidence: verified
- **Possible profile inconsistency:** Smasher's sidearm appears tuned a step above the standard sidearm shown in the weapons chapter (higher normal and critical damage) with no ability text explaining it. Variant or copy error — see `12_needs_polish.md`. `[RB pp.7, 34]` — confidence: draft
- **Leaders are load-bearing, not just flavour.** Killing the enemy Leader shuts off 2 re-roll points per round for the rest of the game. `[RB p.23]` — confidence: verified

## Contract-deck dependency (design blocker)

- Contract cards name **one unit from each available faction**, and the target is drawn from whichever faction the opponent plays. `[RB p.22]` — confidence: verified
- With only two rosters public, **half of every contract card is unpublished**. The contract pages in the beta are flattened images `[RB pp.23–24]` and unread. — confidence: verified (blocked)
- The beta's worked example uses Ulfari / Shade, confirming the mechanism against a real unit. `[RB p.22]` — confidence: verified

## Gaps to flag as unverified

- No MDR or Dominium unit names, statlines, or abilities anywhere in sources read.
- No points values, because the game is fixed-roster — worth stating so readers do not look for a list-builder.
- No indication whether the **VIP-designed squad** becomes a fifth faction or an alternate squad inside an existing one. `[PL §10]`
- "Balanced factions" is a studio claim with no published data; 237 playtests are self-reported. `[PL §01, §08]`
