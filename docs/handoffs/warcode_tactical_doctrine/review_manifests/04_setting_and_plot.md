# Manifest §4 — Setting and Plot

- **Track:** warcode_tactical_doctrine
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-23
- **Citation legend:** see `00_what_this_is_not.md`
- **Headline finding:** the setting is almost entirely **marketing-side**. The beta rulebook carries no lore chapter, no timeline, and no faction fiction — only one scenario's premise.

## Premise (marketing)

- "Somewhere in a **sealed star system**, three factions have been locked in a conflict none of them can win — and **something from outside just made it worse**." `[PL §hero]` — confidence: draft
- Tone words used by the studio: **grimdark**, "dark atmosphere, mature story", 16+. `[PL §01, §hero]` — confidence: draft
- Creative Director framing: "a world you fall into headfirst — and don't want to leave." `[PL §07]` — confidence: draft

## Structural read of the premise

- **Three insiders + one outsider.** MDR, Protagen, and Dominium are the system's native belligerents; **Ulfari are the outside arrival** — "Aliens from outside the system." `[PL §02]` — confidence: draft
- **Sealed system** is doing narrative work: it explains why the war cannot be won, why nobody escalates externally, and why reinforcements never arrive. Useful hook, but no in-fiction mechanism for the seal is given. — confidence: unverified (inference)
- The **Ulfari inciting incident** is stated concretely: "A few of them destroyed an entire station without any warning or explanation." `[PL §02]` — confidence: draft

## What the rulebook contributes

- **Only lore text in the beta is the scenario premise** for *Core of the Machine* `[RB p.26]` — confidence: verified:
  - A long-abandoned, heavily damaged **space drifter**.
  - Reaching the machine's **core** trips security protocols.
  - The damaged machine **cannot tell friend from foe**.
  - A **countdown** starts with no time to escape.
  - Both squads race to reprogram the core to flag themselves friendly and the enemy hostile, turning **automatic turrets** on the loser.
  - Failure state: neither side persuades the core, **both are flagged hostile and destroyed**.
- This premise is mechanically load-bearing, not decorative: the tie condition kills both players, and a per-round "Core of the Machine" activation card applies negative effects to one or more of three rooms. `[RB p.26]` — confidence: verified
- **Protocol Cards** occupy five pages `[RB pp.28–32]` and are flattened images in the extract — likely the richest in-book lore/flavour surface, currently unread. **OCR blocker.** — confidence: verified (blocked)

## Gaps to flag

- **No timeline, no dates, no calendar.** — unverified
- **No named worlds, stations, or the star system itself.** — unverified
- **No explanation of the title "Warcode"** — the phrase never appears as an in-fiction concept in the rules text read. Strong candidate question for the VIP channel. — unverified
- **Ulfari unit names** (Ravener, Wraith, Phantom, Soul Eater, Stalker, Doom, Reaper, Shade) read as externally-imposed codenames, not native language. Whether that is intentional in-fiction is unstated. `[RB pp.35–36]` — unverified (inference)
- **Protagen unit names** (Blast, Hammer, Anvil, Bastion, Blade, Shellshocker, Smasher) are role-descriptive, matching the stated "engineering faction" identity. `[RB pp.33–34]` — draft
