# Manifest §8 — Who Is This For

- **Track:** warcode_tactical_doctrine
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-23
- **Citation legend:** see `00_what_this_is_not.md`

## Audience the studio is aiming at

- **Two-player, head-to-head only.** No solo mode, no co-op, no multiplayer, no teams anywhere in sources read. `[PL §hero]` — confidence: verified (absence of alternatives)
- **One-evening players.** ~120 min play, ~10 min setup, 4 fixed rounds. `[PL §hero]` — confidence: draft
- **16+**, on tone rather than complexity ("dark atmosphere, mature story"). `[PL §hero]` — confidence: draft
- **Refugees from IGOUGO systems.** The studio foregrounds a community quote praising alternating activation over IGOUGO. `[PL §08]` — confidence: draft
- **3D-printing hobbyists** as a first-class audience, not an afterthought — the STL tier leads the tier list and terrain STLs are promised separately. `[PL §09, §11]` — confidence: draft
- **Painters and sculptors.** A dedicated painter role, a gallery section, and "built by sculptors" framing. `[PL §07]` — confidence: draft

## Who it fits well

- Players who want a **short, closed-length game** with no list-building homework — fixed 8-unit rosters, 4 equipment points, done. `[RB pp.2, 16]` — confidence: draft
- Players who like **interactive turns** — alternating activation plus Overwatch means you are never idle. `[RB pp.4, 10]` — confidence: draft
- Players who want to **learn one small ruleset well** rather than track an edition treadmill. Marketing states this explicitly: "doesn't need a new edition to play differently next week." `[PL §03]` — confidence: draft
- **Indoor/derelict-terrain fans** — the beta scenario is a ship interior with rooms, doors, and door-blocking rules. `[RB pp.18, 19, 26]` — confidence: verified

## Who it fits poorly

- **Solo and campaign players** — nothing in sources read supports either. — confidence: verified (absence)
- **List-builders and points-optimisers** — there is no points system; roster is fixed. `[RB pp.2, 33–36]` — confidence: verified
- **Players who need a deep faction-rules layer** — differentiation is thin at chassis level; see `06_faction_concepts.md`. — confidence: draft
- **Anyone who needs a stable, finished ruleset today** — the only public rules are a beta, and two of four factions are unpublished. `[PTR]` `[RB pp.33–36]` — confidence: verified
- **Anyone who needs to buy in now** — physical product does not exist until a Sep 2026 campaign funds and delivers. `[PL §11]` — confidence: draft

**Review wording guidance:** state plainly that The Warcode currently has **no organized play scaffolding of any kind**, and that this is normal for a pre-campaign game — but it is the single biggest gap for a player deciding whether this can replace a game they currently play in events. Do not soften it into "coming soon"; no source promises it.

## Missing-infrastructure checklist (all: not yet evidenced)

- No tournament or event pack; no round timing or chess-clock guidance
- No global draw rule — the only tie handling is one scenario's "everyone dies" `[RB p.26]`
- No FAQ, errata, or living-document process published
- No official digital play route (Tabletop Simulator not owned here; workshop URL still an open track blocker) `[PTR]`
- No published scenario pack beyond the single beta scenario
- No army/roster registration or validation tooling (arguably unnecessary at fixed roster size)
