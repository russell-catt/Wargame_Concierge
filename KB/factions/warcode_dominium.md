# Dominium (The Warcode)

**Stub — marketing only.** Elite enforcement wing promising **morale degradation** on enemies and **ally amplification**. Marketing tags: Flexible, Combination-based, Deep mastery; stated difficulty **Expert**. **No unit roster in beta v0.8.7-F.**

---

## What sources say

- Pre-launch copy describes combat + support + psychological control at once.
- Beta rulebook text read (**pp. 1–37**) contains **no morale, suppression, pinning, or fear subsystem** — either Dominium ships unpublished mechanics or "morale" is marketing shorthand for a stat debuff.

---

## Design tension (draft inference)

"Most mechanically complex squad" + "plan several activations ahead" sits awkwardly with **2 AP per unit** and a short round count — few activations to chain. Protocol Cards (OCR pending) may carry identity load the datasheets do not.
