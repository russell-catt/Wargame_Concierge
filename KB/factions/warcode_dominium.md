# Dominium (The Warcode)

Dominium's playable Custodia Silens formation is a control-and-combination roster using Agility pressure, delayed activations, persistent damage, area fire, and extended Melee Lock.

---

## Play identity

- Justiciar Julius lowers nearby visible enemies' Agility.
- Confessor creates delayed one-AP activations after ordinary activations.
- Tormentor applies armour-ignoring fire and persistent round-end pressure.
- Cremator spreads Burning around a target.
- Lancer imposes [[warcode_melee_lock]] within one inch without base contact.

Exact profiles stay in shipping [`Squad_Datasheet.md`](../../games/the_warcode/factions/dominium/Squad_Datasheet.md), not in KB.

## Lore and doctrine

Dominium is the theocratic power controlling Erda, orbital access, and the Blackout isolation policy. Public and devotional sources frame that control as protection. An internal first-contact record instead documents a non-hostile Erebus-7 encounter followed by suppression and escalation orders. Those orders are not proof of completed operations.

Custodia Silens is an operational formation, not a synonym for the whole faction. Its narrative role comes from [[warcode_tactical_doctrine_field_edition]]; its playability comes from [[warcode_rulebook_v089f]].

## Source ambiguities

- Contract 4186 uses `Justicar`; the roster uses `Justiciar`.
- Directed Energy is called Focused Energy once.
- Hand Flamethrower ammunition and reload wording conflict.
- Inspiration, Agility reductions, round-end effects, and medkit/Burning interactions lack complete ordering.

## Open questions
