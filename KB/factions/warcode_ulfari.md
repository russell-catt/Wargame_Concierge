# Ulfari (The Warcode)

Outside-system aliens built to **close fast and strike first** — lighter armour, higher **Agility**, two units above baseline speed. Beta v0.8.7-F ships **eight fixed units**. Marketing tags: Aggressive, Fast, Melee-focused, Beginner-friendly. Teaching paraphrase only.

---

## Roster (beta)

| Unit | Role (paraphrase) |
|------|-------------------|
| **Soul Eater** | Leader — sidearm + razor blade; highest wounds in squad |
| **Ravener** | Rifleman |
| **Shade** | Rifleman; sniper rule strips 1 Agility from target |
| **Stalker** | Shotgunner |
| **Wraith** | Sidearm + razor blade |
| **Phantom** | Fast; locked double-grenade loadout |
| **Doom** | Heavy weapon; least agile body |
| **Reaper** | Fastest, hardest to hit — **melee-only, no ranged weapon** |

---

## Play identity (draft)

- **Agility** harder to hit than [[warcode_protagen_marines]] across the board; **Reaper** sits at the **Agility cap (5)** — cannot benefit from cover or friendly-screen bonuses that would raise Agility further.
- **Reaper** cannot [[warcode_overwatch]] — no [[warcode_ammo]] / no gun. Deliberate design choice.
- **Engage** at 2 AP fits a simple plan: close, fight. Fewer decisions per activation than juggling shoot + move on 2 AP.

**Tension:** "Melee-focused" marketing vs Protagen carrying higher raw melee attack volume on Blade/Smasher.

---

## Chassis shared with all factions

2 AP per activation, 4 equipment points, Leader re-rolls, [[warcode_contracts]], same cover and overwatch rules — see [[warcode_rulebook_v087f]].
