# Protagen Marines (The Warcode)

Engineering faction in heavy suits — **hold ground, trade mobility for resilience**. Beta v0.8.7-F ships **eight fixed units** (no points build). Marketing tags: Durable, Methodical, Punishing. Teaching paraphrase only — statlines stay in shipping `games/the_warcode/factions/` when promoted.

---

## Roster (beta)

| Unit | Role (paraphrase) |
|------|-------------------|
| **Commander Rickman** | Leader — sidearm + energy blade; squad re-roll income |
| **Blast** | Shotgunner; locked double-grenade loadout |
| **Hammer** | Shotgunner, baseline |
| **Anvil** | Shotgunner, baseline |
| **Bastion** | Heavy weapon; slow; minimal melee |
| **Blade** | Melee specialist; highest melee attack count in squad |
| **Shellshocker** | Slow, heaviest armour, easiest to hit; shotgun |
| **Smasher** | Melee bruiser — **forces Melee Lock on enemies within 1 inch without base contact** |

---

## Play identity (draft)

- **Armour** a step above [[warcode_ulfari]] across the roster; two units are slow.
- **Shotgun-heavy** — short reach pushes advance-behind-cover play.
- **Smasher** is the standout rule — removes the "walk out of melee without touching bases" escape. See [[warcode_melee_lock]].

**Tension:** Protagen also fields the roster's highest melee-attack unit — cuts against Ulfari being "most dangerous in melee" in marketing copy.

---

## Chassis shared with all factions

2 AP per activation, 4 equipment points (grenades/medkits), Leader re-rolls, [[warcode_contracts]], same cover and overwatch rules — see [[warcode_rulebook_v087f]].
