## Play identity (draft)

- **Armour** a step above [[warcode_ulfari]] across the roster; two units are slow.
- **Shotgun-heavy** — short reach pushes advance-behind-cover play.
- **Smasher** is the standout rule — removes the "walk out of melee without touching bases" escape. See [[warcode_melee_lock]].

**Tension:** Protagen also fields the roster's highest melee-attack unit — cuts against Ulfari being "most dangerous in melee" in marketing copy.

## Lore and doctrine

Protagen began as an Erdan corporation, resisted Dominium from the opening of the Blackout, and became a confederated outer-system state. Its dispersed Abyron Belt infrastructure and some Cassini moon bases provide strategic depth.

Protagen public sources emphasize science, engineering, liberty, and open communication, while hostile and faction-affiliated accounts expose tensions around centralized security power, material inequality, and the cost of Marine enhancement. These are attributed perspectives, not one settled social model.

## Chassis shared with all factions

2 AP per activation, 4 equipment points, Leader re-rolls, [[warcode_contracts]], and the shared cover and overwatch rules — see [[warcode_rulebook_v089f]].
