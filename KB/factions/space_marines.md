# Space Marines

Blood Ravens–painted Codex Space Marines force for the owner's son: Gladius Task Force teaching lists split into **Matched** (no Legends) and **Casual** (Legends allowed) paths.

---

## Role in this project

Space Marines make Necron attrition legible by contrast: reasonable durability, reasonable output, few special cases. Content is teachable to a second beginner, not only an opponent profile.

**Chapter paint / rules:** Blood Ravens paint; **Codex Space Marines** rules (not a Chapter-specific supplement). Deathwatch veterans are a **separate identity** — do not mix into Gladius BR lists.

---

## Army rule and detachment

- **Oath of Moment** — nominate one enemy unit; army attacks it better. See [[oath_of_moment]].
