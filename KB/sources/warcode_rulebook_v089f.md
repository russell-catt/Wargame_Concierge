# The Warcode Rulebook V.0.8.9-F

Current free mechanics baseline for The Warcode: unchanged core, cards, maps, Protagen, and Ulfari material plus playable MDR Executive Unit and Custodia Silens rosters.

## Authority and hierarchy

- This publication supersedes [[warcode_rulebook_v087f]] on the same topic.
- Omission is not a patch: unchanged material remains in force.
- The Tactical Doctrine Field Edition is narrative context only; it never overrides this rules source.
- KB pages use teaching paraphrase. Exact wording and profile tables remain in shipping, especially [`Rulebook_Quotes.md`](../../games/the_warcode/rules/Rulebook_Quotes.md).

## Delta from V.0.8.7-F

The 2026-09-24 page-and-resource comparison found no substantive mechanics change on PDF pages 2–36. Contracts, protocol cards, the scenario map, six objective layouts, Protagen Marines, and Ulfari remain equivalent. Four inserted pages add:

- MDR Executive Unit on PDF pages 37–38.
- Custodia Silens on PDF pages 39–40.

The final art page moves to PDF page 41.

## Player-facing additions

MDR is a coordinated support roster built around Sergeant 139, a Comms relay, and round-end healing. Custodia Silens is the playable Dominium formation and combines Agility pressure, delayed activations, damage-over-time effects, area fire, and extended Melee Lock.

See [[warcode_mdr]] and [[warcode_dominium]] for teaching summaries. Protagen and Ulfari were cross-checked as unchanged; see [[warcode_protagen_marines]] and [[warcode_ulfari]].

## Required ambiguity register

The current book does not resolve:

- `Justicar Julius` on contract 4186 versus `Justiciar Julius` on the roster.
- Directed Energy versus one Focused Energy reference.
- Hand Flamethrower ammunition versus its no-reload instruction.
- Ordering and repeat-target questions for Inspired activations.
- Whether AP-costed abilities trigger [[warcode_overwatch]].
- A lower floor or modifier order for stacked Agility reductions.
- Ordering among round-end healing, Burning, Influence, and Choke.
- Restricted-loadout, pickup, multi-item drop, medkit-immunity, and Burning-removal interactions.

These are source gaps, not invitations to invent rulings.
