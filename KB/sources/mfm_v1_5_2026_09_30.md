# MFM v1.5 30 Sep 2026

Live Munitorum Field Manual v1.5 is the points source of record. Necron 1000 V1 is 935. The Space Marine 1000 list was reviewed and not recosted.

The live [Munitorum Field Manual](https://mfm.warhammer-community.com/en) **v1.5**, read 2026-10-01, replaces v1.3 as the points stamp for Necrons. The two WarCom articles that week are commentary, not datasheets. Warmind matched the shared point sample and is corroboration only. Full delta note: `docs/handoffs/dataslate_0930/research/mfm_v1_5_2026_09_30.md`.

**Confidence:** `draft` until the Warhammer 40,000 app or an owned PDF is checked.

## What changed for lists we teach

Cryptek Conclave stays **2DP**, which is the whole budget of a 1000-point list.

- Immortals 5: 70 → 65. Immortals 10: 140 → 130.
- Tomb Crawlers (2): 50 → 60.
- Canoptek Doomstalker: 140 → 130. The 140-versus-145 question is closed.

The active 1000 V1 teaching list is body 905 plus Gauntlet of Compression 20 and Atomic Disintegrators 10 = **935 / 1000**. Same two enhancements. See [[necrons]] and [[cryptek_conclave]].

Three other Conclave lists are over their cap after the same delta, and no unit was removed: the 250 list is 260, the played 500 V2 is 505, the 750 V1 is 755, and the 1000 V2 buy path is 1005. The shipping sheets name the smallest cut and wait for the owner.

## Space Marines

[[space_marines]] was reviewed against this manual and the 1000 Gladius list was **not** recosted. That list stays stamped MFM v1.3. Gladius is **3DP**, which is over a 1000-point list's 2DP budget. Tactical Squad, Devastator Squad, and The Honour Vehement are absent from the current roster. The Whirlwind sits in the Legends capture. A fit note for the 1DP detachments is in the same handoff folder. No profiles were written from the articles.
