# Megabattle prep notes (Google chat, 2026-09-25)

Two owner-supplied notes from a Google chat about the 2026-09-26 Games Workshop megabattle: a joint war plan for the 945 Cryptek Conclave + 1000 Gladius team, and a packing / etiquette checklist. They live in the megabattle section of both 1000-point army guides. The print aids were later condensed to a flowing layout (Necron guide 5 pp, Space Marine guide 4 pp).

## What the source says

**Joint war plan** — three sectors: a **backline firebase** (Whirlwind + Techmarine with Doomstalker + Warrior Brick B + Technomancer as the regrowing screen), a **midboard push** up one shared flank (Captain + Tacticals + Devastators beside Brick A + Immortals, with the Conclave menu pivoting between `[IGNORES COVER]`, `[ANTI-INFANTRY 3+]` and the Atomic anti-Vehicle pick), and a **pocket hammer** (Scarabs sprint into enemy lines turn 1 as a tarpit / landing screen; Chaplain + Assault Terminators arrive turn 2–3 and charge the agreed target).

**Checklist** — rosters, stat pages, two dice colours, two tapes, movement trays, tokens (wounds, Oath, Reanimate, Teleport Homer), line-of-sight tool, shallow carry tray, water; don't argue rules (ask an organiser / roll off), don't page through books, don't reach into other lanes; do pre-measure on the enemy turn, roll simultaneously, declare proxies before deployment, announce targets aloud.
