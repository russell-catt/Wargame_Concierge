## Marketing claims (draft until cross-checked)

- **Four balanced factions:** MDR, Ulfari, Protagen, Dominium — difficulty ramp Beginner → Expert stated on pre-launch.
- **Game shape:** two squads of eight, four rounds, ~120 min playtime, 33" × 24" board, six map layouts (D6).
- **Development:** 27 months, 237 playtests — self-reported, unverified.
- **Gamefound tiers (draft):** Field Commander (STL), Core Box (physical), Full Deployment (all-in).

**Historical limit:** v0.8.7-F supported Protagen and Ulfari only. Current [[warcode_rulebook_v089f]] adds MDR Executive Unit and Custodia Silens; do not carry the old two-roster limit forward.
