# Codex Space Marines — October 2026 preview

Separate product: Codex SM October 2026 preview — Legendary Proxies / Legends honesty, no early stat rewrite.

**A separate product from the August balance package** ([[40k_aug_2026_balance_package]]). WarCom previews a forthcoming **Codex: Space Marines**, launching **October 2026** in the Warhammer 40,000 App — readiness and honesty content only, **not** an early rewrite of any list to preview stats.

**Confidence:** `draft`. Owner-pasted WarCom article body; canonical URL not yet locked (egress blocked at capture time). No table in this repo may be built from these preview numbers until the Codex actually ships.

---

## What the preview says (teaching paraphrase)

- Baseline Adeptus Astartes infantry framed as tougher and harder-hitting: basic warriors (combat knives, chainswords, bolt pistols) move to **Strength 5**; Intercessor bolt rifles gain a second fire mode (S5 and S6); Tacticus-armoured troops move to **Toughness 5**.
- **Phobos-armoured troops stay Toughness 4** but gain **Movement 8"**.
- **Points are expected to rise**, so armies play smaller and more elite.
- Heretic Astartes and Codex-divergent Chapters get similar boosts (example named: Terminators to T6, storm bolters to S5).
- Timeline: **October**, applied automatically in the App when the Codex launches.

Both Legendary Proxies and Legends units remain usable at home / stores / clubs / campaigns; competitive events need event-organiser permission — the same policy pattern as existing Legends rules.

## Why this matters for the owned collection

Owned Blood Ravens Matched starters use **Tactical Squad**, **Devastator Squad**, and (at 1000 pts) **Whirlwind** heavily. When the Codex ships in October, these become a **Legendary Proxy** (Tactical → Intercessor, Devastator → Desolation) or move to **Legends** (Whirlwind) respectively — but **until then, they stay current Faction Pack / MFM v1.3, matched-legal, with their own points and their own datasheet.**

Locked teaching stance (per shipping slice S2b):

1. **Now:** no stat or points swap on any list. Tactical/Devastator/Whirlwind keep fielding exactly as costed today.
2. **October, when the Codex actually ships:** apply Proxy / Legends honesty; do not pre-empt it.
