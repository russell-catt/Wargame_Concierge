## Conflicts / distance clarifications

| Topic | Confusion | Tier 1 resolution |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| 6″ vs 9″ after Da Jump | Early research flagged battle-report “6″ coherency” vs designer **9″** span | **Not a Da Jump exception.** Core uses **both** numbers for different jobs: coherency span **9″** (**03.03**); Ingress set-up distance **6″** from a battlefield edge (**20.04**). Deep Strike enemy gap is **>8″** (**24.09**). WarCom Armageddon Weirdboy sheet: Da Jump → reserves + Deep Strike + ingress — no coherency rewrite. Designer article **9″** matches Core; any “6″ coherency” wording is conflating Ingress edge distance with the unit span. |
