# The Warcode Kickstarter Relaunch (2026-09)

Retrieval-dated source record for the digital-only STL and Print & Play Kickstarter launched 2026-09-23 and scheduled to end 2026-10-23.

## Current offer at retrieval

The campaign is all-or-nothing and delivers rewards digitally. Tiers cover combinations of miniature, terrain, and token STL files plus Print & Play files. Physical manufacturing and shipping are not part of this offer.

RedMakers stated an intention to pursue a physical edition later. No date, platform, contents, price, or guarantee was established as of **2026-09-24**.

Live pledge totals and backer counts are volatile and are not durable KB facts.

## Try-before-backing and version drift

The campaign FAQ points to the free rulebook and a public Steam Workshop item. The Workshop listing was publicly available on **2026-09-24** but still labelled its rules as v0.8.7. Its implementation has not been audited against [[warcode_rulebook_v089f]].

## Coverage limits

Public third-party coverage was sparse at retrieval. The strongest hands-on item was a supplied-preview playthrough, not an independent purchase or a review of the digital-only offer. Other coverage consisted mainly of announcements, cautious pre-launch interest, roundups, and self-selected comments. None proves demand, momentum, balance, or delivery quality.
