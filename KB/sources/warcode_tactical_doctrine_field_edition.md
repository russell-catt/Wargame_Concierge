# The Warcode Tactical Doctrine Field Edition

Narrative dossier for The Warcode theatre, chronology, and factions; useful for source-aware setting context but never a mechanics authority.

## How to read it

The publication frames itself as academy instruction assembled from incomplete and interested evidence. Academy connective prose supplies a working structure, while speeches, sermons, interviews, memoranda, and hostile assessments establish what their speakers claimed. Project synthesis must keep those voices distinct.

Shipping teaches this method in [`Source_Methodology.md`](../../games/the_warcode/lore/Source_Methodology.md). KB remains paraphrase-only.

## Stable setting spine

- Dominium controls Erda and access to its orbit.
- MDR centres power on Huoxing and has only partial reach in the Cassini moon system.
- Protagen uses the distant Abyron Belt and some Cassini moon bases as strategic depth.
- The Kirkwood Belt and Cassini system contain overlapping or contested presence.
- No faction controls the entire theatre.

Huoxing is a planet. Mars is a distinct Kirkwood Belt settlement where the MDR founding congress met. Erebus-7 is identified as a carrier, not established as a station.

## Chronology and terminology

The Erdan calendar begins Year 0 with Dominium's appearance and divides later history into Foundation, Burning, Golden Age, Blackout, and Arrival. The printed Burning phase durations contain an arithmetic inconsistency, which shipping flags rather than silently correcting.

`Blackout`, `Blackout War`, and `Silence War` concern connected history but are not neutral synonyms: they refer respectively to a policy/epoch/event, a historical-source label, and factional usage.

## First-contact conflict

An older pre-launch publisher claim described sudden Ulfari violence. The later lorebook records a non-hostile encounter at Erebus-7 followed by Dominium suppression and escalation orders. It does not prove those orders were completed, who began every later clash, or Ulfari motives. The KB therefore records contested first contact and unknown Ulfari intent.
