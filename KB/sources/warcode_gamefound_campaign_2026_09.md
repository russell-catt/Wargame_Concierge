# The Warcode Gamefound Campaign (2026-09)

Historical record of the mixed digital-and-physical Gamefound campaign: launched 2026-09-15, publicly cancelled 2026-09-17, and recorded ended 2026-09-18 with no funds collected.

## Verified platform chronology

- **2026-09-15:** RedMakers announced the campaign live.
- **2026-09-17:** RedMakers posted that it was cancelling and that pledges would not be charged.
- **2026-09-18:** Gamefound records the platform end and creator cancellation.
- Gamefound states that no funds were collected.

The final platform display retrieved **2026-09-24** showed 63 backers, €7,894 pledged toward €45,000, 17.54%, and 2,800 followers. These are dated platform counters, not evidence about audience quality or causation.

## Publisher claim

RedMakers attributed the cancellation to inadequate reach. That establishes the publisher's diagnosis, not an independently proven cause.

## Offer scope

The campaign combined digital STL and Print & Play routes with physical products. That offer is closed and must not be used to describe the current digital-only Kickstarter.
