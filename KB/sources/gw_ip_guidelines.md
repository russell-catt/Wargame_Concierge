# Games Workshop IP guidelines (community content)

Paraphrase of GW’s published intellectual-property guidance for fan hobby content. **Not legal advice.** **Not a licence** — GW states the guidelines do not constitute formal approval or a waiver of rights.

## Hobby books / teaching material (paraphrase)

Fan stories, hobby books, and magazines based on GW settings may be allowed when they:

- Do **not** copy official text, artwork, or imagery.
- Are **non-commercial** (no money in or out, including ads and fundraising).
- Are not publicly distributed except as **no-charge digital** distribution.
- Are clearly **unofficial**, use **no GW logos**, and put the word **unofficial** prominently on the **front cover**.
- Are not prejudicial to GW’s goodwill, reputation, or integrity.

GW retains underlying world IP; fan content based on their settings **cannot be sold or monetised**.

## Fan-sites (extra constraint)

Fan sites must also **not post or display rules or stats copied** from official material. This repo keeps scoped quote appendices **in a private repo** for personal lookup (see [[warcom_free_core_rules_11e]], [[kill_team_2024_core_rules]] and AGENTS.md Sec 10) — event **print PDFs** use teaching paraphrase + unofficial footer instead.

## WarCom downloadable PDF licence (paraphrase)

WarCom grants a **personal** licence to store and access downloadable rules on your own devices. You may **not** give those files to others, torrent them, print-and-distribute them to third parties, or use them commercially. Wargame_Concierge event print aids are **our** HTML teaching notes, not redistributions of `eng_*` PDFs.

## Enforcement pattern (community)

When unofficial books included copied GW art/text or looked official, GW has requested removal of copied material and **unofficial** labelling on the cover (e.g. Warhammer Armies Project, 2023).
