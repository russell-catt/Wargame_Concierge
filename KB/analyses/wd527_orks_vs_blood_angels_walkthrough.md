**Battle Report: First Contact** — Jack (Orks, War Horde) vs Pearce (Blood Angels). WD527 tier 1.5. Teaching walkthrough of setup → resolution (no datasheet dump).

## One-line summary

Diagonal Crucible deployment on terrain objectives; Orks stack movement/charge tricks; Blood Angels answer with reserves, Oath, and a late Captain bomb — BA win 46–42 VP after five rounds.

## Pre-game sequence (what the article shows)

| Step | What happened |
|------|----------------|
| 1 Muster | Lists to event size; Force Dispositions chosen |
| 2–3 Mission | **Crucible of Battle** deployment card (5 objectives) — related family to Mission 38 |
| 4 Battlefield | 44"×60"; Chapter Approved layout |
| 5 Attacker/Defender | Pearce won roll → **Attacker** |
| 6 Secondaries | **Tactical** — two new cards each turn; 45 VP cap Primary and Secondary |
| 7 Formations | BA Chaplain + Vanguard in strategic reserves |
| 8 Deploy | Defender (Pearce) deploys first — see deployment log in source notes |

## Deployment lessons

1. **Touch objectives early** — Ork Boy on terrain footprint; two Intercessors on NE objective (*Objective Secured*).  
2. **Gretchin on home** — *Thievin' Scavengers* for CP each round.  
3. **Forward Ork wall** — Warboss + Boyz press south; Mek and Wartrakk behind barricades.  
4. **Eradicators** on north objective in cover.

## Round-by-round beats

### Movement phase tricks

- **Wartrakk** slides to northwest objective.  
- **Weirdboy *Da Jump*** → Boyz to reserves + Deep Strike → **immediate Ingress** behind Intercessors (article: slightly m ore than **9″**; Core requires **>8″** from enemies). Place in normal coherency (**2″ / 9″** — **03.03**). Shoot, then charge with Waaagh + *'Ere We Go* (7+ needed). Failed charge → spent Gretchin CP on re-roll → failed again. High-risk, high-reward sequence.

### Mid-game (Orks 18 – BA 15 VP)

- Pearce tags **Oath of Moment** on Dakkajig.  
- **Rapid Ingress** Vanguard + Chaplain onto central threat.  
- Captain Sendini: *Aggressive Onslaught* advance+charge; *Finest Hour* + *Red Rampage* + Red Thirst stack → deletes Mek platform.

### Late game (final tracked score BA 46 – Orks 42)

- Warboss consolidates for **Behind Enemy Lines**.  
- Eradicators denied shots by **terrain blocking LOS** — footprint layout matters.  
- Centre objective still contested going into round 5.
