# SM Matched vs Casual starters

Blood Ravens Gladius learning lists are duplicated so tournament legality and Legends shelf toys never share one ambiguous table.

---

## Decision rule

| Situation | Open |
|-----------|------|
| Event / matched play / stranger at a shop | `Starter_*_Matched.md` |
| Friendly learning with opponent OK on Legends | `Starter_*_Casual.md` |
| Old bookmark `Starter_{N}.md` | Shim → pick Matched (default) or Casual |

---

## What changes on Casual

Owned Legends: Bike Squad (3), Attack Bike (1), Astartes Servitors (4). Points from [[legends_field_manual_sm_2026_08]] (draft, retrieved 2026-08-22).

Casual lists use **one combined points table** (not a Matched core plus a side add-on). To fit all Legends into a cogent force:

| Level | Casual compromise (high level) |
|-------|--------------------------------|
| 250 | Captain + bikes + Attack Bike (Terminators stay on Matched) |
| 500 | Guns + bikes; Terminator brick deferred |
| 750 | Tech + Servitors + bikes + one Term brick (Chaplain/second Term optional variant) |
| 1000 | All three Legends + double Terms; Whirlwind dropped on primary (artillery variant exists) |

Matched 1000 keeps Whirlwind + Techmarine alone — see shipping Matched file.
