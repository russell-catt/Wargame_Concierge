# The Warcode Campaign Transition (2026-09)

The Warcode moved from a cancelled mixed-format Gamefound campaign to a digital-only Kickstarter; the chronology is verified, while explanations for the change remain attributed claims rather than proven causes.

## Verified chronology

1. The pre-launch site promoted a September Gamefound campaign with digital and physical paths.
2. The Gamefound campaign launched **2026-09-15**.
3. RedMakers posted its cancellation statement **2026-09-17**.
4. Gamefound records the platform end on **2026-09-18** and states that no funds were collected.
5. A separate digital-only Kickstarter launched **2026-09-23** and was scheduled to end **2026-10-23**.

## Publisher claims

RedMakers said the game was ready but the launch was not and attributed the cancellation to inadequate **reach**. It also expressed an intention to pursue a physical edition after the digital relaunch. These statements establish the publisher's explanation and intent, not causation or guaranteed delivery.

## Owner testimony

The project owner described the Gamefound launch as having **insufficient buzz**. This is first-hand owner testimony supplied on **2026-09-24**. It is not RedMakers wording and not independent proof.

## Independent coverage

Third-party launch listings confirm that the Gamefound campaign was publicly visible during its short run. They largely repeated campaign material and did not investigate cancellation.

A supplied-preview playthrough found the game approachable and highlighted ammo, terrain, objectives, and contracts, while also flagging a rules error, limited customization, replayability limits, and possible activation snowballing. It tested pre-release material and did not review the digital-only Kickstarter offer.

Other discoverable coverage was sparse and mostly pre-launch commentary, announcement pages, roundups, forum posts, and self-selected comments. It cannot establish broad demand, conversion, momentum, balance, or fulfilment quality.

## Open questions

- Whether reach was the primary cause of cancellation.
- Why Kickstarter was selected for the relaunch.
- Whether coverage affected campaign performance.
- Final Kickstarter totals and fulfilment outcome.
- Timing and form of any future physical edition.
- Whether the public TTS build differs mechanically from v0.8.9-F beyond its visible v0.8.7 label.
