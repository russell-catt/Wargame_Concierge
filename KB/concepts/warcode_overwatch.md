# Warcode Overwatch

**Overwatch** costs **1 AP**, requires **ammo ≥ 1**, cannot be declared while in melee range of an enemy, and **ends the unit's activation** for the round. The unit shoots first when a qualifying enemy performs a listed action in range. Teaching paraphrase of shipping [`Key_Concepts.md`](../../games/the_warcode/rules/Key_Concepts.md) (beta v0.8.7-F, read **2026-08-23**).

---

## The mechanic

**Setup:** Pay 1 AP, place an Overwatch token. No other actions this activation.

**Triggers (enemy in overwatch range):** Shoot, Move, Melee combat, Disengage, Escape, Use equipment, Reload — and **Engage** (overwatch fires, then melee proceeds).

**Does not trigger:** **Pass**; enemy moves behind full cover out of line of sight.

**Ends when:** the overwatching unit **fires**, **takes damage**, or the **round ends**.

Resolution uses normal shooting rules (Agility hit check, penetration, damage).

---

## Why it matters at the table

Overwatch punishes reloads and aggressive moves into lanes. Because **Pass** does not trigger it, baiting overwatch is deliberate — move a cheap unit to provoke fire, then activate elsewhere. Remember: declaring Overwatch spends half a unit's default 2 AP budget for the round.

---

## Warcode vs That other game — do not conflate

That other game's **Guard** is a 1 AP action in Murder Platoon with different timing, Conceal restrictions, and killzone patches. Warcode Overwatch is a **full-round commitment** with a fixed trigger list and normal shooting resolution.

**Collision flag:** Warcode **Overwatch** ≠ That other game **Guard**. Read each trigger list separately.

See [[activations_apl]] and shipping Guard notes under `games/kill_team_2024/` (That other game — separate product, not named in Warcode shipping paths).
