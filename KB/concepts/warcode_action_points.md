# Warcode Action Points

Each activated unit normally receives 2 Action Points, while MDR Order enlarges a future normal activation and Custodia Inspiration creates a delayed one-AP activation.

---

## The mechanic

- **Budget:** 2 AP when a unit activates in the Tactical Phase.
- **Costs:** Most actions pay AP from the unit or weapon profile. **Exceptions at 0 AP:** pick up ground equipment within 1 inch, open/close doors within 1 inch of the doorway, use a medkit.
- **Pass:** End activation with AP unspent. **Does not** trigger enemy [[warcode_overwatch]].
- **Order:** Sergeant 139 spends AP to give one unactivated ally +1 AP through round end; Comms can relay its reach.
- **Inspiration:** Confessor targets allies that already activated, giving each a later one-AP activation after ordinary activations.

Players **alternate activating one unit at a time** until every unit has activated or passed.

---

## Why it matters at the table

AP is the decision currency. A unit that spends 1 AP on Overwatch cannot move or shoot normally that round. **Engage** costs a full **2 AP** — a common opener that commits the activation. Reload and movement through friends or partial cover compete for the same two points.

## Warcode vs That other game — do not conflate

| Warcode | That other game (Murder Platoon) |
|---------|--------------------------------|
| **2 AP per unit activation** | **APL** printed per operative; summed for marker control |
| One **unit** activates (usually one model) | One **operative** activates |
| Pass skips remaining AP | Activation ends when player stops spending AP |

**Collision flag:** Warcode **Action Point** is not That other game's **APL**. Do not read marker-control math from Murder Platoon into Warcode VP scoring.

See also [[activations_apl]] (That other game concept page).

---

## Warcode vs 39.9 (Rawmallet) — do not conflate

39.9 has no per-model AP inside a phase — units move, shoot, and fight in fixed phase order. Warcode's alternating single-unit activations with a 2 AP cap are a different tempo entirely.
