# KB/concepts/

One page per **rules idea or tactical principle** - the things a player has to understand rather than look up.

**Type:** `concept` · **Filenames:** lowercase `snake_case` · **Schema:** [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) Sec 4, Sec 6

## What belongs here

Phases and the turn sequence, objective scoring, screening, trading, target priority, threat ranges, deployment principles - anything with tactical depth beyond a definition.

## What does *not* belong here

**Plain game terms and ability keywords.** Those are glossary entries in [`KB/glossary.md`](../glossary.md), not pages. A keyword earns a concept page only when all three hold:

1. It needs more than ~150 words to explain usefully, **and**
2. At least three other pages link to it, **and**
3. It has real tactical content - timing, interactions, common mistakes.

When promoting a keyword, leave a glossary stub pointing here so there is still one lookup surface. Full rule: [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) Sec 5.

## Write for a beginner

Explain *why* the rule matters and what goes wrong when a player ignores it. A concept page that only restates the rule has not earned its place.

`objective_scoring.md`, `screening.md`, `target_priority.md`, `movement_phase.md`
