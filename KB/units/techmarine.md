# Techmarine

Owned Firstborn Techmarine: the exact Matched 1000 pack keeps him independent with Artificer Armour beside the Whirlwind for repair, accuracy and vehicle-side protection.

---

## Role at the table

Repair / vehicle-support character. On the exact **Matched** 1000 pack he carries Artificer Armour and stays independent within 3" of the Whirlwind: this preserves his vehicle-side Lone Operative protection while his Command-phase blessing repairs the tank and improves its attacks. Attaching him to Devastators would give up that independent protection and is not the pack's default.

On **Casual** 750/1000 he can instead lead Servitors (Mindlock / Servitor Retinue — Legends paraphrase).

**Model note:** photo can read Enginseer-like; treat as Techmarine pending Faction Pack name glance (`unverified` name edge case).
