# Techmarine

Owned Firstborn Techmarine: matched-play legal character (MFM **55**) who babysits Devastators or the Whirlwind; on Casual lists he also unlocks [[astartes_servitors]].

---

## Role at the table

Repair / heavy-weapons neighbour character. On **Matched** 1000 he is costed alone. On **Casual** 750/1000 he leads Servitors (Mindlock / Servitor Retinue — Legends paraphrase).

**Model note:** photo can read Enginseer-like; treat as Techmarine pending Faction Pack name glance (`unverified` name edge case).
