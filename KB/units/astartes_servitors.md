# Astartes Servitors

Four owned Blood Ravens Servitor bodies: **Warhammer Legends** infantry that ride with the Techmarine — Casual Gladius lists only, never Matched.

---

## Role at the table

Cheap bodies that exist to make the **Techmarine** feel like a character with a retinue. In friendly games they can benefit from **Mindlock** while a Techmarine leads them, and **Servitor Retinue** can fold them into a bodyguard unit that Techmarine already leads (e.g. Devastators) — confirm timing on the Legends datasheet before a serious game.

**Matched play:** leave in the case. Techmarine is still legal alone (MFM).
