# KB/setup/

Everything about **the table and the mission**: deployment, terrain, mission rules, and scoring.

**Type:** `setup` · **Filenames:** lowercase `snake_case` · **Schema:** [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) Sec 4, Sec 6

## What belongs here

| Topic | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| Deployment | Deployment map layouts, who deploys first, reserves |
| Terrain | Terrain categories, how cover and line of sight work, layout conventions |
| Missions | Mission packs, mission rules, twists |
| Scoring | Primary and secondary objectives, when points are scored |
| Pre-game | Setup sequence, army selection, the roll-offs before turn one |

## Why this matters most for a beginner

Modern 40K is won on objectives, not on kill count. Setup and scoring pages are what turn rules knowledge into wins, and unit pages mean very little without them. This is why [`KB/ingest_procedure.md`](../ingest_procedure.md) puts setup **before** unit research in the ingest order.

## Cross-linking

Setup pages are natural hubs - link them from `KB/concepts/` pages on scoring and screening, and from faction pages where an army's deployment needs are unusual.
