# Killzones - Volkus and Tomb World

Two owned killzones, paraphrased from shipping (Door Fight / ruin / stronghold / fire-step and Close Quarters Guard aligned to the Jun 17 update log **2026-08-18**). These extras are **not** the core valid-target tree — [[valid_target]] stays generic.

**L1 flag:** setup index was empty. New pages from shipping, not a rewrite of a Wahapedia setup draft.

---

## Volkus — play-now cityfight

**Status in this collection: READY** (recommended first killzone with the 3e Starter). Urban ruins and **strongholds**. Standard **30" × 22"** board.

Cityfight extras (update log; read the shipping page before a tournament):

- **Door Fight:** when selecting an enemy, you may pick one on the **killzone floor**, within **2"** of, and on the **other side of**, a door the active operative is **touching**.
- **Large ruin:** upper level is Ceiling and Vantage; door is Accessible and Heavy. For **1" control range**, ignore that door when determining visibility — ignoring the door for 1" control range does **not** let you Shoot a target that is not actually **visible**.
- **Stronghold** upper level(s) are Ceiling and Vantage. Extra parts (barrels, ramparts, gaps, Stronghold B roof) are on the terrain sheet / shipping page.
- **Condensed Stronghold:** cares whether the target is **wholly within** a stronghold and on the **killzone floor or a fire step**.
- Being on a **fire step** does **not** let you ignore the rest of the stronghold for **obscured**.

Volkus Compound (asymmetric siege) is a second-session pack; confirm Compound Siege terrain is owned before planning around it.

## Tomb World — terrain unassembled, operatives ready

**Killzone: Tomb World** (close-quarters walls, hatchways, 6×7 grid) is **UNASSEMBLED** in this collection. **Tomb World operatives** (Geomancer, Crawlers, Macrocytes, Warriors, Scarabs) are a different item: assembled and game-ready — see [[necrons]]. Do not mix those two facts.

Wall terrain is stricter than Volkus Heavy: you do not move through or see over/through it except at hatchways/breaches.

### Close Quarters Guard (Gallowdark and Tomb World)

Jun 17 update log p.3, shared with Gallowdark (no separate Gallowdark page yet):

- **Guard:** the first bullet also fires if the operative **performs any action, moves, or is set up**.
- **On Guard:** that operative **cannot counteract** that turning point.

This is the errata delta, not the whole Close Quarters chapter — read the Core Book / mission pack.
