# 🎒 The Megabattle Survival Checklist: Do's, Don'ts, & Table Etiquette
*For Russell and Son — Warhammer 40k 11th Edition Megabattle Pack*

## 📦 Before You Leave: The Packing List

### Essential Gear
- [ ] **Rosters:** Printed copies of the Blood Ravens list (1,000 pts) and Necrons list (975 pts).
- [ ] **Rules/Datasheets:** The stat pages for your respective units (especially weapon profiles).
- [ ] **Dice:** Two distinctly different sets/colors (e.g., Red/Blue for Marines, Green/Silver for Necrons).
- [ ] **Tape Measures:** Ideally one for each of you to speed up simultaneous play.

### Megabattle Quality-of-Life
- [ ] **Movement Trays:** For the 20 Necron Warriors and 10 Tactical Marines.
- [ ] **Tokens:** Wound trackers, Oath of Moment marker, Reanimation markers, and the Teleport Homer token.
- [ ] **Laser Pointer/Line-of-Sight Tool:** Essential for verifying visibility across a massive, crowded table.
- [ ] **Water & Snacks:** Megabattles are endurance events. Keep a water bottle handy (but off the table surfaces!).

## 🚫 The Megabattle "DON'TS"
*Avoid these common massive-game pitfalls to keep the event fun and moving fast.*

*   **DON'T bring loose models in a deep box.** You will constantly be picking up your models. Use a small display board, baking sheet with magnets, or a shallow tray to carry your 1,000-point force around the venue.
*   **DON'T get bogged down in rule arguments.** If a weird interaction happens across multiple tables, ask an organizer immediately or just roll a 4+ to decide it on the spot. Keeping the game moving is more important than being 100% right.
*   **DON'T flip through books.** Niche stratagems waste time. Stick to your 2 or 3 favorite, memorized stratagems.
*   **DON'T cross into other players' lanes without asking.** Mega tables are packed. Avoid leaning over or moving your models through an ally's or opponent's sector unless your team commanders have coordinated a flanking move.

## ✅ The Megabattle "DO'S" & Pro Tips
*How to be the best players in the room and maximize your efficiency.*

*   **DO pre-measure during the enemy's turn.** While the opposing team is moving their massive armies, figure out exactly how far your son's Captain or your Doomstalker needs to reach. When your turn begins, you should already know your targets.
*   **DO roll dice simultaneously.** If you and your son are attacking different targets in the same phase, roll your dice at the exact same time to cut your team's shooting phase execution time in half.
*   **DO clearly mark your proxies before deployment.** Point out the Chaplain's lightning claw (counting as a relic shield) and the Terminator models to your immediate opponents *before* any models hit the table.
*   **DO announce your target intentions clearly.** With dozens of models in the way, say out loud: *"This Necron squad is firing into that specific Ork unit behind the ruin."*
