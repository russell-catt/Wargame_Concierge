# ⚔️ Joint War Plan: Blood Ravens & Cryptek Conclave
*1,000 pts Space Marines + 1,000 pts Necrons Team Synergy Guide*

## 🏰 Sector 1: The Backline Firebase
**Units involved:** Whirlwind + Techmarine (Space Marines) & Canoptek Doomstalker + Necron Warriors Brick B + Technomancer (Necrons).

### 🎯 The Strategy
Establish an unassailable defensive anchor in your team's deployment corner. 

### 📐 Positioning & Synergy
1.  **The Babysitter:** The Techmarine must stay within 3" of the Whirlwind. Use *Blessing of the Omnissiah* every Command Phase to give the Whirlwind +1 to Hit and heal it.
2.  **The Statue Gun:** Deploy the Canoptek Doomstalker where it has a long, open fire-lane across the center of the board. **Do not move it.** Let it lock in the `[HEAVY]` bonus.
3.  **The Screen:** Park Necron Warriors Brick B directly in front of this entire firebase. Keep them stationary to maximize their own `[HEAVY]` weapon profile.
4.  **The Result:** Brick B stops fast melee units from charging the Whirlwind or Doomstalker. If anyone shoots at Brick B, the Technomancer heals them. If anyone charges into this zone, the Doomstalker fires Overwatch on a 5+.

## 🛡️ Sector 2: The Midboard Push
**Units involved:** Captain + Tactical Squad + Devastator Squad (Space Marines) & Cryptek Geomancer + Necron Warriors Brick A + Immortals + Plasmancer (Necrons).

### 🎯 The Strategy
Advance up a single designated flank together to seize and secure the high-value center objective markers.

### 📐 Positioning & Synergy
1.  **Extended Firepower:** Your Warlord Geomancer uses the *Gauntlet of Compression* to give Brick A +6" weapon range. Pair them right beside the Tactical Squad. They can sit further back in safe cover while still reaching targets in No Man's Land.
2.  **The Multi-Tool Buffs:** Every Shooting Phase, use your Cryptek Conclave abilities dynamically:
    *   Give Brick A `[IGNORES COVER]` to flush enemies out of large midboard ruins.
    *   Give the Immortals `[ANTI-INFANTRY 3+]`. 
3.  **The Tank Melter:** The Plasmancer's *Atomic Disintegrators* enhancement gives the Immortals `[ANTI-VEHICLE 5+]` and `[ANTI-MONSTER 5+]`. If a giant enemy war machine pushes into the center lane, your Immortals can pivot to automatically wound it on 5s, letting the Blood Ravens focus entirely on clear infantry targets.
4.  **Resource Engine:** Use the Captain's *Rites of Battle* for free Space Marine stratagems. If using the *Quantum Abacus* on your Royal Warden, bait your opponent into targeting Brick A with stratagems to try and farm Command Points back for your team.

## ⚡ Sector 3: The Pocket Hammer
**Units involved:** Chaplain + Terminator Assault Squad (Space Marines) & Canoptek Scarab Swarms (Necrons).

### 🎯 The Strategy
A coordinated Turn 2 or Turn 3 strike designed to completely eliminate the enemy team's most dangerous scoring threat.

### 📐 Positioning & Synergy
1.  **The Bug Shield:** On Turn 1, sprint your 6-model Scarab Swarm max distance into enemy territory. Push them right into the face of a scary enemy unit to trap them in their own lines.
2.  **The Teleport Drop:** On Turn 2, your son triggers his *Teleport Homer* token or uses *Rapid Ingress* to drop the Chaplain and Assault Terminators safely behind the Scarab shield.
3.  **The Strike:** Point this combined unit directly at the designated team **Oath of Moment** target. 
4.  **The Payoff:** The Chaplain grants the Assault Terminators **+1 to their Wound rolls** in melee. With 4 models swinging *Twin Lightning Claws* (re-rolling wounds via `[TWIN-LINKED]`) and 1 swinging a high-impact Thunder Hammer, this unit will delete almost anything it touches upon impact.
