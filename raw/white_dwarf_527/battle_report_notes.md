## Key round beats

### Round 1–2 movement tricks

- Wartrakk claims NW objective; Big Hakka holds centre.  
- *'Ere We Go* + Weirdboy *Da Jump* → Grimcrag's Boyz to reserves → immediate **Ingress** slightly more than **9″** behind Intercessors (table play; Core Deep Strike is **>8″** from enemies); place in **2″/9″** coherency (**03.03** — not the Ingress **6″** edge distance); shoot, charge 7+ with Waaagh bonus; failed → CP re-roll from Gretchin → failed again.

## Teaching takeaways

1. **Terrain objectives** — touch footprints early even if unit will leave.  
2. **Gretchin CP** — fuels charge re-rolls.  
3. **Charge roll first** — plan targets after dice.  
4. **Ingress timing** — reserves arrive in Move Units step; immediate ingress after Da Jump.  
5. **OC swings** — battle report tracks VP by Primary/Secondary each round.
