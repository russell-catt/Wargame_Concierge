## Side A — Turn sequence and early phases

- **Header:** Warhammer 40,000: Quick Reference (shortened rules; bracket IDs = Core)
- **Active player (01.03):** player whose turn it is vs opposing player
- **Terrain (13):** exposed / light / dense features; obscuring areas

### Turn sequence (07.02)

1. Start of Turn step  
2. Command phase — marshal resources, battle-shock  
3. Movement phase — move units; reserves arrive  
4. Shooting phase — ranged attacks  
5. Charge phase — charge moves  
6. Fight phase — both players melee  
7. End of Turn step — non-mission rules; mission objectives  

### Command phase (08)

Steps: Start → Gain Core CP → Battle-shock → Command abilities → End.

- **Gain Core CP (08.02):** both players +1 CP  
- **Battle-shock (08.03):** roll for battle-shocked or ≤ half-strength units; while battle-shocked: OC = 0, no stratagem targeting, no actions  

### Movement phase (09)

Move types (09.02): Remain Stationary, Normal, Advance, Fall Back, Disembark, Ingress.

- **Engagement range:** 2" horizontal, 5" vertical  
- **Terrain (13.06):** Infantry/Beast/Swarm/Mobile through light/dense per keywords  

### Shooting phase (10)

Shooting types (10.02): Normal, Assault, Close-quarters, Indirect.

- **Benefit of Cover (13.08):** Infantry/Beast/Swarm in obscuring terrain — worsen BS by 1  
- **Hidden (13.09):** dense terrain keywords hidden until they shoot; detection 15"  
- **Obscuring (13.10):** blocks LOS from outside the area  

## Side B — Charge, fight, attack sequence

### Charge phase (11)

Charge (11.02): 2D6 charge roll; end within 1" of target; become engaged. Eligible: on battlefield, within 12" of enemy, unengaged, did not Advance or Fall Back.

### Fight phase (12)

Pile In → Fight (alternating; Fights First) → Consolidate.

### Making attacks (04)

Select weapons → targets → resolve (gather dice, attack sequence, other weapons).

### Attack sequence (05)

**Hit (05.01):** unmod 1 fails; unmod 6 critical hit; ≥ BS/WS hits.

**Wound (05.02):** unmod 1 fails; unmod 6 critical wound; S vs T table → see [`games/warhammer_40k_11e/rules/Wound_Roll_Reference.md`](../../games/warhammer_40k_11e/rules/Wound_Roll_Reference.md).

**Save (05.03):** defender groups models; armour (AP applies) or invulnerable (no AP).

**Damage (05.04):** unsaved attacks cost D wounds; allocate lowest to highest save results.
