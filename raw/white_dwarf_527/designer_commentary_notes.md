# WD527 — Designer rules commentary (research notes)

**Source:** `WD_527_2.pdf` pages 33–36, 51 (tier 1.5)  
**Transcribed:** 2026-08-24  
**Mechanics:** paraphrase; verify against tier-1 Core before tournament use.

## A New Era of War — Force Disposition and missions (p33)

- **Force Disposition** tags describe army goals (exterminate, hold ground, retrieve relics).  
- Each player picks one Disposition; pairing sets **each player's Primary** — narratives differ even on the same table.  
- **Example:** Take and Hold vs Purge the Foe → Immovable Object vs Unstoppable Force Primaries (card art on same spread).

### Tactical missions (secondaries)

- Draw **two** Secondary Mission cards **each turn** (Tactical mode in battle report).  
- No limit on how many secondaries can be active.  
- **VP cap per battle round** on secondaries applies.  
- **Challenger mechanic removed** from the pack.

## Movement changes (p34–35)

- **Coherency:** every model within **2"** of another model in the unit; every model within **9"** of every other model (tier 1 — confirm Core 03.xx).  
- **Engagement range:** 2". Cannot **end** a move engaged unless charging (etc.).  
- **No pivot tax** when rotating models.  
- **Monsters/Vehicles** may move through non-Monster/Vehicle enemy models.  
- **Dense terrain:** Infantry/Beast/Swarm/Mobile pass through; others add vertical distance.  
- **Fly:** −2" Move; ignore vertical distance over terrain/enemies.  
- **Reinforcements:** arrive in **Move Units** step (not end of Movement phase).  
- **Ingress move:** setup within distance of board edge, >9" horizontally from enemies; pre-round-3 not in opponent DZ.  
- **Charge:** roll 2D6 **first**; roll sets max reach **and** which enemies are eligible targets. Must end engaged with declared targets.  
- **Pile-in / Consolidate:** separate steps; active player moves all units first, then opponent.

## Attack sequence changes (p36)

- **Fast rolling** default for identical attacks.  
- **Allocation groups:** CHARACTER alone; others grouped by matching W, Sv, InSv; defender declares hit order; characters last.  
- **Indirect Fire (10.07):** can target non-visible; target always has Benefit of Cover; no hit re-rolls; unmod 1–5 fails (1–3 if stationary and target visible).  
- **Terrain shooting:** Infantry/Beast/Swarm in terrain area get Benefit of Cover; not fully visible = Benefit of Cover; LOS blocked through terrain area unless shooter inside area.

## Battle report — terrain objectives (p52, Pearce Rules Focus)

- Objectives are **terrain footprints** on the mat — the area **is** the objective (aligns with Core 14.01 teaching in repo).  
- Footprints define control boundary and cover/visibility interactions.

## Battle report — Benefit of Cover (p52)

- Infantry/Beast/Swarm wholly in terrain area: Benefit of Cover (−1 to hit BS).  
- Vehicles/Monsters: Benefit of Cover if not fully visible due to intervening terrain.

## Battle report — Charge (p59, Jack Rules Focus)

- Roll charge distance **before** picking which eligible enemy is the target.  
- Example: enemy at 8.5" needs 9" roll; failed roll cannot pivot to a closer target unless that target was also within the rolled distance.

## Battle report — Leaders and Support (p59–60)

- **Leader** + **Support** can attach to one bodyguard unit (one of each).  
- Example: Warboss + Bannernob; Librarian + Ancient.

**Distance clarification (2026-08-24):** Early pass flagged “6″ coherency after Da Jump” vs designer **9″** span. **Both Core numbers are real — different jobs.** Coherency span = every model within **2″** of a squadmate and **9″** of every other model (**03.03**). Ingress set-up distance = **6″** from a battlefield edge (**20.04**). Deep Strike / ingress enemy gap = **>8″** (**20.04** / **24.09**). WarCom Armageddon Weirdboy **Da Jump** only puts the unit in Strategic Reserves with Deep Strike for an (even BR1) ingress — it does **not** change coherency. Designer article **9″** matches Core; treat any “6″ coherency” line as conflating Ingress edge distance with the unit span, not as an Ork exception.
