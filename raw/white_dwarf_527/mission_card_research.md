## Mission identity

- **Name:** Mission 38 — Converging Ambition  
- **Source line:** White Dwarf Bunker / Warhammer 40,000  
- **Flavor:** Rival armies clash over vital sites; Force Disposition drives conflicting Primary goals.

## Primary mission structure

Replaces **stages 2 and 3** of the standard Chapter Approved mission sequence (per card). Player picks **one** Force Disposition if their army has several.

### Five disposition primaries (score at end of your turn unless noted)

| Disposition | Icon | Scoring |
|-------------|------|---------|
| **Purge the Foe** | Red sword | 4VP if ≥1 enemy unit destroyed this turn; **6VP** if more enemies destroyed this turn than friendlies destroyed last turn; **10VP** if you control opponent's home objective |
| **Reconnaissance** | Green eye | 3VP if ≥3 friendly units wholly in 3 different quarters, none within 6" of centre; **or** 6VP if ≥4 units in 4 quarters (same centre rule); **2VP per** enemy unit destroyed this turn |
| **Take and Hold** | Green skull | End of **your Command phase** or end of turn 5: **3VP per** objective you control; **+2VP cumulative per** objective (excl. home) if you also control home |
| **Priority Assets** | Yellow compass | End of **your Command phase** or end of turn 5: **4VP** if you control ≥1 objective; **+5VP cumulative** if one is central; **+5VP cumulative** if you held that central at end of your **previous** Command phase |
| **Disruption** | Blue X | **4VP per** objective you control; **4VP** if you control more objectives than opponent; **2VP per** table quarter with **no** enemy units |

## Deployment map (from card)

- Standard opposing deployment zones (red / blue on card art).  
- **14"** from long table edges to deployment lines (per diagram).  
- **9"** no-deploy circle at table centre.  
- **Five objectives:** one central; four corner objectives **10"** from nearest table corner.  
- Objective markers on card use disposition icons (home / centre / corner pairing).
