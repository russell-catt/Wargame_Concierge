## Protocol "Magnet" (room)

Flavor: THE FLOOR IN THE ROOM BECOMES HEAVILY MAGNETIZED. MOVEMENT BECOMES DIFFICULT.  
Rule: ALL UNITS THAT START THEIR MOVEMENT IN THIS ROOM SUFFER A -3 INCH PENALTY TO THEIR MOVEMENT PROFILE.

## Protocol "Hunt" (room)

Flavor: TURRETS ACTIVATE THROUGHOUT THE ROOM. TARGET ACQUISITION BEGINS.  
Rule: AT THE END OF THE ROUND, ALL UNITS IN THE ROOM WITH FULL HEALTH TAKE 3 DAMAGE.

## Protocol "Total Hunt"

## Protocol "Electricity" (room)

Flavor: ELECTRICAL PULSES START COURSING THROUGH THE ROOM. IT TAKES GREAT EFFORT TO DODGE THE ELECTRICAL SHOCKS.  
Rule: AT THE END OF THE ROUND, ROLL ONE D6 FOR EACH UNIT IN THE ROOM. IF THE RESULT IS 3 OR LESS, THE UNIT TAKES 3 DAMAGE.
